content_legacy: '## English


  The concept of "聽天下之內治" (governing the internal affairs of the realm) refers to
  the administrative responsibilities of the empress (后) in the Confucian political
  system. According to the *Book of Rites* (《禮記·昏義》), the empress presides over the
  inner chambers (六宮) — comprising three consorts (三夫人), nine palace ladies (九嬪),
  twenty-seven ranked concubines (二十七世婦), and eighty-one imperial consorts (八十一御妻)
  — to manage the internal affairs of the royal household. This internal governance
  encompasses the education of women, the regulation of family relations, and the
  maintenance of moral standards within the palace. The text states that by fulfilling
  this role, the empress ensures "天下內和而家理" — harmony within the realm and proper management
  of the family. The doctrine of "內治" stands in complementary opposition to "外治" (external
  governance), which falls under the emperor''s jurisdiction. Together, the emperor''s
  governance of external affairs and the empress''s governance of internal affairs
  form a complete system of cosmic and social order, reflecting the yin-yang duality
  that underlies Confucian political philosophy. The performance of these duties by
  the empress is directly connected to the principles established during the wedding
  ceremony, as the "著代" (establishing the successor''s role) principle extends to
  the empress''s responsibility for training future generations of women in the palace.

聽天下之內治，乃后之職責也。《禮記·昏義》述后之建置云：「古者天子后立六宮、三夫人、九嬪、二十七世婦、八十一御妻，以聽天下之內治，以明章婦順；故天下內和而家理。」言后率六宮之屬，以治閨門之內之事也。內治之事，非徒治其身而已，乃所以化天下之女者也。治之如何？明章婦順，使天下之為人婦者，皆知所以順乎舅姑、和乎室人、當於夫者，此后之責也。天下之治，肇於閨門；閨門之治，本於昏禮。昏禮成，而後有夫婦之義；有夫婦之義，而後有父子之親；父子之親，而後有君臣之正。是故后之聽內治，實本於昏禮之義以推之者也。后之聽內治，與天子之聽外治，相須而成；天子理陽道，后治陰德，此天地陰陽之大義，而治道之所以全也。
