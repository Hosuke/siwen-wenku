## English

**Minor Encoffining** (小斂, xiǎo liàn) constitutes the first formal stage of preparing the deceased for burial in traditional Chinese ritual practice. The "Record of Major Mourning" (喪大記) in the *Book of Rites* (禮記) provides comprehensive details on this ceremony's procedures and regulations.

### Timing and Participants

Minor encoffining occurs while the body remains in the inner chamber (*hù nèi*, 戶內). According to 喪大記: "小斂，主人即位於戶內，主婦東面，乃斂。" After the ritual concludes, the principal mourner (represented by the deceased's son) stands to the east, stamps his feet, and the mistress of the house (*zhǔ fù*, 主婦) does the same.

### The Ritual of Unbinding and Binding

Upon minor encoffining, males untie their hair strings and bind their heads with hemp (袒說髦，括發以麻), while females perform the *zhuā* (髽, hair bun) ritual. Both men and women wear hemp mourning belts (*dài má*, 帶麻) in the room's inner area.

### Materials and Garments

The 喪大記 specifies exact materials based on rank:

**Encoffining Mats:**
- *Rulers (君)*: Bamboo mats (簟席)
- *Dukes and Ministers (大夫)*: rushes/cattail mats (蒲席)
- *Scholars (士)*: reed mats (葦席)

**Shroud Bands (絞):**
- One longitudinal strip, three horizontal strips (縮者一，橫者三)

**Coverlets (衾):**
- *Rulers*: Brocade coverlet (錦衾)
- *Dukes/Ministers*: White silk coverlet (縞衾)
- *Scholars*: Black silk coverlet (緇衾)

**Garment Quantities:**
A total of nineteen sets of garments (衣十有九稱) were prepared. Rulers displayed their garments in the eastern portion of the pavilion (序東); ministers and scholars displayed theirs in the inner chambers (房中), all with western collars, arranged with the finest toward the north.

### The Encoffining Process

The text states: "凡斂者袒" — those performing encoffining must bare their shoulders. Additionally: "小斂大斂，祭服不倒，皆左衽結絞不紐" — in both minor and major encoffining, sacrificial garments must not be inverted, all with left-facing lapels, bound with cords rather than loops.

### Symbolic Elements

After encoffining, the principal mourner takes his position, dons outer garments, wears the mourning belt and headband, and stamps his feet. A visitor wearing a fur robe adds his military belt beneath, along with the mourning belt and headband, and stamps alongside the mourner in alternating rhythm.

### Candle Lighting Protocol

Regarding illumination: Rulers had two candles upstairs and two downstairs; ministers had one upstairs and two downstairs; scholars had one upstairs and one downstairs.

## 中文

**小斂**者，斂屍之初禮也。《禮記·喪大記》詳載其制，俾後世知所準循。

### 時節與方位

小斂之禮，行於戶內。《喪大記》云：「小斂，主人即位於戶內，主婦東面，乃斂。」卒斂之後，「主人馮之踴，主婦亦如之。」

### 袒括與髽

斂時，主人袒衣去飾。《經》云：「主人袒說髦，括發以麻，妇人髽，帶麻於房中。」蓋男子袒而說去假髮，以麻束髮；女子則髽，用麻為帶。

### 席與絞

席有差等：
- 君用簟席
- 大夫用蒲席
- 士用葦席

絞有定數：「布絞，縮者一，橫者三。」

### 衾與衣

衾亦有等：
- 君錦衾
- 大夫縞衾
- 士緇衾

衣之數：「衣十有九稱。君陳衣于序東；大夫士陳衣于房中；皆西領北上。」

### 斂者之儀

經云：「凡斂者袒，遷尸者襲。」又：「小斂大斂，祭服不倒，皆左衽結絞不紐。」

斂者既斂必哭，亦有定制。

### 燭火之節

「君堂上二燭、下二燭，大夫堂上一燭、下二燭，士堂上一燭、下一燭。」

此皆視死如視生之義也。

## 日本語

**小斂**（しょうれん）は、伝統的な中国において死者を埋葬のために包み込む第一段階の儀式である。『禮記』喪大記には、この儀式の手順と規則が詳細に記されている。

### 時と場所

小斂は室内（戶內）で行われる。「小斂、主人即位於戶內、主婦東面，乃斂」とある。儀式終了後、主人と主婦は共に足踏みした。

### 髪の処理

小斂において、男性は髪を解き、麻で束ねる（括發以麻）。女性は髽（スア、髪を結い上げた形）を作る。両者とも麻の喪章（帶麻）を房中に着用した。

### 材料と衣服

**敷物：**
- 君主：筵席（竹製のむしろ）
- 大夫：蒲席（ガマのむしろ）
- 士：葦席（アシのむしろ）

**布バンド（絞）：**
- 縦に一本、横に三本

**布団型覆い（衾）：**
- 君主：錦の布団
- 大夫：白絹の布団
- 士：黒絹の布団

**衣服：**全部で十九セット（「衣十有九稱」）。君主は東屋に、大夫と士は部屋の中央に、西領で北上位に配置した。

### 包み込みの順序

「凡れ斂する者は袒す」— 包む者は肩を露出させた。祭服は逆さにせず、全て左前で、布で結んだ。
