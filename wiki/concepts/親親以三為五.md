content_legacy: '## English


  The principle of **"Qin Qin Yi San Wei Wu"** (親親以三為五) articulated in the *Book of
  Rites* establishes a systematic framework for calculating mourning obligations based
  on the degrees of kinship proximity. This concept recognizes that emotional attachment
  and ritual duty diminish progressively as blood relationships become more distant.


  The text states: "親親，以三為五，以五為九。上殺，下殺，旁殺，而親畢矣。" This means that mourning obligations
  are calculated by expanding the circle of kinship: beginning with the three immediate
  family members (父母、妻子、昆弟), expanding to include grandparents and grandchildren (五),
  and further extending to great-grandparents and great-grandchildren (九). The doctrine
  of "殺" (diminishment) operates in three directions: upward (ancestors), downward
  (descendants), and sideways (collateral relatives).


  This graduated system reflects the Confucian understanding that human relationships
  form a concentric structure radiating from the self. As relationships extend outward,
  the intensity of mourning and ritual obligation correspondingly decreases. When
  all the graduated mourning periods have been observed, the kinship bond is considered
  complete and the mourning obligations cease.


  The principle underscores a fundamental insight: the ritual system must balance
  emotional reality with social necessity. While filial piety demands universal application,
  practical considerations require differentiation based on actual kinship proximity.

「親親以三為五，以五為九」出自《禮記·喪服小記》，乃儒家喪服制度之理論基石。其義以為，喪服之輕重、喪期之長短，皆依血緣親疏以為差等。

經文云：「親親，以三為五，以五為九。上殺，下殺，旁殺，而親畢矣。」言服制由近及遠，漸次遞減也。三為五者，加祖父母、昆弟之子也；五為九者，再加高祖、昆弟之孫也。蓋一體之親有三，父子一也，昆弟二也，夫婦三也；及是三者，皆可以推定其實殺之數。

「殺」者，遞減之義也。上殺者，由己身而上，親死者之衰漸殺；下殺者，由己身而下，子孫之服漸殺；旁殺者，由己身而旁，昆弟從昆之服漸殺。逮其旁殺至於緦麻，則親盡而恩竭，故曰「親畢矣」。

此制之要義，在乎明人倫之等差，知恩義之輕重。儒家以為，人道之大端莫先於親親尊尊，而親親尊尊之義，實於五服喪紀見之。五服既畢，則不相為服，宗族之義亦由是而分焉。
