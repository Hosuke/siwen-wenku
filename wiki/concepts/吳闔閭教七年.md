  The text presents the legendary military training program of King Helü of Wu, detailing
  the seven-year preparation that enabled Wu''s southern campaign against Chu. This
  account appears in the "Middle Chapter Against Aggression" (非攻中) as part of Mozi''s
  critique of aggressive warfare.


  The narrative emphasizes the extraordinary dedication and sacrifice required for
  military success: King Helü invested seven full years in training his forces before
  any campaign. The soldiers carried weapons, wore armor, and marched three hundred
  li without resting. When the Wu army finally emerged, it struck like thunder, overwhelming
  the state of Chu and forcing its submission to Song and Lu.


  The text notes specific details of the campaign: departing from Lin (林), passing
  through Zhulin (注林), emerging through the narrow path of Ming''ai (冥隘之徑), and finally
  engaging at Baiju (柏舉), where the decisive battle occurred. After this stunning
  victory, the Wu forces swept through Chu''s capital, making the Chu king flee and
  forcing both Song and Lu to submit in reverence.


  Yet Mozi employs this example not to praise Wu''s martial prowess, but to illustrate
  the ultimate futility of such aggression. He immediately follows this account with
  the story of how Wu''s own arrogance led to its downfall, demonstrating that military
  success divorced from moral governance is self-destructive.

《墨子·非攻中》詳述吳王闔閭之七年軍事訓練，以為征伐之鑒戒。闔閭立志報仇，七年之間，日夜操練士卒，不敢懈怠。將士披甲執兵，奔走三百里而不舍，夜宿叢林而不息，終成精锐之旅。

一旦興師，聲勢赫然，如雷電之擊。《非攻中》描繪其行軍路徑：「次注林，出於冥隘之徑，戰於柏舉，中楚國而朝宋與魯。」柏舉一戰，大敗楚師，楚王奔亡，吳師遂入郢都。宋、魯之君，皆朝貢於吳，吳國之威，遂震於天下。

然墨子引此事例，非為稱美吳之武功，乃在揭示征伐之終極無益。七年訓練之苦，一旦驕佚，則前功盡棄。闔閭死後，夫差嗣立，雖亦威震四方，然終因驕奢而亡國。墨子借此欲使當世之王公大人知悉：以征戰得國者，亦必以征戰失國也。
