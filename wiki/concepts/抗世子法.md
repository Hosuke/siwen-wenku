  **抗世子法** (Teaching the Crown Prince by Example) refers to the pedagogical method
  employed by the Duke of Zhou (周公) in instructing the young King Cheng (成王) in the
  principles of rulership. The term \"抗\" in classical Chinese means to \"raise up\"
  or \"elevate,\" suggesting that the Duke of Zhou elevated and codified the method
  of teaching the heir apparent through direct observation and living alongside a
  sage ruler.\n\nAccording to the *Liji* (Record of Rites), when the young King Cheng
  was too幼 (young) to assume the throne, the Duke of Zhou acted as regent. Recognizing
  that mere verbal instruction would be insufficient to impart the nuanced understanding
  of filial piety, ministerial loyalty, and proper governance, the Duke of Zhou implemented
  a revolutionary pedagogical approach: he placed Bo Qin (伯禽), King Cheng's designated
  tutor, alongside the young king, and arranged for King Cheng to reside in close
  proximity to the exemplary conduct of the sage.\n\nThe *Liji* records the Duke of
  Zhou's rationale: \"抗世子法於伯禽，欲令成王之知父子、君臣、長幼之道也\" (The Duke of Zhou raised the method
  of teaching the heir for Bo Qin, wishing for King Cheng to understand the ways of
  father-son, ruler-minister, and elder-younther relations). When King Cheng committed
  errors, the Duke of Zhou would personally discipline Bo Qin, thereby instructing
  the young king through vicarious correction.\n\nThis method was grounded in the
  belief that an heir prince must personally observe and internalize virtue through
  continuous proximity to righteous conduct. The Duke of Zhou further explained: \"仲尼曰：昔者周公攝政，踐阼而治，抗世子法於伯禽，所以善成王也\"
  (Confucius said: formerly when the Duke of Zhou served as regent and governed from
  the throne, he raised the method of teaching the heir for Bo Qin, thereby benefiting
  King Cheng).\n\nThe method encompasses three dimensions:\n\n1. **父子之道** (Father-son
  way): Through observing and serving his father King Wu, the heir learns filial piety\n2.
  **君臣之道** (Ruler-minister way): Through understanding proper hierarchical relations,
  the heir learns ministerial loyalty\n3. **長幼之道** (Elder-younther way): Through witnessing
  age-based order, the heir learns proper social conduct\n\nThis system of indirect
  instruction through proximity and example became foundational to Confucian heir
  education. The Duke of Zhou's approach recognized that true understanding of governance

「抗世子法」者，周公教誨世子之術也。語出《禮記·文王世子》。

周公相成王時，成王尚幼，不能親政。周公深知己身之責，非僅攝政而已，更需令成王知曉為君之道。然則世子之教，豈可徒賴言語？遂立「抗世子法」，以身教代言教，令成王與伯禽共居，時時觀摩聖德。

其法要義有云：「抗世子法於伯禽，欲令成王之知父子、君臣、長幼之道也。」周公特意使伯禽執教世子，每做成王有過，則撻伯禽以示誡。此乃間接之教，寓規諫於師長，使成王觀之而自悟。

仲尼嘗論此事曰：「昔者周公攝政，踐阼而治，抗世子法於伯禽，所以善成王也。聞之曰：為人臣者，殺其身有益於君則為之，況於其身以善其君乎？周公優為之！」

此法之妙，在於三端：

一、**父子之道**：成王朝於武王，日三問安，觀文王之孝，而知孝道。
二、**君臣之道**：成王居於朝堂，見周公攝政之恭，而知臣節。
三、**長幼之道**：成王與伯禽共處，習兄友弟恭，而知有序。

周公深悉：欲為人君，必先知為人子；欲知治國，必先知事人。此「抗世子法」，實為儒家世子教育之根本大法，而其效驗，垂範百世。

所出：《禮記·文王世子》
