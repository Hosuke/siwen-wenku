content_legacy: '## English


  The "Hundred Spirits" (百神) refers to the vast array of supernatural beings recognized
  in classical Chinese cosmology, including celestial bodies, atmospheric phenomena,
  mountains, rivers, forests, and other natural forces capable of producing beneficial
  or harmful effects upon the world. The Book of Rites establishes the principle that
  those who possess dominion under heaven must perform sacrifices to these spirits.


  The text distinguishes between the universal sovereign and subordinate lords: "Those
  who possess the realm sacrifice to the Hundred Spirits" (有天下者祭百神), while feudal
  lords sacrifice only within their own territories: "If the territory exists, they
  sacrifice; if the territory is lost, they do not sacrifice" (在其地則祭之，亡其地則不祭).


  This hierarchy of spiritual sacrifice mirrors the political hierarchy of the Zhou
  system, where the Son of Heaven''s universal dominion obligates universal religious
  duties, whereas feudal lords'' local authority generates only local religious responsibilities.

百神者，天地間種種神祇之總稱也。《禮記·祭法》明言祭祀百神之主體與範圍。

祭祀百神，乃天下共主之責。《祭法》云：「有天下者，祭百神。」孔穎達疏曰：「百神，謂山淵、風雨、星辰、天地之屬。」此言奄有天下者，無問方內方外，凡百神靈，皆在祭典之中。

諸侯則不然。《祭法》云：「諸侯，在其地則祭之，亡其地則不祭。」諸侯祭祀百神，以封地為限——地存則祭，地失則止。此與其政治責任相符，蓋諸侯之祭祀，非如天子之普徧，乃地方性之祭祀也。

百神之內涵，據《祭法》所記，包括：王宮（祭日也）、夜明（祭月也）、幽宗（祭星也）、雩宗（祭水旱也）、四坎壇（祭四方也），以及山林、川谷、丘陵之能出雲、為風雨、見怪物者，皆曰神。

是故祭百神之制，實為天命觀念之宗教表現——王者承天命而治天下，故有責任維護天人之和，而祭祀百神乃其重要途徑也。
