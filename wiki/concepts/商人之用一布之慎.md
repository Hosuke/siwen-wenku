content_legacy: '## English


  In "Valuing Righteousness" (貴義), Mozi criticizes the inconsistency of scholars who
  claim to value righteousness but are less careful with their conduct than merchants
  are with commerce. He observes: "A scholar''s use of his person is not as cautious
  as a merchant''s use of one coin. A merchant, in using one coin, does not dare to
  proceed recklessly but must choose the good. Yet a scholar''s use of his person
  is not like this: whatever he desires to do, he does. The severe receive punishments;
  the light receive censure and shame."


  Mozi draws a striking contrast between the merchant''s prudence in commercial dealings
  and the scholar''s recklessness in moral conduct. The merchant, dealing with a single
  coin (布), exercises careful judgment, selecting only honest goods and refusing dishonest
  ones. The scholar, whose conduct affects his very person and may lead to punishment
  or shame, acts on mere whim and desire without such caution.


  This inconsistency is evidence of false concern for righteousness. True attention
  to righteousness should manifest in the same careful deliberation that merchants
  apply to their business. The scholar who speaks of righteousness but acts without
  circumspection is like one who endangers his own life while claiming to value it.
  The passage reveals Mozi''s pragmatic approach—actions speak louder than words,
  and those who truly value righteousness will demonstrate it through careful conduct,
  not reckless speech.

  商人は錢一布を以ってしても、敢て繼苟にして讎むことなく、必ず良き者を選択して之れを商う。之れ商人の利益を得て害を遠ざくる所以なり。反觀すれば士人は義に其の身を使うも、謹慎することを知らず、唯意の所欲するがままに之れを行い、遂に重い者は刑罰に陷り、軽い者は毀醜を被る。此豈商人の一布を使うが如く慎むに若かぬや。

《墨子·貴義》載墨子之言：「今士之用身，不若商人之用一布之慎也。」此言直刺當時學者言行不一之弊。

商人用錢，區區一布，不敢繼苟而讎焉，必選擇良者而行之。此商人之所以能獲利而遠害也。反觀士人，用其身於義，不知謹慎從事，唯意之所欲則為之，遂致厚者入刑罰，薄者被毀醜。此豈非不若商人之用一布之慎乎？

墨子以商人之審慎對比士人之輕率，揭示一種深刻的矛盾：言義者反不如商人之重利。商人之慎，在於防範經濟損失；士人之忽，在於招致刑罰毀辱。利害大小之較，不辨自明。

此說反映了墨子「三表法」之精神——言語之是非，當以實際利害為準則。士人若真知義之可貴，自當如商人之慎用一布，謹言慎行，方能遠刑罰而就美德。[[口言之身必行之]]之義，亦當以此慎為前提也。
