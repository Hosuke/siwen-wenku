content_legacy: '## English


  The **Four Principles of Mourning** (四製) constitute the theoretical foundation of
  mourning rituals as presented in the *Book of Rites* (《禮記》). These four principles—恩
  (benevolence/affection), 理 (righteousness/principle), 節 (propriety/measure), and
  權 (adaptability/wisdom)—represent the guiding considerations for determining appropriate
  mourning periods, garments, and conduct.


  According to the text, "喪有四製，變而從宜，取之四時也" (Mourning has four systems, changing according
  to circumstances, drawn from the four seasons). Each principle corresponds to a
  cardinal virtue: 恩 aligns with 仁 (benevolence), 理 with 義 (righteousness), 節 with
  禮 (propriety), and 權 with 智 (wisdom). Thus, "仁義禮知，人道具矣" (Benevolence, righteousness,
  propriety, and wisdom complete the Way of humanity).


  The Four Principles operate as follows:


  1. **恩製 (Benevolence-based System)**: Mourning determined by the depth of familial
  affection. The most intimate relationships require the longest mourning periods.
  Example: Three years of mourning for one''s father, the closest bond.


  2. **義製 (Righteousness-based System)**: Mourning determined by social and political
  obligations. The ruler-subject relationship, though lacking blood kinship, demands
  equal mourning duration as the father-son bond because of its fundamental importance
  to social order.


  3. **節製 (Propriety-based System)**: Mourning governed by measured restraint, ensuring
  that grief does not destroy one''s life or prevent eventual return to normalcy.
  The three-year limit prevents excessive mourning.


  4. **權製 (Adaptability-based System)**: Mourning flexibly adjusted to individual
  circumstances—physical condition, social status, and practical necessity—demonstrating
  practical wisdom rather than rigid adherence.


  These four principles work in concert, allowing the mourning system to balance emotional
  expression with social necessity, personal circumstance with collective ritual.

**喪服四製**者，《禮記》所載喪服制度之理論綱領也。其義以恩、理、節、權四者為本，對應仁、義、禮、智四德，故曰：「仁義禮知，人道具矣。」

《禮記》云：「喪有四製，變而從宜，取之四時也。」言喪服之製非一成不變，乃因時制宜，準乎四時之序。春生、夏長、秋收、冬藏，猶親疏之別、輕重之分也。又云：「有恩有理，有節有權，取之人情也。」則四製之原，出於人情之真。

四製之分如下：

**一曰恩製**，以恩情深淺定喪期之長短。恩厚者服重，恩薄者服輕。父子之恩最厚，故為父斬衰三年，此恩製之明證。

**二曰義製**，以義理輕重定喪服之等差。君臣之義與父子之恩並重，故為君亦斬衰三年。經言「貴貴尊尊，義之大者也」，明義製之要義。

**三曰節製**，以禮節節制哀情，使不過於毀傷。《禮記》：「三日而食，三月而沐，期而練，毀不滅性，不以死傷生也。」又「喪不過三年」，皆節製之義。

**四曰權製**，以變通智慧應變人之殊情。經載：「禿者不髽，傴者不袒，跛者不踴，老病不止酒肉」，皆因人身之情狀而權宜變通者也。

四製相輔，缺一不可。恩制主情，義制主理，節制主制，權制主變。合而言之，乃成喪服之完具制度。
