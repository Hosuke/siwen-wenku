content_legacy: "## English\n\n**尚同中** (Shàng Tóng Zhōng, \"Upward Conformity: Middle
  Chapter\") is the second of three chapters in the Mozi explaining the Mohist doctrine
  of *shàngtóng* (向上統一, upward unification). While [[尚同]] establishes the theoretical
  foundations, this chapter provides extensive elaboration through hypothetical reconstruction,
  practical governance procedures, and critique of contemporary abuses.\n\n  ### The
  Genealogical Structure of Unified Governance\n\n  The text constructs a complete
  hierarchical chain from the village level upward: *lǐzhǎng* (里長, village head) →
  *xiāngzhǎng* (鄉長, district officer) → *guójūn* (國君, lord of the state) → *tiānzǐ*
  (天子, Son of Heaven). Each level performs the same essential function: unifying the
  moral standards (*yì*, 義) of those under one's jurisdiction. The procedure follows
  a consistent pattern:\n\n  1. The subordinate official reports upward, swearing
  to unify with the superior's standards\n  2. The superior, being the most virtuous
  person in that domain, is qualified to lead\n  3. Unity of moral standards produces
  effective governance\n\n  > \"里去而不善言，學鄉長之善言；去而不善行，學鄉長之善行。\"\n  > (\"Cast away bad
  speech, learn the good speech of the district officer; cast away bad conduct, learn
  the good conduct of the district officer.\")\n\n  ### The Problem of Contemporary
  Governance\n\n  Mozi critiques contemporary rulers (王公大人) for reversing the proper
  selection mechanism:\n\n  > \"政以為便嬖宗族、父兄故舊，立以為左右，置以為正長。\"\n  > (\"They use governance
  for the sake of favorites, family members, old acquaintances—establishing these
  as left and right officials, appointing them as leaders.\")\n\n  This violates the
  principle that officials must be selected for virtue, not favoritism. When people
  recognize that their leaders are appointed through favoritism rather than merit,
  they refuse to unite with them (*比周隱匿，莫肯尚同其上*). The result is that *shǎngyù* (賞譽,
  rewards and praise) cannot encourage goodness, and *xíngfá* (刑罰, punishments) cannot
  restrain violence.\n\n  ### The Cosmic Dimension: Heaven's Supervision\n\n  The
  chapter introduces a crucial cosmic dimension: even the Son of Heaven must submit
  to the *Tiān* (天, Heaven). Natural disasters—floods, droughts, plagues, untimely
  frosts—are presented as Heaven's punishment (*天之降罰*) for failure to maintain unified
  standards with the superior (*不尚同乎天*).\n\n  The sage-kings understood this principle:\n\n
  \ > \"明天鬼之所欲，而避天鬼之所憎，以求興天下之利，除天下之害。\"\n  > (\"They understood what spirits and ghosts
  desire, and avoided what they hate, in order to promote the world's benefits and
  eliminate the world's harms.\")\n\n 祭祀鬼神時，必備的酒醴粢盛、犧牲、珪璧幣帛，皆需合乎度量，不敢怠慢。此為「正長」之基本態度——上則天鬼厚之，下則萬民利之。墨子以為，賞罰之所以能勸善沮暴，源於上下一心、義同道合。\n\n

尚同者，墨子政治哲學之要義，謂天下之人當與上同心、同一是非準則也。

  墨子以為，天下之亂生於民之義異。原文云：「百姓爲人，若苟百姓爲人，是一人一義，十人十義，百人百義，千人千義，逮至人之衆不可勝計也，則其所謂義者亦不可勝計。此皆是其義而非人之義，是以厚者有鬬而薄者有爭。」欲止天下之爭，必使天下同一天下之義，此尚同之說所由起也。

  尚同之道，先自下而上，逐級統一。墨子設喻云：「故子墨子言曰：然胡不賞使家君試用家君發憲布令其家，曰：『若見愛利家者必以告，若見惡賊家者亦必以告。』」家君得善人而賞之，得暴人而罰之，則家治。繼而國君總其家之義，以尚同於天子。天子又總天下之義，以尚同於天。如是則上下相通，善惡同賞，治道成矣。

  然尚同非盲從也。墨子以為，上之所以賞罰者，當「明於民之善非」，即能辨別是非善惡，而非僅凭一時之毀譽。原文云：「上之爲政，得下之情則治，不得下之情則亂。」故尚同之本，在於上能周知下情而下能稟承上意，二者相須為用。

  墨子又論尚同之效云：「治天下之國若治一家，使天下之民若使一夫。」蓋以為既能尚同，則治理天下如治理一家，馭使萬民如馭使一人，其效至簡而至要也。

  尚同之要在乎「賞罰」二字。墨子以為，賞罰不得下之情，則善者無以勸，暴者無以沮。必使賞罰明於上，而下之情達於上，然後善人受賞而暴人受罰，國家乃治。
