  **弘毅** (Hong Yi, "Broad Vision and Resolute Will") is a foundational Confucian virtue
  describing the essential qualities of the ideal *shi* (scholar-official). This concept
  originates from the *Analects* (《論語》· 泰伯): "士不可以不弘毅，任重而道遠" — "The scholar-official
  must be broad-minded and resolute, for the burden is heavy and the road is long."

  ### Meaning of the Terms

  - **弘 (Hong):** Denotes expansive vision, broad learning, and generous spirit. It
  refers to the capacity to embrace great responsibilities and see beyond narrow self-interest.

  - **毅 (Yi):** Denotes resolute will, steadfastness, and perseverance. It refers
  to the determination to pursue one''s path despite hardships.

  ### Classical Foundations

  The virtue of 弘毅 connects to several interrelated concepts documented in this wiki:

  - [[修身]] emphasizes that the gentleman''s way requires cultivating integrity across
  all circumstances — wealth, poverty, life, and death.

  - [[學不可以已]] stresses that learning knows no end;懈怠 leads to loss of all prior achievements.

  - [[士信愨而後求知能]] teaches that the scholar-official must first establish trustworthiness
  before pursuing knowledge and ability.

  - [[大勇]] describes the courage that comes from self-reflection: "Though millions
  oppose, I shall advance."

  ### Modern Interpretation

  From a contemporary perspective, 弘毅 can be understood as:

  - **弘 (Broad Vision):** Wide-ranging knowledge, large perspective, and open-mindedness.
  Modern scholars benefit from interdisciplinary learning and a sense of social responsibility
  beyond their immediate profession.

  - **毅 (Resolute Will):** Unyielding determination and perseverance. In the face
  of setbacks, failures, or discouragement, one must persist.

  ### Contemporary Relevance

  Modern individuals — whether in academia, business, or public service — can embody
  弘毅 by:

  1. Cultivating broad horizons through diverse reading and experiences

  2. Maintaining steadfast commitment to one''s values and goals

  3. Accepting that significant contributions require long-term effort

  Those who pursue quick success or abandon their path midway lack the quality of
  弘毅.

**弘毅**者，儒家修身之要義，士之所以異於常人之本也。出《論語·泰伯》：「士不可以不弘毅，任重而道遠。」
### 弘毅之義
- **弘**：襟抱之廣、志趣之大。士之於學，當博覽群書、通達古今，不拘泥於一隅；於責任，當有以天下為己任之心胸。
- **毅**：志節之堅、意志之定。士之於道，當百折不撓、鍥而不捨，雖遇艱險而不改其守。
### 古義闡微
弘毅非一朝一夕之功，乃終身行之之謂也。參諸篇義：
- [[修身]] 云：「君子之道有四：貧則見廉，富則見義，生則見愛，死則見哀。」此君子之弘毅，反身而誠者也。
- [[學不可以已]] 云：「學不可以已。」此弘之義，學無止境，襟抱日廣。
- [[士信愨而後求知能]] 云：「士信愨而後求知能焉。」此毅之義，先立其本，堅定其志。
- [[大勇]] 云：「自反而縮，雖千萬人吾往矣。」此弘毅兼備之效也。
### 今之詮釋
今世之人，或急功近利，或半途而廢，皆不足以語弘毅。唯能守其志、廣其學、堅其行，斯為士矣。
夫弘毅之於今世：
- 於學者，則廣求新知、不固步自封；
- 於從政者，則心懷天下、不戀祿位；
- 於事業者，則百折不撓、不輕言敗。
能弘能毅者，方堪擔當大任。
