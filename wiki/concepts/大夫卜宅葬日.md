  ## English


  **大夫卜宅葬日** refers to the ritual practice whereby ministers (大夫) engaged in divination
  to determine appropriate burial sites and dates. This practice represents a crucial
  intersection of religious observance, political hierarchy, and ritual propriety
  in ancient Chinese mourning conventions.


  According to the *Liji*, when a minister planned a burial, divination was conducted
  with specific ritual


  ---


  大夫將葬，則卜宅與葬日。其占時所服：有司麻衣、布衰、布帶，因喪屨，緇布冠不蕤。占者皮弁。若筮，則史練冠長衣以筮，占者朝服。大夫之喪，既薦馬，哭踴而出，乃包奠而讀書。又大宗人相，小宗人命龜，卜人作龜焉。
