  "Tian De" (天德, Heavenly Virtue) and "Tian Zei" (天賊, Heavenly Outcast) represent
  the two possible fates for states and rulers according to Mozi''s philosophy of
  Heaven''s Will (天志).


  Those who practice "Heavenly Virtue" follow the path of universal love (兼愛): they
  do not attack the small, humiliate the weak, harm the few, deceive the foolish,
  despise the humble, mock the poor, or rob the old. All states under heaven harm
  each other neither with fire, water, poison, nor weapons. Their governance benefits
  Heaven above, ghosts and spirits in the middle, and the people below — threefold
  benefit with nothing unbeneficial. Such persons embody "sagely wisdom, benevolence
  and righteousness, loyalty and kindness, filial piety and compassion." Their good
  names gather from across the world.


  Those who embody "Heavenly Outcast" follow the opposite path of discrimination (別):
  they attack the small, humiliate the weak, harm the many, deceive the foolish, despise
  the humble, mock the poor, and rob the old. Their states use fire, water, poison,
  and weapons to destroy each other. Their governance benefits neither Heaven above,
  nor ghosts and spirits in the middle, nor the people below — threefold non-benefit
  with nothing beneficial. Such persons are called "bandits, thieves, the unrighteous,
  the disloyal, the unkind, the unfilial." Their evil names gather from across the
  world.


  The criterion is simple: follow [[天志]] and practice 兼愛 (universal love), or rebel
  against Heaven and practice discrimination.

天德與天賊，乃[[墨學]][[天志]]說之核心對比概念。

行天德者，循[[兼愛]]之道：大不攻小、强不侮弱、衆不賊寡、詐不欺愚、貴不傲賤、富不驕貧、壯不奪老。天下庶國莫以水火毒藥兵刃相害。事上利天，中利鬼，下利人，三利而無所不利。此之謂聖知、仁義、忠惠、慈孝。天下善名聚焉。

行天賊者，循別（兼惡）之道：大則攻小、强則侮弱、衆則賊寡、詐則欺愚、貴則傲賤、富則驕貧、壯則奪老。天下庶國方以水火毒藥兵刃相賊害。事上不利天，中不利鬼，下不利人，三不利而無所利。此之謂寇亂、盜賊、不仁不義、不忠不惠、不慈不孝。天下惡名加焉。

[[順天之意]]者，兼也，天德之所成；[[反天之意]]者，別也，天賊之所以立。
