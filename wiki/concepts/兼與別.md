content_legacy: '## English


  The dichotomy of *jian* (兼, universal/inclusive) and *bie* (別, partial/exclusive)
  constitutes a central philosophical framework in Mozi''s thought, particularly as
  elaborated in "Tianzhi Zhong" (The Will of Heaven, Part II). This conceptual pair
  serves as the primary criterion for distinguishing between sage governance and tyrannical
  rule, and between heavenly virtue and heavenly crime.


  According to Mozi, those who practice *jian* (兼) govern according to the principle
  of universal care: they do not attack small states when occupying large ones, they
  do not disturb small families when possessing large ones, the strong do not plunder
  the weak, the many do not oppress the few, the cunning do not scheme against the
  foolish, and the noble do not despise the humble. Their actions benefit Heaven above,
  the ghosts in the middle realm, and the people below. When all three realms receive
  benefit, nothing remains without benefit—this Mozi calls *tian-de* (天德, Heavenly
  Virtue).


  Those who practice *bie* (別), by contrast, act according to partiality and self-interest:
  they attack small states when occupying large ones, disturb small families when
  possessing large ones, the strong plunder the weak, the many oppress the few, the
  cunning scheme against the foolish, and the noble despise the humble. Their actions
  harm all three realms—Heaven, ghosts, and people. When all three realms suffer harm,
  nothing remains unharmed—this Mozi terms *tian-zei* (天賊, Heavenly Crime).


  The ancient sage kings Yao, Shun, Yu, Tang, Wen, and Wu followed *jian* and were
  rewarded by Heaven with rulership, wealth, and enduring fame. The tyrants Jie, Zhou,
  You, and Li followed *bie* and were punished by Heaven with loss of their kingdoms
  and enduring infamy. The *jian-bie* distinction thus forms the ethical and political
  core of Mohist governance, grounded ultimately in the [[天志|Will of Heaven]].

兼與別，乃墨子哲學之核心對比，見於《天志中》之系統闡述。墨子以為，治亂之分、賞罰之由，皆視此一對範疇以為斷。

兼者，兼愛之義。處大國不攻小國，處大家不亂小家，强不劫弱，衆不暴寡，詐不謀愚，貴不傲賤。觀其事，上利乎天，中利乎鬼，下利乎人，三利無所不利，是之謂[[天德與天賊|天德]]。聖王堯舜禹湯文武，悉從事乎兼，故得天之賞，使貴為天子，富有天下，名譽至今不息。

別者，別相人類之義。處大國則攻小國，處大家則亂小家，强劫弱，衆暴寡，詐謀愚，貴傲賤。觀其事，上不利乎天，中不利乎鬼，下不利乎人，三不利無所利，是之謂[[天德與天賊|天賊]]。暴王桀紂幽厲，從事乎別，故得天之罰，天縱棄之而不葆。

墨子以為，[[天志]]有好善罰暴之能，人間賞罰之根據在於天，而天賞罰之標準則在兼與別。欲得天賞者，當行兼道；欲避天罰者，當戒別行。此墨家[[兼愛]]說之政治意涵，亦其天人合一思想之要義所在。
