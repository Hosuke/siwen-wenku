  The concept of **從輕而重** ("from light to heavy") appears in the *Book of Rites* chapter
  服問 (On Mourning Garments), establishing the principle that mourning obligations
  can escalate when the degree of kinship intimacy increases.


  The passage states: "《傳》曰：有從輕而重，公子之妻為其皇姑。" This addresses the specific case where
  the wife of a noble son (*公子之妻*) must observe mourning for her father-in-law (皇姑)
  — an escalation that reflects her changed status upon marriage into the family.
  The underlying principle is that mourning grades are not fixed but responsive to
  relational dynamics within the family system.


  This concept exemplifies the nuanced approach to mourning in classical Confucian
  ritual: obligations expand and contract based on the living person''s position within
  kinship networks. When a woman marries into a noble household, her mourning obligations
  shift accordingly, acknowledging her new relational bonds.


  The complementary principle of 從重而輕 (from heavy to light) governs cases where mourning
  obligations diminish — such as when a wife''s parents, having received mourning
  from their daughter-in-law, may reduce their obligations toward her after her marriage,
  recognizing the new primary bond.

**從輕而重**，語出《禮記·服問》，為喪服制度中一大原則，謂喪服之輕重可隨親屬關係之變化而升等。

《傳》曰：「有從輕而重，公子之妻為其皇姑。」此言公子之妻為其翁姑（即丈夫之父母）服喪，由輕而重。蓋未嫁之時，與夫家並無服屬；既嫁之後，則俨然為其家人，自當依禮制服三年之喪。此隨其身分之轉變，而服制因之升等也。

考《儀禮·喪服》之制，凡女子適人者，為父母期，而為舅姑功衰。此皆從輕而重之例。蓋未嫁在家，本無為舅姑之服；一旦適人，便生服屬，故服從而生也。

其對義為從重而輕，見下條。輕重之間，反映儒家喪禮視親屬關係為動態流變之特色，而非僵硬固定之條文。
