content_legacy: '## English


  The **Seasonal Welcoming Rituals** (迎氣, also called 迎四時 or simply 迎) were court
  ceremonies in which the Son of Heaven personally led the highest officials to the
  four directional suburbs to welcome the arriving seasonal vital energy (氣). These
  rituals constituted a fundamental expression of the ancient Chinese belief in the
  correspondence between human governance and cosmic cycles.


  According to the *Monthly Ordinances* (月令), the rituals occurred at each seasonal
  transition: on the day of Li Chun (立春, Beginning of Spring), the ruler welcomed
  spring at the eastern suburb; on Li Xia (立夏), summer at the southern suburb; on
  Li Qiu (立秋), autumn at the western suburb; and on Li Dong (立冬), winter at the northern
  suburb.


  Three days prior to the seasonal transition, the Grand Historiographer (大史) would
  announce to the ruler: "On such-and-such day, Li Chun arrives, and the flourishing
  virtue resides in wood." The ruler then observed a purifying fast (齊). On the day
  itself, the Son of Heaven personally led the Three Dukes (三公), Nine Ministers (九卿),
  and various lords (諸侯、大夫) to the appropriate directional suburb. Upon return, the
  ruler conferred rewards at court.


  The ritual''s purpose was fundamentally political: by demonstrating proper correspondence
  with the seasonal order, the ruler maintained the Mandate of Heaven and ensured
  the prosperity of the realm. The *Zhou Li* (周禮) records the existence of the Office
  of the Four Seasons (春官、夏官、秋官、冬官), each responsible for the rituals and governance
  appropriate to its season.


  In the Han dynasty, Emperor Wen established dedicated Altars of the Five Emperor
  (五帝壇) at the four suburbs and seasonal directions, formalizing the connection between
  directional deities, seasonal energies, and imperial legitimacy.

**迎氣**者，天子帥公卿諸侯大夫，於四郊迎接四時之氣之禮也。亦稱「迎四時」「迎春夏秋冬」。

據《禮記·月令》：「孟春之月，以立春。先立春三日，大史謁之天子曰：『某日立春，盛德在木。』天子乃齊。立春之日，天子親帥三公九卿諸侯大夫以迎春於東郊。」其夏、秋、冬禮亦然：「立夏之日，天子親帥三公九卿大夫以迎夏於南郊」；「立秋之日，天子親帥三公九卿諸侯大夫以迎秋於西郊」；「立冬之日，天子親帥三公九卿大夫以迎冬於北郊」。

迎氣之義，深遠矣。春氣始生，屬木，色青，帝大皞，神句芒；夏氣盛長，屬火，色赤，帝炎帝，神祝融；秋氣收殺，屬金，色白，帝少皞，神蓐收；冬氣閉藏，屬水，色玄，帝顓頊，神玄冥。[[五行]]之德，循環終始，天子以禮接之，是謂「先天而天弗違，後天而奉天時」。

迎氣既畢，還反賞功臣，行慶施惠。是以明德和令，下及兆民。此[[王道]]之要義也。
