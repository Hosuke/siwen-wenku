content_legacy: '## English


  **Nourishing the Heart Through Few Desires** (養心寡欲) is a famous teaching from Mencius
  (7.35) presenting desire reduction as the supreme method of moral self-cultivation.


  Mencius states: "There is nothing more beneficial to the nourishing of the heart
  than to make few one''s desires." He then illustrates this with a striking quantitative
  analogy: "If a person''s nature is one of few desires, then even if he loses some
  of his moral nature, it will be but little lost. If a person''s nature is one of
  many desires, then even if he preserves some of his moral nature, it will be but
  little preserved."


  The passage establishes a clear inverse relationship: the strength of one''s desires
  directly correlates with the difficulty of preserving moral nature. Those who cultivate
  few desires create conditions where their innate moral nature (*性善) can flourish
  naturally. Conversely, those consumed by many desires create internal conflict that
  drowns out moral intuitions.


  This teaching reflects Mencius''s broader understanding of the psychology of moral
  development. He recognizes that human beings have both sensory appetites and moral
  capacities, but the relative strength of each determines moral outcomes. The path
  of [[修身]] thus requires not just external cultivation but internal regulation of
  desires.


  The parallel to Buddhist and Daoist teachings on desire is notable, though Mencius
  grounds this in the Confucian framework of *xing shan* (innate goodness of nature)
  and active self-cultivation rather than transcending the world.

「養心莫善於寡欲。其為人也寡欲，雖有不存焉者，寡矣。其為人也多欲，雖有存焉者，寡矣。」此語出《孟子·盡心下》，為儒家修身工夫之要言。

養心者，存養本心之謂也。本心即[[四端]]之心，[[仁-儒學-之|仁]]義禮智之端，乃天之所予人者也。然此心常為人欲所蔽，故須涵養以全之。如何而可？孟子以為，莫善於寡欲。

欲者，耳目口鼻四肢之感官欲求也。色聲臭味安佚，人情之所同欲也。然欲多則心為之累，[[浩然之氣]]為之窒礙，本心之端不得伸矣。故欲寡者，本心易存；欲多者，本心易失。寡欲之人，雖或有失，失亦不多；多欲之人，雖或有存，存亦有限。此 quantitativeneeds reasoningを示者也。

儒家所言寡欲，非如佛老之絕欲滅欲，乃節其流弊、使不過焉爾。孟子嘗言「可欲之謂善」，蓋人之所欲，本非不善，特不可縱之無節耳。節之有道，使耳目口鼻之欲不害心之德，斯謂之寡欲。

由是觀之，[[誠意]]正心之學，實以寡欲為基。《[[大學]]》云「欲正其心者，先誠其意；欲誠其意者，先致其知」，致知者，知欲之當節也；誠意者，節欲而不使妄也。內外交修，本末兼顧，此儒家寡欲之學所以異於他家之清虛寂滅者也。
