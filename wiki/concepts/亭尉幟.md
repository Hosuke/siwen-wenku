content_legacy: '## English


  The "Pavilion Commander''s Flag System" (亭尉幟) represents the core tactical communication
  mechanism in Mozi''s defensive doctrine. The system establishes precise correlations
  between enemy positions, drum signals, and flag displays, creating an unambiguous
  communication chain from outer perimeter to inner city.


  **Flag Specifications:** The pavilion commander''s banner pole measures **two zhang
  and five chi** (approximately 8.3 meters), while the banner cloth itself is **one
  zhang and five chi** in length and **half a fu** in width.


  **Signal Progression:** The system maps enemy advance to a graduated drum-and-flag
  response:


  | Enemy Position | Drum Beats | Flags Raised |

  |----------------|------------|--------------|

  | Beyond the outer moat | 3 beats | 1 flag |

  | At the water moat circuit | 4 beats | 2 flags |

  | At the outer fence (藩) | 5 beats | 3 flags |

  | At the breastwork (馮垣) | 6 beats | 4 flags |

  | At the female wall (女垣) | 7 beats | 5 flags |

  | At the main wall | 8 beats | 6 flags |

  | Halfway up the main wall | Continuous drumming | All flags |


  **Night Operations:** During darkness, the system replaces visual flags with **fire
  signals** using the same numerical progression, ensuring continuous communication
  regardless of visibility conditions.


  **Retreat Signals:** When enemies withdraw, the system reverses the flag sequence
  to match the advance progression but without drum accompaniment, signaling a cautious
  rather than aggressive response.


  **Banners for Commanders:** City commanders of forty-five chi height control forty尺
  banners; second-tier commanders use thirty尺; third-tier twenty-five尺; fourth-tier
  twenty尺; and fifth-tier fifteen尺. No commander operates below forty-five chi.


  This systematic approach to military communication demonstrates Mozi''s commitment
  to [[守圍城之法]] (Principles of Defending a Besieged City) through organized, predictable
  signal protocols that minimize confusion in crisis situations.

亭尉幟制，乃墨子城防守備之核心戰術通信機制，確立敵軍位置、鼓聲、幟影之精確對應，形成由外而內、層層傳遞之明確通信鏈。

**幟制規格**：亭尉之竿長二丈五尺，帛長丈五尺，廣半幅，此為基本幟制。

**信號遞進**：敵軍逼近與鼓幟之對應：

- **寇傅攻前池外廉**：城上當隊鼓三，舉一幟
- **到水中周**：鼓四，舉二幟
- **到藩**：鼓五，舉三幟
- **到馮垣**：鼓六，舉四幟
- **到女垣**：鼓七，舉五幟
- **到大城**：鼓八，舉六幟
- **乘大城半以上**：鼓不休

**夜以火數**：夜以火代幟，依此數傳遞信息，確保晝夜無間。

**退卻之幟**：寇卻解，輒部幟如進數，而無鼓。此示敵退而守兵仍需戒備，不可輕動。

**將旗尺寸**：城將為隆長五十尺；四面四門將長四十尺；其次三十尺；其次二十五尺；其次二十尺；其次十五尺；高無下四十五尺。蓋城將之幟特大，遠而易辨，此[[墨學]]所謂「上同於長」之義。
