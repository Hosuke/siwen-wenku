  **"Ritual and Music Cannot Be Absent Even for a Moment"** is a fundamental Confucian
  principle articulated in the *Record of Music* (樂記) chapter of the *Book of Rites*.
  The text states: "君子曰：禮樂不可斯須去身" — "The exemplary person declares: ritual and music
  cannot be departed from even for a moment."


  This principle expresses the Confucian conviction that moral cultivation is not
  a discrete activity to be engaged in at specific times, but rather a continuous
  process that must permeate one''s entire existence. The text immediately explains
  the consequences of lapses in this cultivation: "If the heart experiences even a
  moment of discord or unhappiness, then base and deceitful intentions will enter;
  if one''s outward appearance experiences even a moment of undignified conduct, then
  careless and disrespectful attitudes will enter."


  The doctrine establishes ritual (禮) and music (樂) as the twin pillars of self-cultivation,
  each operating on a different dimension of the person. Music acts on the inner heart
  (動於內), generating harmonious emotions and sympathetic identification with others.
  Ritual governs the outer body (動於外), producing dignified conduct and proper social
  relationships. Together they ensure that "內和而外順" — inner harmony expressed through
  outer compliance.


  This principle has profound implications for the Confucian understanding of the
  relationship between self-cultivation and social order. Since the exemplary person
  embodies the unity of inner virtue and outer propriety, any momentary lapse creates
  an opening for moral degradation. The [[修身]] (self-cultivation) of the individual
  is thus directly linked to the governance of family, state, and world (家、國、天下).
  The [[誠-儒學]] (concept of sincerity) achieves its full significance only when sincerity
  pervades every moment of existence, as suggested by the *Doctrine of the Mean*:
  "至誠無息" — "utmost sincerity is without cessation."'

**「禮樂不可斯須去身」**出自《禮記·樂記》，為儒學修身之根本原則。其文云：「君子曰：禮樂不可斯須去身。」

所謂「斯須」，猶言片刻之間。此句強調道德修養非某時某刻之活動，乃須臾不可離之日常功夫。蓋人心之感於物也，頃刻之間即可受外物誘惑而生好惡無節之患，故禮樂之養，不可有一時之懈怠。

《樂記》進而言其理據：「致樂以治心，則易直子諒之心油然生矣。」又云：「致禮以治躬則莊敬，莊敬則嚴威。心中斯須不和不樂，而鄙詐之心入之矣。外貌斯須不莊不敬，而易慢之心入之矣。」

由是觀之，樂主動於內，養心之和；禮主動於外，治躬之莊。內外交養，方為完人。[[修身]]之道，正在斯須之間。片刻之放縱，則天理滅而人欲肆矣。
