  **宮商角徵羽** (Gōng-Shāng-Jué-Zhǐ-Yǔ) are the five names of the ancient Chinese pentatonic
  scale, which in Confucian political-moral philosophy are systematically correlated
  with the five fundamental social roles: ruler (君), minister (臣), people (民), affairs
  (事), and material goods (物). This correlation, found in the *Yueji*, establishes
  music as a microcosm of political order.


  The text states: **「宮為君，商為臣，角為民，徵為事，羽為物。五者不亂，則無怗懘之音矣。」** When the five notes are
  properly ordered, the music is harmonious; when disordered, the music becomes discordant,
  reflecting and potentially causing political disorder.


  The text elaborates the consequences of each note''s corruption:

  - **宮亂則荒，其君驕** — When the gong-note is disordered, there is extravagance, for the
  ruler becomes arrogant.

  - **商亂則陂，其官壞** — When the shang-note is disordered, there is corruption, for the
  officials are damaged.

  - **角亂則憂，其民怨** — When the jue-note is disordered, there is anxiety, for the common
  people harbor resentment.

  - **徵亂則哀，其事勤** — When the zhi-note is disordered, there is sorrow, for affairs become
  burdensome.

  - **羽亂則危，其財匱** — When the yu-note is disordered, there is danger, for material resources
  become depleted.


  **「五者皆亂，迭相陵，謂之慢。如此，則國之滅亡無日矣。」** — When all five notes are disordered and mutually
  usurping one another, this is called *man* (negligence). In such a case, the nation''s
  destruction is imminent.


  This theory represents a complete politicization of music, where every aspect of
  the sonic world reflects moral and political reality. The five notes are not merely
  aesthetic categories but cosmic principles that must be properly maintained. Disorder
  in music is both symptom and cause of political disorder.


  The text also connects this to the **陰陽** (yinyang) cosmology, as the five notes
  relate to the transformations of yin and yang energies and the proper seasons. Proper
  music, like proper governance, maintains the right balance and ordering of these
  fundamental forces.

宮商角徵羽者，古代中國五声音阶之名也。《樂記》以此五音配君臣民事物，建立音樂與政治之對應關係，音樂實為政治秩序之表徵。

《樂本》篇云：「宮為君，商為臣，角為民，徵為事，羽為物。五者不亂，則無怗懘之音矣。」此明五音各安其位，則音樂和諧；五音失序，則音樂乖亂，而政治亦隨之紊亂。

其論五音混亂之禍：
- 宮亂則荒，其君驕——宮音失序則樂聲放縱，反映君驕慢之象。
- 商亂則陂，其官壞——商音失序則樂聲偏邪，反映官邪之風。
- 角亂則憂，其民怨——角音失序則樂聲憂愁，反映民怨之深。
- 徵亂則哀，其事勤——徵音失序則樂聲悲哀，反映庶事繁擾。
- 羽亂則危，其財匱——羽音失序則樂聲危殆，反映財用匱乏。

「五者皆亂，迭相陵，謂之慢。如此，則國之滅亡無日矣。」五音迭相侵陵，則慢音成而亡國之兆立見。

此說以音樂為政治之映現，音亂則政亂，音和則政和。五音非僅美學範疇，乃宇宙秩序之具體化也。
