content_legacy: '## English


  The doctrine that ghosts and spirits reward the virtuous and punish the violent
  represents one of the core arguments in the latter section of Mozi''s "Exposing
  the Ghosts" (明鬼下). This teaching asserts that supernatural beings actively intervene
  in the human realm to enforce moral justice, rewarding those who practice virtue
  and exacting vengeance upon the wicked.


  Mozi argues that the existence of social chaos—robbery, violence, and moral decay—stems
  from widespread doubt regarding the reality of spirits and their capacity to reward
  the good and punish the evil. If people genuinely believed that spirits possessed
  such power, they would refrain from wrongdoing. The text presents multiple historical
  examples of ghosts fulfilling this cosmic punitive function: Duke Du Bo''s spirit
  shooting King Xuan of Zhou, Zhuang Ziyi''s ghost striking down Duke Jian of Yan,
  and the god Gou Mang appearing to Duke Ling of Zheng to bless him.


  Crucially, Mozi emphasizes that the spirits'' punitive power transcends social status.
  They can overcome "wealth, rank, numbers, strength, physical prowess, and sharp
  weapons." Even the most powerful tyrants—Jie of Xia and Zhou of Shang—could not
  escape supernatural retribution despite their armies and strength. This democratization
  of accountability, operating beyond human political authority, forms a key element
  of Mozi''s moral philosophy.


  The doctrine also extends to sacrificial contexts. Mozi argues that proper reverence
  in sacrifice—clean vessels, well-formed offerings, timely ceremonies—ensures divine
  favor, while disrespect invites punishment. This establishes a clear reciprocity
  between human piety and supernatural blessing.

鬼神之賞賢而罰暴，乃墨子學說之要義，見於《墨子·明鬼下》。墨子以為，鬼神具有賞善罰惡之能，能察知人間善惡，必予以相應之賞罰。此說為墨子批判時世、救世人之根本論據。

墨子以為，天下之所以亂，源於衆人疑惑鬼神之有無，不明鬼神能賞賢罰暴。苟天下之人皆信鬼神之賞罰，則天下豈復有亂哉？故墨子力證鬼神之存在，以為治世之要。

《明鬼下》詳述多則鬼神報應之事：杜伯化鬼射宣王，莊子儀顯靈誅燕君，觀辜因祭祀不謹而被殺於壇上，皆鬼神賞罰之驗。墨子尤其強調，鬼神之罰，不避富貴衆強、勇力強武、堅甲利兵。夏桀、殷紂雖貴為天子、富有天下，終不能免鬼神之誅。此說所以破世人僥倖之心，使其知鬼神之明無所不察，鬼神之罰無所不勝。

又《禽艾》之道云：「得璣無小，滅宗無大。」言鬼神所賞，雖小必賞之；所罰，雖大必罰之。此鬼神賞罰之公正無私，亦墨子所據以為說者也。
