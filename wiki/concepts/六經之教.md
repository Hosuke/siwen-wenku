content_legacy: '## English


  The **Six Classics Teaching** (六經之教) is a pedagogical framework articulated by Confucius
  in the *Book of Rites: Explanation of the Classics* (禮記·經解). Confucius taught that
  entering a nation''s educational system reveals the character of its people—their
  virtues and deficiencies alike stem from which classic has shaped them.


  Each of the six classics produces a distinctive moral and intellectual quality in
  those who study it:


  | Classic | Cultivated Virtue | Excess (失) |

  |---------|-------------------|-------------|

  | 詩 (Book of Songs) | 溫柔敦厚 – Gentleness and sincerity | 愚 – Foolishness |

  | 書 (Book of Documents) | 疏通知遠 – Broad knowledge of the past | 誣 – Falsehood |

  | 樂 (Book of Music) | 廣博易良 – Openness and kindness | 奢 – Extravagance |

  | 易 (Book of Changes) | 絜靜精微 – Clarity and subtlety | 賊 – Harmfulness |

  | 禮 (Book of Rites) | 恭儉莊敬 – Reverence and propriety | 煩 – Over-elaboration |

  | 春秋 (Spring and Autumn Annals) | 屬辭比事 – Narrative precision | 亂 – Disorder |


  The true scholar, however, transcends these dangers: one who is gentle without foolishness
  has truly mastered the *Songs*; one who knows the past without falsehood has truly
  mastered the *Documents*; one who is open without extravagance has truly mastered
  *Music*; one who is subtle without harm has truly mastered the *Changes*; one who
  is reverent without over-elaboration has truly mastered the *Rites*; one who is
  precise without disorder has truly mastered the *Spring and Autumn*.


  This framework reflects the Confucian conviction that education must be balanced—that
  each field of learning corrects the other''s potential excess.

**六經之教**出自《禮記·經解》，為孔子所述之教育哲學。孔子曰：「入其國，其教可知也。」蓋治國之風尚，可由其民之性情觀之；而民性之善惡，悉由六經之教化所成。

六經各有所長，養人之德性，亦各有所失：

- **《詩》**以教人，養其溫柔敦厚之性；然失之則愚。
- **《書》**以教人，養其疏通知遠之德；然失之則誣。
- **《樂》**以教人，養其廣博易良之情；然失之則奢。
- **《易》**以教人，養其絜靜精微之智；然失之則賊。
- **《禮》**以教人，養其恭儉莊敬之行；然失之則煩。
- **《春秋》**以教人，養其屬辭比事之能；然失之則亂。

真正的儒者，能兼通六經之長而去其弊：
- 溫柔敦厚而不愚，則深於《詩》者也
- 疏通知遠而不誣，則深於《書》者也
- 廣博易良而不奢，則深於《樂》者也
- 絜靜精微而不賊，則深於《易》者也
- 恭儉莊敬而不煩，則深於《禮》者也
- 屬辭比事而不亂，則深於《春秋》者也

此說見聖人因材施教之旨，亦見六經相輔相成之理。
