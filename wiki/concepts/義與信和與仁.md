  **Righteousness, Trust, Harmony, and Benevolence** (義與信，和與仁) constitute the four
  cardinal virtues essential for effective governance, as articulated in the *Book
  of Rites: Explanation of the Classics* (禮記·經解).


  The passage establishes these four virtues as the "instruments" (器) of hegemon-kings
  (霸王之器):


  1. **和 (Harmony)**: When a ruler issues orders and the people respond with joy,
  this is called *harmony*.

  2. **仁 (Benevolence)**: When those above and those below share mutual affection,
  this is called *benevolence*.

  3. **信 (Trust)**: When the people obtain what they desire without having to beg
  for it, this is called *trust*.

  4. **義 (Righteousness)**: When the ruler removes the harms of heaven and earth,
  this is called *righteousness*.


  Crucially, the text warns: "Those who govern the people may have the intention to
  govern well, but without these instruments, they will not succeed." These four virtues
  are not mere abstract ideals—they are practical tools of statecraft. Without 義與信、和與仁,
  even the best intentions in governance will fail.


  This framework places ethical virtues at the center of political effectiveness,
  rejecting the notion that power alone can sustain rule.

**義與信，和與仁**出自《禮記·經解》，為孔子論治國之要道。經云：「義與信、和與仁，霸王之器也。」此四者乃霸王治世之具，不可或缺。

四德之義如下：

- **和**：發號出令而民說，謂之和。君王發布政令，民眾欣然奉行，是為和。
- **仁**：上下相親，謂之仁。君臣相親相愛，是為仁。
- **信**：民不求所欲而得之，謂之信。百姓所需自然得遂，不待祈求，是為信。
- **義**：除去天地之害，謂之義。驅除天災人禍，使民安寧，是為義。

經文又云：「有治民之意而無其器，則不成。」言治國者雖有好意，苟無此四者為工具，終不能成治功。

此說將道德修養與政治效能相結合，強調仁義信和非徒美德，乃治世之具也。
