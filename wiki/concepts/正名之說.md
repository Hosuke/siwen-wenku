content_legacy: '## English


  The logical passages on proper naming (正名) establish principles for correct designation
  and categorical distinction. The key formulation states: "Those who rectify names:
  ''that that'' may apply to that, ''this this'' may apply to this. ''That this''
  does not apply, for that [should be] moreover this. ''That this'' also applies,
  [for] that and this stop at that and this."


  This establishes the principle of one-to-one correspondence between names and their
  referents — each designation should apply consistently to its proper category without
  confusion or equivocation. The text analyzes cases of same and different naming,
  noting that when names are applied inconsistently, debate becomes impossible because
  neither party can establish victory.


  The discussion also addresses the problem of reference: how one can know that a
  designation correctly points to its intended object. This epistemological concern
  underlies the Mohist emphasis on clarity and precision in language, which they considered
  essential for effective governance and social order.

《墨子·經說下》正名之說，立命名之準則。蓋「彼彼此此可，彼此止於彼，此此止於此」，名與實當一一對應，不可混淆。彼此不可者，彼且此也，言名實相亂之弊。

同與異之分，是邏輯之要務。同名者，或謂之狗，其或謂之犬，是一名而二所指也。異名者，或謂之牛，牛或謂之馬，是二名而各有當也。俱無勝，是不辯也。辯也者，或謂之是，或謂之非，當者勝也。

又有指之論：子智是，有智是吾所無舉，重。則子智是而不智吾所無舉，是一。謂有智焉，有不智焉也。此論知識之確認，如何知所指與能指相符，乃墨家認識論之要義。
