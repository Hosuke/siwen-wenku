## English

**Héngchǎn héngxīn** (恒產恆心), meaning "constant property and constant heart/mind," is a key concept in *Mencius* · *Teng Wengong shàng*. Mencius declares: "The way of the people is this: those who have constant property have constant virtue; those who lack constant property lack constant virtue" (民之為道也，有恒產者有恒心，無恒產者無恒心).

This principle establishes the material foundation for moral character. Without stable means of livelihood, people lack the psychological stability necessary for virtuous conduct. When desperate, they will "放僻邪侈，無不為已" — abandon themselves to vice, crime, and chaos.
