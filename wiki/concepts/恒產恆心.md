  ## English


  **Héngchǎn héngxīn** (恒產恆心), meaning "constant property and constant heart/mind,"
  is a key concept in *Mencius* · *Teng Wengong shàng*. Mencius declares: "The way
  of the people is this: those who have constant property have constant virtue; those
  who lack constant property lack constant virtue" (民之為道也，有恒產者有恒心，無恒產者無恒心).


  This principle establishes the material foundation for moral character. Without
  stable means of livelihood, people lack the psychological stability necessary for
  virtuous conduct. When desperate, they will "放僻邪侈，無不為已" — abandon themselves to
  vice, crime, and chaos.


  The corollary is political: to govern justly, a ruler must ensure the people''s
  basic economic security. Mencius criticizes rulers who punish criminals after the
  fact ("及陷乎罪然後從而刑之，是罔民也") rather than preventing crime by providing adequate livelihoods.

孟子告齊宣王：「無恆產而有恆心者，惟士為能。若民，則無恆產，因無恆心。苟無恆心，放辟邪侈，無不為已。」明君制民之產，必使仰足以事父母，俯足以畜妻子；樂歲終身飽，凶年免於死亡。然後驅而之善，民之從之也輕。若仰不足以事父母，俯不足以畜妻子，樂歲終身苦，凶年不免於死亡，此惟救死而恐不贍，奚暇治禮義哉？
