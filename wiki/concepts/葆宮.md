## English

The Command Headquarters (葆宮) system represents the organizational heart of Mozi's defensive command structure. During a siege, the security of the command center determines the fate of the entire defense; if the headquarters falls or is compromised, the city inevitably follows. Mozi devotes considerable attention to the physical construction, access control, and operational protocols that protect this vital installation.

Physically, the 葆宮 is constructed with triple walls, each layer providing additional protection against espionage and attack. The walls are reinforced with clay pots and tiles (瓦釜), arranged in a pattern that both strengthens the structure and provides defensive positions for the guards. The headquarters is located on high ground, with watchtowers providing surveillance of the surrounding area while remaining concealed from below—the principle being that those below should not see those above, nor those above know whether those below are present or absent.

Access to the 葆宮 is strictly controlled through a system of passes (符). The gatekeeper maintains a register of all who enter and exit. Each person passing through the gate must present their credentials; those without proper documentation are detained until their identity can be verified. The gatekeeper himself operates under the direct authority of the city governor (太守), ensuring that no shortcuts or exceptions are permitted.

Within the headquarters, the intelligence network is carefully managed. Spies and agents (諜者) operating outside the walls are required to return to the 葆宮 regularly to report their findings. Their families receive special provisions—comfortable housing, regular meals, wine and meat—as incentives to ensure their loyalty and cooperation. When an agent returns from a mission, they are housed in separate quarters from their regular family, in a special guest building (異宮), until their debriefing is complete.

The text also describes the "three difficulties" (三難) that must be overcome to secure the headquarters: the outer perimeter must be fortified with watchtowers, the inner perimeter must also have watchtower coverage, and the building itself must be connected to the main defense by an elevated corridor (復道) extending fifty feet into the headquarters courtyard.

## 中文

葆宮保安制度，乃墨子城防守備之樞要也。城圍之時，葆宮之安危關係全城之存亡，故其制不得不密。

葆宮之牆必三重，牆之垣，守者皆累瓦釜牆上，此物理之防也。守樓臨質宮而善周，必密塗樓，令下無見上，上見下，下無知上有人無人，此情報之防也。

門有吏主者門里，筦閉必須太守之節，此人事之防也。葆衛必取戍卒有重厚者，請擇吏之忠信者、無害可任事者。令將衛自築十尺之垣，周還墻，門、閨者，非令衛司馬門。

葆宮之內，又有間諜之養。守入城，先以諜為始，得輒宮養之，勿令知吾守衛之備。諜者為異宮，父母妻子皆同其宮，賜衣食酒肉，信吏善待之。

發諜必使鄉邑忠信善重士，有親戚妻子，厚奉資之。必重發諜，為養其親若妻子，為異舍，無與員同所，給食之酒肉。遣他諜，奉資之如前諜。反，相參審，信，厚賜之。

又有三難之防：外環隅為之樓，內環為樓，樓入葆宮丈五尺為復道。葆不得有室，三日一發席蓐，略視之。布茅宮中，厚三尺以上。

## 日本語

葆宮の保安制度は、墨子の城防守備体系の組織的核心をなす。包囲之中、司令部の安全は全市の存亡を左右す。墨子は物理的構造、アクセス管理、Operationalな手順に注意を費やしたり。

物理的には、葆宮は三重の壁で囲まれ、各層は諜報活動と攻撃から追加の保護を提供す。壁は瓦と土器（瓦釜）で補強され、構造の強化と守卫の防御陣地を提供す。司令部は高台に位置し、見張塔は周围的状況を監視すると共に、下からは見通せぬ如くす。下なる者は上なる者を見ず、上なる者は下なる者がいるか否かを知らぬ，此れ情報防禦の原則なり。

葆宮への出入りは、符（通行証）による厳格な管理システムを通じて統制さる。門番は全て出入りする者の登録を維持す。各通行者は資格証明を提示せねばならぬ。適切な書類なき者は、身元確認が完了するまて拘留さる。門番自身は太守の直接の権威の下に置かれ、いかなる近道や例外も許されぬ。
