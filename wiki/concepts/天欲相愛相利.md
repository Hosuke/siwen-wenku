  **Heaven Desires Mutual Love and Mutual Benefit (天欲相愛相利)** is Mozi''s key argument
  for establishing Heaven''s moral authority. After claiming that Heaven should be
  the standard for governance, Mozi proceeds to explain what Heaven actually desires:
  "天必欲人之相愛相利，而不欲人之相惡相賊也" (Heaven necessarily desires that people love each other and
  benefit each other, and necessarily does not desire that people hate each other
  and harm each other).


  Mozi''s proof for this claim is empirical and observational: "何以知天之欲人之相愛相利，而不欲人之相惡相賊也？以其兼而愛之、兼而利之也"
  (How do we know Heaven desires mutual love and mutual benefit? Because Heaven universally
  loves and universally benefits [all creatures]). The supporting evidence is the
  fact that Heaven provides for all people without distinction: "今天下無小大國，皆天之邑也；人無幼長貴賤，皆天之臣也"
  (Now under heaven, there are no small or large states that are not Heaven''s cities;
  there are no people, young or old, noble or humble, who are not Heaven''s subjects).
  Thus, Heaven''s impartial provision for all is taken as evidence of Heaven''s impartial
  love for all.


  The logical consequence follows: if Heaven provides for all and loves all, then
  Heaven must desire mutual love and mutual benefit among people, not mutual hatred
  and harm. This argument establishes the moral content of [[天志]] and provides the
  foundation for Mozi''s doctrine of [[兼愛]] (universal love).

「天欲相愛相利」者，墨子論證天意之道德內涵也。墨子既言「法天」，遂進而問曰：「天何欲何惡哉？」答曰：「天必欲人之相愛相利，而不欲人之相惡相賊也。」

然則何以知天之欲相愛相利？墨子自問自答：「以其兼而愛之、兼而利之也。」——天兼而愛之，兼而利之，故知天欲人之相愛相利。

何為「兼而愛之、兼而利之」？墨子釋之曰：「今天下無小大國，皆天之邑也；人無幼長貴賤，皆天之臣也。」——普天之下，無論大國小國，皆天之土；無論幼長貴賤，皆天之民。天之於人，一視同仁，無有偏私。此所以為「兼」也。

由是觀之，天苟兼而有之、兼而食之，則天豈說以不欲人之相愛相利哉？——天既養育萬民，則天必欲萬民相親相愛、相利相惠，而不欲萬民相惡相賊。此墨子「天志」說之要義，亦其「兼愛」說之根據也。
