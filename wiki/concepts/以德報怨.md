  (以德報怨) represents one of the most nuanced ethical teachings in the Confucian tradition.
  According to the *Record of Standards* (表記), Confucius articulated the consequences
  of different response patterns:\n\n**With virtue, repay virtue:** This encourages
  the people and promotes moral cultivation. When kindness is met with kindness, social
  harmony is reinforced.\n\n**With resentment, repay resentment:** This disciplines
  the people and maintains social boundaries. Retribution serves as a deterrent.\n\n**With
  virtue, repay resentment:** This produces \"those of lenient disposition\" (寬身之仁)
  — people who, through self-cultivation, transcend the cycle of revenge but may appear
  weak.\n\n**With resentment, repay virtue:** This identifies \"people fit for punishment\"
  (刑戮之民) — those whose sense of justice has become fundamentally corrupted.\n\nThe
  text cites the *Book of Songs*: \"No word goes unanswered, no virtue goes unrequited\"
  (無言不仇，無德不報), establishing the principle of cosmic reciprocity. The *Great寓* (大甲)
  further reinforces: \"The people have no means of peace without a ruler; the ruler
  has no means to command the four quarters without the people.\"\n\nConfucius ultimately
  concluded: \"Those who, without desires, naturally embody benevolence; those who,
  without fears, naturally despise inhumanity — such persons are unique under heaven.\"
  The exemplary person (君子) therefore \"discusses the Way from within oneself\" and
  \"establishes laws according to the people,\" demonstrating that true virtue must
  be rooted in authentic self-cultivation rather than mechanical rule-following.\n\n\n##

**以德報怨**乃儒家重要倫理命題，見於《禮記·表記》。孔子論述其多重義蘊：

以德報德，則民有所勸；以怨報怨，則民有所懲。此正道義循環之理。善行得善報，則天下趨善；惡行得惡報，則姦邪斂跡。

以德報怨，則為「寬身之仁」。鄭玄注：「寬身之仁，若人以德報怨，身自寬厚矣。」此謂君子修身之效，不與小人較量，然非無原則之退讓。

以怨報德，則為「刑戮之民」。朱熹《四書章句集注》：「以怨報德，則為刻薄之人，終陷於刑戮矣。」此言忘恩負義之徒，必遭天譴。

孔子引《詩》曰：「無言不仇，無德不報。」又引《大甲》：「民非後無能胥以寧；後非民無以辟四方。」此明君民相依之理，亦天道好還之證。

終而言之：「無欲而好仁者，無畏而惡不仁者，天下一人而已矣。」蓋聖人之心，空明無滓，故能以德化怨，而非逞一時之快。此即儒家「以德報怨」之真義，非消极之容忍，乃積极之涵化也。
