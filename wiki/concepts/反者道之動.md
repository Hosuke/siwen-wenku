content_legacy: '## English


  **Reversal as the Movement of the Dao** (反者道之動) is a fundamental Daoist principle
  articulated in *Wenzi*, expressing the cyclical and returning nature of cosmic change.
  The concept teaches that all things in the universe move according to the pattern
  of reversal—the strong become weak, the weak become strong, and everything eventually
  returns to its opposite.


  In *Wenzi*, this principle is explicitly stated: "Reversal is the constant nature
  of the Dao; gentleness is its hardness; weakness is its strength." This means that
  what appears to be weakness (柔) is actually the Dao''s true strength, while apparent
  hardness is destined to fail.


  The principle operates on multiple levels:


  1. **Cosmic level**: The Dao gives birth to all things but does not possess them;
  it accomplishes tasks but does not claim credit. All things return to the Dao as
  their ultimate source.


  2. **Moral level**: Those who humble themselves will be exalted; those who yield
  will overcome; those who are soft will be strong.


  3. **Practical level**: "Accumulate softness and you become hard; accumulate weakness
  and you become strong. Observe what is accumulated, and you will know survival and
  destruction."


  The concept warns against rigidity and fixedness, teaching that those who cling
  to strength will be broken (兵強則滅，木強則折), while those who embrace yielding will endure.

**反者道之動** 出自《文子》，乃道家核心義理，謂天下萬物皆循反復之道運行，強者弱之，弱者強之，終歸於本原。《文子》云：「反者道之常也，柔者道之剛也，弱者道之強也。」

此理有三層義涵：

一曰**宇宙之理**。道生物而不有，成化而不宰，萬物恃之而生，莫知其德；恃之而死，莫之能怨。蓋道之動，在於使萬物各遂其性，終則復歸於道。所謂「應化無形」，「遂兮通兮，不虛動兮」，皆此義也。

二曰**處世之方**。以卑取尊，以退先，柔弱勝剛強。聖人知反覆之道，故能守柔處下，反而取勝。《文子》又言：「欲剛者必以柔守之，欲強者必以弱保之。」此聖人之智也。

三曰**修養之要**。積柔則剛，積弱則強。觀其所積，以知存亡。故「強勝不若己者，至於若己者而格；柔勝出於己者，其力不可量」。是以君子貴柔守弱，不與物爭，而天下莫能與之爭。
