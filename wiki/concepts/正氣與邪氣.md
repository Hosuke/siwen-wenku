content_legacy: '## English


  The *Wenzi* articulates a fundamental moral psychology distinguishing between 正氣
  (upright qi) and 邪氣 (crooked qi). Upright qi arises when one''s inner nature is
  properly aligned with righteousness and reason. It manifests as conduct that "follows
  principle and acts without attachment to things." Such a person is "internally convenient
  to their nature and externally accordant with righteousness." Upright qi is characterized
  by moderation, foresight, and a freedom from anxiety about consequences.


  Crooked qi, by contrast, emerges from indulgence in taste,声色 (sounds and colors),
  and unexamined emotions. It is driven by "pursuing flavors,淫於聲色, and issuing forth
  in joy and anger without any regard for future troubles." Crooked qi is the moral
  condition of the person enslaved to sensory pleasure and emotional reactivity.


  The text insists that these two states "相傷" (mutually harm each other) and "不可兩立"
  (cannot coexist). "一起一廢" (when one rises, the other falls) describes their exclusive
  relationship. The sage therefore "捐欲而從性" — abandoning desire to follow nature. This
  means governing the senses (目好色, 耳好聲, 鼻好香, 口好味) rather than being governed by them.
  Each sense "不知所欲，皆心為之制，各得其所" — not knowing its own desires, each is regulated by
  the heart-mind and attains its proper place.


  The practical consequence is that "內便於性，外合於義" produces moral authority. A ruler
  whose inner alignment is genuine does not need to compel obedience through force.
  The term [[精誠]] (sincerity and spiritual power) captures a related dimension of
  this alignment, while [[浩然之氣]] describes the expansive cultivated qi of the superior
  person. 正氣 differs from both: it is the baseline moral condition one achieves through
  proper governance of desire, without which crooked qi will necessarily arise.

《文子》此篇提出正氣與邪氣之辨，為道家心性修養之要義。正氣者，「內便於性，外合於義，循理而動，不繫於物」。其人動止皆依道理，不為外物所拘，無喜無怒，居處安然，寢夢皆寧。此乃體道者之氣象，以[[虛無恬淡]]之心合乎天理自然。

邪氣則反是。「推於滋味，淫於聲色，發於喜怒，不顧後患者」，皆邪氣之所生。溺於嗜欲，眩於聲色，喜怒任情而不節，此正氣消亡之兆。邪氣之害，不僅斫喪己德，亦且擾亂世事：「邪與正相傷，欲與性相害，不可兩立，一起一廢」。

二者之關係，既相反又相成——正盛則邪衰，邪盛則正微，終無中立之理。聖人明知此勢，故「捐欲而從性」。捐欲者，非無欲也，乃制欲而不為欲所制。「目好色，耳好聲，鼻好香，口好味，合而說之，不離利害」，然「耳目鼻口不知所欲，皆心為之制，各得其所」——感官各有其職，皆聽命於心，心治則五官皆得其正，心亂則邪氣乘之而起。

[[心術]]者，治心之術也。正氣之要在治心，心治則氣順，心亂則氣逆。此與[[守道虛無]]之義相通，皆以虛靜為本，以去欲為要先。
