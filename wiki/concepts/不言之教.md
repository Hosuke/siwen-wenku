content_legacy: '## English


  The "Teaching Through Non-Speech" (不言之教) is a central doctrine in Volume 2 of the
  *Wenzi*, presented through the voice of Laozi. The text states:


  > 聖人在上，懷道而不言，澤及萬民，故不言之教，芒乎大哉！


  Translation: "When the sage is above, he embraces the Way without speaking, yet
  his beneficence reaches the ten thousand people. Thus the teaching through non-speech
  is grandly vast!"


  The doctrine maintains that the highest form of instruction does not rely on verbal
  communication. Instead, the ruler''s inner virtue radiates outward naturally, transforming
  the people without explicit teaching. This contrasts with conventional pedagogy,
  which requires explicit instruction. The *Wenzi* argues that the sage''s silence
  is not an absence of teaching but rather its highest form—a teaching that operates
  through subtle influence and the alignment of one''s conduct with cosmic principles.


  The text further elaborates that true guidance comes through "不言之令，不視之見" (non-verbal
  commands, seeing without looking), suggesting that the sage governs through a kind
  of spontaneous order that emerges from his harmonious alignment with the Dao, rather
  than through explicit decrees or visible demonstrations.


  This doctrine connects to Wenzi''s broader emphasis on 精誠 (sincere spirit) and 神化
  (divine transformation) as the mechanisms through which the sage''s inner cultivation
  affects the external world.

「不言之教」為[[文子]]卷二所載老子之要義。其言曰：

「聖人在上，懷道而不言，澤及萬民，故不言之教，芒乎大哉！」

此義以為，最高之教化不在言辭，而在無聲之感化。聖人持道不言，其德自澤被萬民，此非無教，乃教之至大者也。

[[文子]]以為，不言之教所以為大，以其超越言說之限。言教者，以言語曉人以理；不言之教者，以身形氣化感人於無形。聖人之所以能不言而教，源於其內懷至道、外應萬物之境界。及其在上，天下自然從化，非由命令刑罰，乃由感應相通。

文中復言「不言之令，不視之見」，以明聖人之治，無需明詔大賞，而民自趨於善。此與[[無為而治]]相表裡，皆道家治道之要旨。

---

又，《徐無鬼》記仲尼之楚一章：「丘也聞不言之言矣，未之嘗言，於此乎言之。市南宜僚弄丸而兩家之難解，孫叔敖甘寢秉羽而郢人投兵。丘願有喙三尺。」其言不用言語而以行事解難，正是 [[不言之教]] 之義。
