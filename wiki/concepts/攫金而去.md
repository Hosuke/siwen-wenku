  ## English


  The Liezi recounts a striking anecdote about a man from Qí who had an insatiable
  desire for gold. One dawn, he dressed properly and went to the market. Passing by
  a gold merchant''s stall, he seized the gold and departed. Being apprehended by
  the officials, he was asked: "There were people all around you—how could you seize
  someone else''s gold?" He replied: "When I took the gold, I did not see any people.
  I saw only the gold."
