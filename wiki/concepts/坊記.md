  "Fang Ji" (坊記, Prevention through Propriety) is a chapter from the *Book of Rites*
  (禮記), traditionally attributed to the Confucian school and compiled during the Western
  Han dynasty. The chapter derives its name from its central concept: "坊" (fáng),
  meaning to dam, prevent, or restrain — as a dam prevents floodwaters from overflowing.


  The chapter opens with the master saying: "君子之道，辟則坊與？坊民之所不足者也" — "Is not the way
  of the gentleman like a dam? It damms up what is insufficient among the people."
  This establishes that ritual propriety (禮), punishment (刑), and commands (命) serve
  as preventive mechanisms against moral and social disorder.


  The chapter covers numerous topics: appropriate wealth distribution, hierarchical
  distinctions in dress and court positioning, the prohibition of same-surname marriage,
  gender separation protocols, proper mourning rites, correct attitudes toward profit
  and positions, and the proper attribution of credit and blame. Each section follows
  the formula: [ritual principle] → [consequence of following] → [consequence of transgression]
  → [citation from classic texts].


  The Fang Ji represents a systematic Confucian approach to social governance through
  ritual prevention rather than mere punitive correction.

《坊記》是《禮記》第四十二篇，全篇以「坊」為核心概念，論述禮義法度防範民心、節制人欲之功能。

「坊」字从土从方，本義為堤防，引申為防範、約束之意。篇首即云：「君子之道，辟則坊與？坊民之所不足者也。」言君子治國之道，如堤防之於水患，當先為民立坊，使有所依循，有所節制。

全篇大意可歸納為數端：
一、**節制財富**：聖人制富貴，使民富不足以驕，貧不至於約。
二、**明別尊卑**：貴賤有等，衣服有別，朝廷有位，使民有所讓。
三、**別男女之嫌**：「男女無媒不交，無幣不相見」，以防淫泆。
四、**慎婚姻之事**：「取妻不取同姓」，以厚別也。
五、**正喪祭之禮**：民之卒事，不可不慎。
六、**辨君臣之分**：「父母在，不敢有其身，不敢私其財」。

此篇與[[禮者治之治]]篇相表裡，皆論禮之治世功能。《坊記》尤重「坊」之義，以為聖人治民，當先防範於未然，不可待其犯法而後刑之。
