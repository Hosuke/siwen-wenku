## English

The doctrine of **死而不弔者三** (three circumstances where condolences should not be offered) appears in *Liji · Tan Gong Shang* as a specific taboo in mourning practice. The text states: "死而不弔者三：畏、厭、溺。"

These three cases represent deaths that occurred due to the deceased's own fault or improper conduct:

1. **畏** — death from fear or cowardice (as in suicide from facing shame)
2. **厭** (yā) — death from being crushed or suffocated (improper burial position)
3. **溺** — death by drowning

The principle underlying this prohibition is that such deaths result from improper conduct or failure of self-preservation, and therefore do not warrant the normal expressions of communal sympathy. A person who dies by their own action (such as from cowardice or recklessness) brings shame upon themselves and their family, unlike those who die in circumstances beyond their control.

This teaching relates to the broader Confucian emphasis on proper behavior even in extremity — see [[泰山其頹]] for Confucius's own dignified acceptance of death, and [[不奪人之喪亦不可奪喪]] for the principle that mourning should not be arbitrarily denied. Compare [[聞喪不得奔]] regarding the boundaries of mourning obligations.

## 中文

「死而不弔者三」出自《禮記·檀弓上》，記孔子之言曰：「死而不弔者三：畏、厭、溺。」

此三者皆死於非命，然其死也由於己之不當，非不幸之遭遇也：

一曰「畏」——死於畏懼，如自經溝瀆者，不敢抗爭而輕生是也。此類死，由於怯懦，故不可弔。

二曰「厭」——死於壓覆，如牆垣傾頹而壓死人，或居室崩壞而埋人者是也。此雖非自盡，然亦出乎人為之疏失。

三曰「溺」——死於溺水，或因涉水失足，或因舟楫覆沒皆是。此類死亡，亦涉己之疏忽。

三者之共同特點：皆死於己之不當，而非遭逢意外之不幸。儒家以為，此等人既不能謹慎以保其身，則其死不足以動人哀矜，故不為之弔。

此說與[[不奪人之喪亦不可奪喪]]之義相對：一為不可奪喪，此為不當弔。皆見儒家論喪禮之精微。
