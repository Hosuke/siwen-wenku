  **The Meaning of Village Drinking Ceremony** (*Xiang Yin Jiu Yi*) is a chapter from
  the *Book of Rites* (*Liji*) that systematically expounds the ritual significance,
  cosmological correspondences, and political-philosophical implications of the Village
  Drinking Ceremony (*鄉飲酒禮*).


  The text opens by articulating the fundamental principles underlying the ceremony:
  "尊讓絜敬" (deference, restraint, purity, and reverence). The host receives the guest
  outside the village school with three bowings, three deferential gestures, and three
  courteous declines before ascending—these ritualized courtesies embody "致尊讓" (the
  expression of deference). The washing of hands and raising of the cup signify "致絜"
  (the attainment of purity). The sequence of bowing upon arrival, bowing after washing,
  accepting the cup, presenting it empty, and completing the ceremony—all express
  "致敬" (the demonstration of reverence). Together, these constitute the manner in
  which superior persons (*君子*) interact with one another: through deference and reverence,
  disputes are avoided, and the disasters of violence are forestalled.


  The ceremony''s spatial arrangement maps onto cosmological principles. The guest
  and host respectively represent heaven and earth; the assistant (*介*) and substitute
  (*僎*) represent yin and yang; the three honored guests represent the three lights
  (sun, moon, and stars). The threefold deferral corresponds to the three days required
  for the moon to form its luminescence (*月之三日而成魄*). Seating at the four cardinal
  positions corresponds to the four seasons.


  The text elaborates the directional cosmology: the stern, condensed qi of heaven
  and earth originates in the southwest and flourishes in the northwest—this is "天地之尊嚴氣"
  and "天地之義氣" (the dignified and righteous qi of heaven and earth). The warm, generous
  qi originates in the northeast and flourishes in the southeast—this is "天地之盛德氣"
  and "天地之仁氣" (the abundant and benevolent qi of heaven and earth). The guest is seated
  in the northwest to embody righteousness (*義*), while the host occupies the southeast
  to embody benevolence (*仁*).


  The ceremony also demonstrates the principle "先禮而後財" (ritual precedes wealth). When
  the ritual cup is raised and the offerings of food are presented, these occur at
  the seat''s edge, indicating the ceremony is not primarily for eating and drinking
  but for the practice of ritual propriety. The emptying of the cup at the western
  steps similarly shows that the seat''s purpose transcends material concerns.


  Regarding age-based hierarchy (*尚齒*): those aged sixty may sit while those aged
  fifty stand in attendance. The soup and meat offerings increase by one dish for
  each decade of age (three dishes for sixty, four for seventy, five for eighty, six
  for ninety). This makes manifest the principle of honoring the elderly and provides
  for their nourishment.


  The musical performance demonstrates harmony without impropriety (*和樂而不流*). The
  progression from entering and ascending to sing three songs, the reed pipe entering
  for three performances, alternating songs, and the combined performance—all concludes
  with the appointment of a director of端正 (*司正*), who ensures the assembly remains
  orderly and joyful without degenerating into license.


  Confucius''s observation of this ceremony leads him to declare: "吾觀於鄉，而知王道之易易也"
  (Having observed the village, I understand how easily the King''s Way may be realized).
  The chapter concludes by establishing the ceremonial as the foundation of governance
  and education (*政敎之本*): "三賓者，政敎之本，禮之大參也" (The three honored guests represent the
  root of governance and education, the great triad of ritual).


  This text demonstrates the characteristic Rites school (*禮家*) methodology of reading
  microcosmic ritual forms as expressions of macrocosmic Dao, transforming mundane
  village hospitality into a paradigm of social and political order.

  『禮記』第四十五篇『鄉飲酒義』は、郷飲酒の儀礼の象徴的意味と政治的・宇宙論的意義を系統的に闡明する文章である。

  礼の天地を象り日月を法ぐる仕組みは、席次の配置に顕著に表れる。賓主は天地を象り、介と僎は陰陽を象り、三賓は三光（日・月・星）を象り、四面の座は四時を象る。三度の礼譲は「月の三日にして魄となる」ことに肖う。三度の辞退の儀は、建國に三卿を立てることに通じ、「礼には三讓あり、建國必ず三卿を立てる」と記される所以である。


  方位的気韻論は精緻を極める。天地厳凝の気は西南に始まり西北に盛んとなる、これは「天地の尊厳気」「天地の義気」である。天地温厚の気は東北に始まり東南に盛んとなる、これは「天地の盛徳気」「天地の仁気」である。賓は西北に座して人以後に義を接し、主は東南に座して人以後に徳厚を接する。仁義相接すれば、賔主有事し俎豆有数にして、聖道立つ。この理は[[仁-儒學-之]]と[[義-儒學-之]]の相関を証左する。


  礼の精髓は「先礼にして後に財す」にある。祭荐・祭酒は敬礼の表れ嚌肺は嘗礼の表れ、啐酒は成礼の表れである。席の端にて飲食の爲め而非ず、行礼の爲めなりと語る。觶を竭めて西階上に致すも、席の上が飲食の爲め而非ず、先礼にして後に財なるの義である。先礼にして後に財なれば、民敬讓作って爭わず。此れ「礼は理なり」の要義であり、[[禮尚往來]]の精神と相通じる。

《鄉飲酒義》為《禮記》第四十五篇，系統闡述鄉飲酒禮之義理、象徵意涵及政治哲學意蘊。

篇首揭櫫「尊讓絜敬」四字為全篇之主腦：主人於庠門之外拜迎，三揖而後至階，三讓而後升，是「致尊讓」之義；盥洗揚觶，是「致絜」之義；拜至、拜洗、拜受、拜送、拜既，是「致敬」之義。君子以尊讓相接，故能不爭；絜敬相待，故能不慢。不慢不爭，則遠於鬬辨，無暴亂之禍。此聖人製禮之本意也。

禮之象天法地，備見於座次之設：賓主象天地，介僎象陰陽，三賓象三光，四面之坐象四時。三揖之讓，象月之三日而成魄。三讓之儀，與建國立三卿相參，蓋「禮有三讓，建國必立三卿」也。

其論方向之氣，尤為精微：天地嚴凝之氣始於西南，盛於西北，此「天地之尊嚴氣」「天地之義氣」也；天地溫厚之氣始於東北，盛於東南，此「天地之盛德氣」「天地之仁氣」也。賓坐西北，接人以義；主坐東南，接人以德厚。仁義相接，則賔主有事，俎豆有數，而聖道立焉。

禮之精義，在乎「先禮而後財」：祭薦祭酒敬禮也，嚌肺嚐禮也，啐酒成禮也。於席末而言，是席之正非專為飲食，乃為行禮也。卒觶致實於西階上，言是席之上非專為飲食，乃先禮而後財之義也。先禮而後財，則民作敬讓而不爭。此「禮者理也」之要義，亦與[[禮尚往來]]所揭櫫之禮儀精神相發明。

至於尚齒之制：六十者坐，五十者立侍以聽政役，所以明尊長也；六十者三豆，七十者四豆，八十者五豆，九十者六豆，所以明養老也。民知尊長養老，而後乃能入孝弟；成教而後國可安。此[[尚齒]]之義，亦見於[[養老之禮]]之相關記載。

樂之和諧，亦有深義：工入升歌，笙入三終，間歌三終，合樂三終，樂備而出。一人揚觶，乃立司正焉。知其能和樂而不流，弟長而無遺，安燕而不亂。此[[樂與天地同和]]之義，亦見[[德音與溺音]]之辨。

孔子觀鄉而知王道易易，蓋鄉飲酒之禮，實為政治教化之本：「古之制禮也，經之以天地，紀之以日月，參之以三光，政教之本也。」三賓之設，乃「政教之本，禮之大參」者也。此與[[大學八條目]]修身、齊家、治國、平天下之次第相貫通。
