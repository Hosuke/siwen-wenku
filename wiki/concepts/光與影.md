content_legacy: '## English


  Mozi''s optical theories in the "Canon and Explaining the Canon" (經說下) present a
  sophisticated understanding of light and shadow formation based on geometric principles.
  The text examines how light travels in straight lines, how shadows are formed, and
  how reflections behave in plane and curved mirrors.


  The key passage describes the mechanism of shadow formation: "Light travels in straight
  lines like an arrow" (光之人煦若射). When light from a source reaches a person, the lower
  parts of the body reflect upward while the higher parts reflect downward due to
  the straight-line propagation of light. The feet block the lower light, creating
  a shadow above; the head blocks the upper light, creating a shadow below.


  For reflections, Mozi distinguishes between single and double light sources creating
  single versus double shadows, and analyzes curved mirror reflections where the focal
  point determines image size and orientation.

《墨子·經說下》之光學思想，論述光影形成之理，蓋以幾何原則為基。光之行也，直若射然，故光之人煦若射，下者之人也高，高者之人也下。足蔽下光，故成景於上；首蔽上光，故成景於下。此論閾明了光以直線傳播之本質。

論影之數：有「景」與「光」之別。景者，影也。光至則景亡，若光在，則盡古息。當一光之時，影單；光夾則影雙。鏡鑒之分：平鑒（平面鏡）與凹鑒（凹面鏡）之別。中之內者，凹面鏡近焦則所鑒大；中之外者，則遠焦所鑒小，而必易其正反。

光與影之說，為古代光學之重要論述，其幾何分析之精密，足見墨家之科學精神。
