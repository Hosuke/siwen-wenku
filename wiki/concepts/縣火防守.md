  **縣火防守** describes Mozi''s tactical deployment of suspended fire (縣火) as a defensive
  weapon against attacking forces. The text prescribes: "縣火，四尺一鉤樴，五步一竈，竈門有鑪炭" (Suspended
  fires at four chi intervals, braziers at five paces, with charcoal in the stove
  openings).


  The defensive sequence unfolds systematically. First, when enemies enter the city
  gate, they are met with flames: "令適人盡入，煇火燒門，縣火次之" (Order the attackers to enter
  completely, burn the gate with flaming fires, then the suspended fires). Meanwhile,
  defenders position themselves on departing equipment (出載而立) to cover the retreat.
  "兩載之閒一火，皆立而待鼓而燃火，即具發之" (A fire between every two departing loads, all standing ready,
  waiting for the drum to ignite and launch). If the enemy retreats to extinguish
  fires, "縣火復下" (the suspended fires descend again), creating relentless pressure.
  Mozi concludes that this harassment causes the enemy "甚病" (great suffering), often
  leading to their withdrawal.

**縣火防守**者，以懸火攻敵之術也。《墨子·備梯》述其部署：「縣火，四尺一鉤樴，五步一竈，竈門有鑪炭。」縣火者，懸於城上之高處，待敵攻城時投下之火攻具也。

其戰術運用，有嚴密之程序：先納敵入城門，「煇火燒門，縣火次之」，使敵陷入火海之中。城上守卒「出載而立，其廣終隊」，以待鼓聲為號。同時「兩載之閒一火，皆立而待鼓而燃火，即具發之」，形成火牆以截敵退路。敵若退而救火，則「縣火復下」，連續打擊，使敵「甚病」。如此運用縣火，配合[[行城襍樓|行城襍樓]]之防守，足以卻敵而解圍。此與一般之[[五兵|五兵]]不同，乃以火為主之特殊防禦手段，體現墨家善用地利、隨機應變之城守思想。
