content_legacy: '## English


  The "Seven Halls" (七廟) system represents the apex of the ancestral temple hierarchy
  in Zhou dynasty ritual practice, as codified in the *Book of Rites*. This elaborate
  structure reflected both filial piety and political legitimacy, serving as a visible
  manifestation of dynastic continuity and social order.


  According to *Regulations for Sacrifices* (祭法), the king''s seven halls comprised:


  **The Five Immediate Ancestor Halls (月祭之):**

  1. **考廟** (Father''s Hall) — dedicated to the most recent deceased father

  2. **王考廟** (Grandfather''s Hall) — dedicated to the paternal grandfather

  3. **皇考廟** (Great-grandfather''s Hall) — dedicated to the great-grandfather

  4. **顯考廟** (Great-great-grandfather''s Hall) — dedicated to the great-great-grandfather

  5. **祖考廟** (Founder''s Hall) — dedicated to the dynasty''s founder


  All five received monthly offerings (月祭).


  **The Two Distant Ancestor Shrines (祧):**

  Beyond the five halls were two additional distant ancestor shrines (有二祧), which
  received offerings only during the seasonal grand sacrifices (享嚐乃止).


  **The Degradation System:**

  The text outlines a carefully graduated system of spiritual degradation:

  - When a new ancestor was added, the oldest "Father''s Hall" would be promoted

  - The former "Founder''s Hall" would be moved to the distant ancestor shrine (遠廟為祧)

  - If a distant shrine became too ancient, it was degraded to an altar (去祧為壇)

  - An altar, if no longer prayed to, became a wild spirit (去壇為墠 → 去墠為鬼)


  This system ensured that only the most relevant ancestors remained in active worship
  while maintaining cosmic harmony through proper ritual attention to all lineage
  members.


  The parallel hierarchies for different ranks included:

  - **諸侯**: Five halls, one altar, one mound (五廟一壇一墠)

  - **大夫**: Three halls, two altars (三廟二壇)

  - **適士**: Two halls, one altar (二廟一壇)

  - **官師**: One hall (一廟)

  - **庶士庶人**: No hall, only ghost (無廟，死曰鬼)

七廟之制，乃周代宗廟制度之最高形態，見於《禮記·祭法》。此制以祖先靈庌之配置，反映宗法倫理與政治秩序，為歷代帝王宗廟制度之準則。

據《祭法》所載，天子七廟之制如下：

**近祖五廟（皆月祭之）：**
一、**考廟**——祀父；
二、**王考廟**——祀祖父；
三、**皇考廟**——祀曾祖父；
四、**顯考廟**——祀高祖父；
五、**祖考廟**——祀始祖。

此五廟皆行月祭，每月供奉。

**遠廟二祧：**
更有遠廟二祧，藏主其中，惟享嚐之際乃祭，尋常不預。

**遞降之制：**
七廟之行，有其遞降之序：
- 新主入廟，則舊主遞升；
- 遠廟（祖考以上）遷入二祧；
- 祧主過遠，則降為壇；
- 壇主無禱，則降為墠；
- 墠又無禱，則去之為**鬼**。

如此，則祖宗功德不忘，而祭祀亦有所節制。

諸侯五廟，大夫三廟二壇，適士二廟一壇，官師一廟，庶士庶人無廟而死曰鬼，各有差等。此宗廟之制，實與政治等級相表裏，昭示「慎終追遠」之義，亦見先王「分地建國，置都立邑」之封建秩序。
