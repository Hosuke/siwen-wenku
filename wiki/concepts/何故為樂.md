## English

The question **"何故為樂"** (Why do we make music?) appears in the *Mozi* chapter "Gongmeng" as part of Mozi's philosophical interrogation of Confucianism. This exchange reveals Mozi's logical method and his critique of what he considered empty verbal formulas.

When Mozi asked a Confucian scholar "Why do we make music?" the scholar replied, "Music is made for the sake of joy." Mozi immediately pointed out that this answer was tautological, like answering "Why do we build houses?" with "Houses are built for the sake of houses." A meaningful answer, Mozi suggested, should specify the practical benefits: just as houses provide shelter from cold in winter and heat in summer, and distinguish between male and female quarters, music too should serve identifiable social or moral purposes.

This critique reflects Mozi's broader utilitarian philosophy. For Mozi, all practices—including music—must demonstrate clear benefits to society (the three standards of ancient sage-kings, benefit to the people, and utility in governing). The Confucian answer "music for joy" failed to justify music's social utility and appeared to Mozi as mere aesthetic indulgence without moral grounding.

This exchange anticipates later Mohist debates about the value of ritual and music, and influenced later critiques of Confucian aestheticism as divorced from practical governance.

## 中文

**何故為樂**之問，出自《墨子·公孟》。墨子問於儒者曰：「何故為樂？」儒者曰：「樂以為樂也。」墨子以為此答等同詞反覆，未能揭示樂之本旨。

墨子先設問以例之：「今我問曰『何故為室』，曰『冬避寒焉，夏避暑焉，室以為男女之別也』，則子告我為室之故矣。」蓋造室有實際之用，故可答其故。而問樂，答以「樂以為樂」，是猶曰「何故為室」，曰「室以為室也」，以詞反覆，等於未答。

墨子之意，以為樂當有濟世利民之功用，不可徒為悅耳娛心之具。此與墨家功利主義相合，而與儒家「成於樂」「樂由中出」之說異趣。墨子以為，先王制樂，非以求樂，乃以為治天下之具。若專以為樂，則失其本義矣。

所出：《墨子·公孟》

## 日本語

**「何故為楽」**という問いは、《墨子・公孟》篇に登場する。儒者が「音楽は楽しみために作る」と答えたとき、墨子はこれが同義反復に過ぎないと批判した。

墨子は家を例に挙げ、「冬は寒さを避け、夏は暑さを避け、男女の違いを区別するため」という実用的な答えを示し、音楽も同様に社会的な目的を説明すべきだと論じた。儒者の答えは芸術的快楽への没頭を示唆し、功利主義的な墨家の哲学とは相容れなかった。

この対話から、墨家の実践的功利主義と儒家の審美的享楽主義との間の根本的な価値観の相違が明らかになる。

所出：《墨子・公孟》
