  **Ritual as a Dam** (禮坊止水) is a central metaphor from the *Book of Rites: Explanation
  of the Classics* (禮記·經解), illustrating ritual''s preventive function in maintaining
  social order.


  Confucius compares ritual to physical instruments of correction:


  - Just as a **scale** (衡) determines weight, ritual judges the轻重 of moral conduct

  - Just as a **plumb line** (繩墨) establishes straightness, ritual discerns the曲直
  of human action

  - Just as **compass and square** (規矩) measure circles and rectangles, ritual evaluates
  the方圓 of social propriety


  When these instruments are properly set, they cannot be deceived. Likewise, when
  the noble person (君子) thoroughly understands ritual, imposters cannot deceive with
  cunning or fraud.


  The passage then draws a striking analogy: ritual prevents social chaos just as
  a dam prevents flooding:


  > "Ritual, in regulating the state, is like the scale to weight, the plumb line
  to straightness, the compass and square to circles and rectangles. Therefore, if
  the scale is properly hung, one cannot be deceived about weight; if the plumb line
  is properly laid, one cannot be deceived about straightness; if the compass and
  square are properly set, one cannot be deceived about circles and rectangles; if
  the noble person thoroughly understands ritual, one cannot be deceived by cunning."


  The text warns against dismantling these protective barriers: those who destroy
  old dams will suffer flooding; those who abandon old ritual will face chaos. The
  conclusion is emphatic: "Ritual''s teaching and transformation is subtle—it stops
  evil before it takes form, causing people daily to move toward goodness and away
  from wrongdoing without their even realizing it."

**禮坊止水**出自《禮記·經解》，以水堤防範洪水為喻，闡明禮儀在維繫社會秩序中的預防功能。

孔子以度量之器比擬禮之用：

- 衡之於輕重——權衡定輕重，禮則辨是非
- 繩墨之於曲直——繩墨正曲直，禮則別邪正
- 規矩之於方圓——規矩定方圓，禮則正規模

器設而姦詐不能欺，君子審禮則姦詐無所用。

又以坊（堤防）止水喻禮止亂：

> 「禮之於正國也，猶衡之於輕重也，繩墨之於曲直也，規矩之於方圓也。」

又云：「以舊坊為無所用而壞之者，必有水敗；以舊禮為無所用而去之者，必有亂患。」

此喻見聖人防微杜漸之意——禮之教化也微，其止邪也於未形，使人日徙善遠罪而不自知。

人道經緯萬端，規矩無所不貫，誘進以仁義，束縛以刑罰，故德厚者位尊，祿重者寵榮，所以總一海內而整齊萬民也。禮之坊民，猶水之坊水，防其淫侈，救其彫敝，此禮之大用也。
