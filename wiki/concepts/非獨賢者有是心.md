  **"Not Only the Worthy Possess This Heart"** is a fundamental assertion in Mencius''s
  theory of human nature, found in Chapter 6 of "Gaozi Part I." This statement appears
  within the context of Mencius''s response to various theories about the nature of
  human beings.


  When asked about his claim that human nature is good, Mencius responds: "As to the
  feelings and emotions, they may be led to goodness—what I call good is precisely
  this. If one becomes not good, it is not the fault of one''s faculties." He then
  enumerates the four moral beginnings: the feeling of commiseration (惻隱之心, 仁), the
  feeling of shame and dislike (羞惡之心, 義), the feeling of deference and respect (恭敬之心,
  禮), and the feeling of right and wrong (是非之心, 智). These four constitute the seeds
  of [[四端]].


  The crucial point is that these moral emotions are not acquired externally: "仁義禮智，非由外鑠我也，我固有之也，弗思耳矣。」These
  virtues are inherent, not imposed from without. The problem is that people fail
  to reflect upon (弗思) this inherent moral nature.


  Mencius then introduces the concept that the capacity for moral action is universal:
  "非獨賢者有是心也，人皆有之，賢者能勿喪耳。" The worthiness of the sage does not consist in possessing
  a special moral faculty unavailable to others, but rather in the ability to preserve
  (勿喪) what all human beings naturally possess.


  He further illustrates through the analogy of physical appetites that all humans
  share similar capacities: just as all mouths share similar tastes and all ears share
  similar hearing, all hearts share a common approval of reason and righteousness
  (理也，義也). The sage simply recognizes this shared moral nature earlier: "聖人先得我心之所同然耳。"


  This universalist claim forms the philosophical foundation for Mencius''s belief
  that all humans can become like Yao and Shun (see [[人皆可以為堯舜]]).

「非獨賢者有是心」出自《孟子·告子上》第六章，乃孟子性善說之核心論述。孟子與公都子論性，首明惻隱、羞惡、恭敬、是非之心，人皆有之，此四者即[[四端]]之說。

孟子言：「惻隱之心，仁也；羞惡之心，義也；恭敬之心，禮也；是非之心，智也。仁義禮智，非由外鑠我也，我固有之也，弗思耳矣。」此數語的要，在明善性非外鑠，而是吾心本有；常人未暇深思，故昧而不察耳。

及門人問：「性善之說，彼皆非與？」孟子答曰：「乃若其情則可以為善矣，乃所謂善也。若夫為不善，非才之罪也。」蓋為不善者，非本性使之然，乃放失其心之所致。

繼而孟子明言：「非獨賢者有是心也，人皆有之，賢者能勿喪耳。」此語的要，在破「性善為聖賢所獨有」之疑。孟子之意：善性人人本具，賢者之所以為賢，不在有別於人之善性，而在能保守勿喪之耳。

孟子又以口耳目之事為喻：「口之於味也，有同嗜焉」「耳之於聲也，有同聽焉」「目之於色也，有同美焉」，而「至於心，獨無所同然乎？」心之所同然者，[[義-儒學-之|義]]也，理也。聖人先得我心之所同然，故理義悅我心，猶芻豢悅我口。
