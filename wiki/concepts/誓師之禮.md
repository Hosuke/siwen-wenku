  The pre-battle oath ceremony (誓師之禮) in Mozi''s "Ying Di Ci" represents a comprehensive
  ritual program for mobilizing the city for war, combining ancestral worship, public
  oaths, and coordinated military action.


  **Sacrificial Phase**: Priests and historians first announce the impending battle
  to the Four Directions (四望), mountains, rivers, and the community altar (社稷). They
  offer sacrifices in front of the army''s banners, then retreat. The ruler, dressed
  in plain white (素服), takes a solemn oath in the Grand Temple (太廟):


  "The enemy practices the way of non-benevolence, does not cultivate righteousness,
  and seeks only to be king. They say: ''I will certainly destroy your altars, ruin
  your state, and exterminate your people.'' The sons of your ancestors nightly protect
  themselves from disaster, laboring to assist me. Let us unite our hearts and combine
  our strength, each from left and right, each dying to defend.''"


  After the oath, the ruler dines and lodges to the right of the central temple, with
  the priests stationed at the altar.


  **Combat Phase**: When all officials are prepared, fighting begins. Drums sound
  at the gates; flags are planted on the right, banners on the left. Archers fire
  three volleys of arrows, and victory is announced when all five weapon types are
  readied. Then the forces descend from the walls, depart through the gates, and ascend
  vantage points outside the city to observe the battlefield.


  **Assault Procedure**: Commands are given, trumpets sound, and the military marshal
  (司馬) leads archery salvos from the right gate, using reeds and thatch arrows. Bowmen
  with crossbows follow. Infantry emerges from the left gate, waving and throwing
  wooden and stone projectiles. Finally, the priests and lineage officers announce
  victory at the altar, and the ritual concludes with an steaming vessel (甑) placed
  over the offerings—a symbolic renewal of communal bonds through shared sacrifice.



  ## 日本語


  誓師の禮とは、出陣前に廟において行われる祭祀と宣誓の儀式である。『墨子』「迎敵祠」篇の末尾に詳しい。


  祝・史はまず四方、山川社稷に戰爭開始を告知し、次いで君主は素服で太廟において嚴かに誓う：「敵は不道を行い、義を修めず、ただ王たれんと欲す。必ずや汝らの社稷を滅ぼし、百姓を絶たんとする。われら心を一つにし力を合わせ、左右より死を賭して守るべし」と。


  全ての役人が準備を整えるや、戰闘が始まる。鼓は門で鳴り、旗は右に、旌は左に據えられる。弓兵は三射し、五種の武器が全て整った時点で勝利を宣言する。兵力は城壁から降下し、城門から出て城外の高臺に昇り、戦場を見守る。


  最後に祝・史・宗人が社に勝利を報告し、甑を覆せて儀式を閉じる。これは共有の犠牲を通じて共同體の絆を更新する象徵的行為である。墨家の非攻・兼愛の理念に基づき、防御戰でも正當な理由を持つことが強調されている。'

誓師之禮者，出征前祭祀誓眾之儀也。墨子所述迎敵祠末章，詳載此禮：

**祭祀程序**：祝、史乃告於四望山川社稷，先於戎，乃退。公素服誓於太廟，曰：「其人為不道，不脩義詳，唯乃是王，曰：『予必懷亡爾社稷，滅爾百姓。』二參子尚夜自厦，以勤寡人，和心比力兼左右，各死而守。」既誓，公乃退食，舍於中太廟之右，祝、史舍于社。

**戰鬥程序**：百官具御，乃斗，鼓于門，右置旂、左置旌于隅，練名，射參發，告勝五兵咸僃。乃下，出挨，升望我郊。乃命鼓，俄升，役司馬射自門右，蓬矢射之，茅參發，弓弩繼之，校自門左，先以揮，木石繼之。

**復命程序**：祝、史、宗人告社，覆之以甑。

其誓詞大義：敵人不脩義德，唯以力爭，欲滅吾社稷、百姓。吾當上和天意，下安民心，和心比力，死守不退。此墨家非攻兼愛之義，雖處守勢，而師出有名，理直氣壯也。
