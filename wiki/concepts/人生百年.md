  **"人生百年"** (The Hundred-Year Life Stages) is a passage from *The Book of Rites*,
  *Qu Li Shang*, outlining the ages and appropriate activities of human life from
  childhood to old age. The text states:


  "人生十年曰幼，學。二十曰弱，冠。三十曰壯，有室。四十曰強，而仕。五十曰艾，服官政。六十曰耆，指使。七十曰老，而傳。八十、九十曰耄，七年曰悼；悼與耄，雖有罪，不加刑焉。百年曰期，頤。"


  This framework establishes that at ten one is a "youth" (幼) devoted to learning;
  at twenty a "weak youth" (弱) who undergoes the capping ceremony; at thirty a "strong
  adult" (壯) who establishes a household; at forty a "vigorous adult" (強) who enters
  official service; at fifty one is "venerable" (艾) and capable of governing; at sixty
  one is "elderly" (耆) and may direct others; at seventy one is "old" (老) and may
  pass on responsibilities; at eighty or ninety one is "decayed" (耄) and exempt from
  punishment even for crimes; and at one hundred one is in the "awaiting stage" (期)
  requiring care (頤).


  This passage reflects the Confucian emphasis on [[修身]] (self-cultivation) appropriate
  to each stage of life, and connects to [[養老之禮]] (rites for the elderly) and the
  broader system of [[絜矩之道]] (the measuring square of proper conduct).

**人生百年**出自《[[禮記]]·曲禮上》，詳述人之一生年齒之序與所當為。其文曰：「人生十年曰幼，學。二十曰弱，冠。三十曰壯，有室。四十曰強，而仕。五十曰艾，服官政。六十曰耆，指使。七十曰老，而傳。八十、九十曰耄，七年曰悼；悼與耄，雖有罪，不加刑焉。百年曰期，頤。」

十年曰幼，當及時而學。二十曰弱，行冠禮以示成人。三十曰壯，居室以立家。四十曰強，出仕以任事。五十曰艾，歷練既深，可以服官政。六十曰耆，精力漸衰，但可指麾部署。七十曰老，致仕傳家事於子孫。八十、九十曰耄，年邁智衰，雖有小罪，不加刑罰，示矜恤也。百年曰期，頤養天年，待人奉養而已。
