## English

In the *Mozi* chapter "Gongmeng," Mozi presents a systematic critique of Confucianism, identifying **four policies** that he believes are sufficient to bring ruin to the world. This passage represents one of the earliest and most direct attacks on Confucian philosophy in ancient Chinese literature.

The first criticism concerns the denial of spiritual forces: Confucianism treats Heaven as non-intelligent and ghosts as non-spiritual, failing to satisfy the spirits and ghosts. Such teachings can bring ruin to the world. The second criticism targets excessive mourning practices—the elaborate burials with multiple coffin layers, abundant burial garments, treating funeral arrangements like moving house, three years of weeping supported by others, walking with staffs, ears unable to hear, eyes unable to see—all sufficient to bring ruin. The third criticism condemns musical practices—stringed instruments, singing, drumming, dancing, habitual engagement with sound and music—sufficient to bring ruin. The fourth criticism indicts fatalism—maintaining that poverty, wealth, longevity, death, order, chaos, safety, and danger are entirely determined by fate, incapable of increase or decrease. When rulers follow this, they will not govern; when inferiors follow it, they will not labor.

Chengzi (a Confucian disciple) protested that Mozi was slandering Confucianism, to which Mozi replied that if Confucianism indeed lacked these four faults, then his criticism would indeed be slander. But since Confucianism possesses these four faults, his criticism merely reports what exists.

## 中文

《墨子·公孟》篇中，墨子論述**儒之道足以喪天下者四政**，系統批判儒學之弊，此為先秦諸子中最早批判儒學之重要文獻。

第一，天鬼不悅。儒者以天為不明，以鬼為不神，天鬼不說，此足以喪天下。第二，厚葬久喪。重重棺槨，多為衣衾，送死若徙，三年哭泣，扶後起，杖後行，耳無聞，目無見，此足以喪天下。第三，弦歌鼓舞。習為聲樂，此足以喪天下。第四，以命為有。謂貧富壽夭、治亂安危有極，不可損益。為上者行之，必不聽治；為下者行之，必不從事。

程子聞之，謂墨子毀儒。墨子答曰：「儒固無此若四政者，而我言之，則是毀也。今儒固有此四政者，而我言之，則非毀也，告聞也。」言己之批判，乃據實而論，非毀謗也。

所出：《墨子·公孟》

## 日本語

《墨子・公孟》篇において、墨子は儒学の**四つの政弊**を体系的に批判した。これは先秦諸子百家の文献中最古の儒学批判之一である。

第一に、天鬼を不快にする。儒者は天を不明瞭とし、鬼を神明としない。第二に、厚葬久喪。重い棺桶、数々の衣料、埋葬を搬家のように扱い、三年間の哭泣、杖に頼って歩く。第三に、弦歌鼓舞。音楽と舞踊に耽る。第四に、運命論。貧富・寿命・治乱・安全・危険は全て運命で決定され、変更不可能と主張する。

程子が墨子の儒学批判に抗議すると、墨子は「儒学にこの四つの弊害がなければ、これは誹謗となる。しかし今、儒学にはこの四つの弊害があるのだから、これは報告に過ぎない」と反論した。

所出：《墨子・公孟》
