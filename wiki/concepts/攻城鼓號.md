  The **Siege Drum and Signal System (攻城鼓號)** documented in the Mozi established a
  graduated communication protocol that tracked enemy movements through distinct geographical
  stages during a siege assault.


  ### The Eight-Stage Approach System


  The system assigned specific drum beats and flag numbers to each stage of enemy
  penetration:


  | Stage | Location | Drum Beats | Flags Raised | Penetration Level |

  |-------|----------|------------|--------------|-------------------|

  | 1 | 前池外廉 (Outer edge of front moat) | 3 | 1 | Initial contact |

  | 2 | 水中周 (Around the water) | 4 | 2 | Moat crossing |

  | 3 | 藩 (Defense barrier) | 5 | 3 | Outer defenses |

  | 4 | 馮垣 (Low wall) | 6 | 4 | First wall breach |

  | 5 | 女垣 (Parapet wall) | 7 | 5 | Inner defenses |

  | 6 | 大城 (Main city wall) | 8 | 6 | City walls |

  | 7+ | 乘大城半以上 (Over half the city) | Continuous | — | Critical stage |


  ### Night Attack Protocols


  During nighttime assaults, drums were supplemented with fire signals: "夜以火，如此數"
  — "At night, use fire signals, following the same numbers." The drums continued
  without cease as the enemy advanced: "乘大城半以上，鼓不休" — "When they scale over half the
  city, beat drums without rest."


  ### Retreat Signaling


  When enemy forces withdrew, the system reversed: "寇卻解，輒部幟如進數，而無鼓" — "When the enemy
  retreats and disperses, arrange flags according to the advance numbers, but without
  drums." This absence of drums clearly communicated withdrawal without revealing
  defensive movements.


  ### Officer Deployment Flags


  The system also coordinated officer positioning: officers were marked by flags on
  different body positions — "城上吏卒置之背，卒於頭上，城下吏卒置之肩，左軍於左肩，右軍於右肩，中軍置之胷" — defenders
  on the back, soldiers on the head, below the wall on shoulders, left army on left
  shoulder, right army on right shoulder, central army on the chest.

《墨子》攻城鼓號之制，以鼓聲與幟數標定敵軍攻城進度，構成階梯式防禦指揮體系。

### 八進之數

敵軍漸近，城上依次遞增鼓數、幟數：

- 寇傅前池外廉——鼓三，舉一幟
- 到水中周——鼓四，舉二幟
- 到藩——鼓五，舉三幟
- 到馮垣——鼓六，舉四幟
- 到女垣——鼓七，舉五幟
- 到大城——鼓八，舉六幟
- 乘大城半以上——鼓無休息

### 夜攻之號

夜以火代鼓，循此數而施放信號。敵乘城半以上，則鼓聲不止，以警全城。

### 退兵之號

「寇卻解，輤部幟如進數，而無鼓。」退去敵軍時，依前數部幟而停鼓，無鼓即示退兵之象。

此制與 [[蛾傅]] 等攻城術相對應，構成墨家完整的攻城退攻信號體系。
