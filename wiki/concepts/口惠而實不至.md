## English

The concept **口惠而實不至** (Promises Without Fulfillment) appears in the 表記 as a cautionary warning about the dangers of insincere speech. Confucius states: "子曰：口惠而實不至，怨菑及其身，是故君子與其有諾責也，寧有已怨。" — "The Master said: When verbal kindness is not followed by actual fulfillment, resentment and disaster will reach one's person. Therefore, the superior person would rather incur resentment for having refused than be blamed for a broken promise."

This passage addresses a fundamental tension in Confucian ethics between the virtue of 恕 (reciprocal consideration) and the dangers of false generosity. The phrase "口惠" (verbal kindness/bounty) refers to gracious words and promises made without the intention or ability to fulfill them—essentially a form of moral deception. When such promises inevitably fail to materialize, the disappointed recipient naturally feels resentment, which the text warns "菑及其身" (reaches the person himself).

The solution Confucius proposes is counterintuitive: "與其有諾責也，寧有已怨" — better to be criticized for refusing a request than to be condemned for failing to fulfill a promise. This prioritizes reliability over superficial agreeableness, establishing that one's word must be one's bond. A君子 who refuses a request honestly preserves both his integrity and the recipient's accurate expectations, whereas one who makes false promises destroys trust and invites exactly the resentment he sought to avoid.

This teaching connects to the broader 表記 discussion of [[不以辭盡人]], which distinguishes between the "water-like" integrity of the superior person and the "sweet wine" corruption of the petty person who indulges in pleasing rhetoric without substance.

The chapter also quotes from the 國風: "言笑晏晏，信誓旦旦。不思其反，反是不思，亦已焉哉！" — "Their words were warm and smiling, their vows were most sincere. They did not consider reversal; when reversed, they did not reconsider, so it ended thus!" This poem illustrates the eventual collapse of relationships built on insincere promises.

## 中文

「口惠而實不至」見於《禮記·表記》，孔子以此為修身之戒。其言曰：「子曰：口惠而實不至，怨菑及其身，是故君子與其有諾責也，寧有已怨。」

「口惠」者，口頭之施惠、空言之施恩也。徒以言詞悅人，而無實惠及人，則是欺飾之行也。及其不踐前言，則受者心生怨望，而此怨望必返於其身。故君子與其許諾而見責於人，寧可先時拒絕而受人怨尤。

何以言之？蓋有諾責者，言已出口而事不及成，是不信之實也。有已怨者，求人不遂而暫生怨心，是一事之私也。不信之失大，已怨之恨小。故君子取其小而避其大也。

此與《表記》前文所言「君子之接如水，小人之接如醴」之義相通。君子之交淡如水，言約而信行；小人之交甘如醴，言甘而事乖。淡以成者，水雖無味而能滋潤萬物；甘以壞者，醴雖可口而不能久置。

又引《國風》之言：「言笑晏晏，信誓旦旦。不思其反，反是不思，亦已焉哉！」此詩言人初時誓言坦然，及其相反，悔而不及。此正口惠不終之戒也。

## 日本語

「口惠而実不至」は『禮記·表記』に見える言葉であり、空虚な約束の危険性を警告する。 Confuciusは「口惠みて實際に至らざれば、怨菑は其の身に及ぶ。是故に君子は其の諾責あるも、寧ろ已怨あるを佳とす」と述べる。

この教えは、表面的な思いやりと実際の行動の間の緊張を明らかにする。「口惠」とは、見せかけの親切や、履行する意思や能力のない約束を意味するSuch promises inevitably fail, leading the disappointed recipient to feel resentment—resentment that inevitably returns to the person who made the false promise.

孔子の解決策は逆説的である。約束を断ったことで一時的な批判を招く方が、破られた約束で非難されるよりも良い。この教えは、見かけ上の好感より信頼性を重視し、言葉と行動の一致を保つことの重要性を強調する。
