  ## English


  The doctrine "以政治國，以奇用兵" represents a core Daoist strategic principle found in 《文子·卷十二》:
  "以政治國，以奇用兵。先為不可勝之政，而後求勝於敵。"


  This concept establishes a fundamental distinction between two domains of action:


  **Governance (政)**: The state should be governed through proper, upright means (正).
  This involves establishing institutions, cultivating virtue, and creating systems
  that cannot be defeated. "先為不可勝之政" means first establishing a political order that
  cannot be overcome — one that is inherently stable, legitimate, and strong.


  **Warfare (兵)**: Military affairs, however, operate under different principles.
  Success in battle comes through strangeness or surprise (奇) — strategic deception,
  unconventional tactics, and exploiting the enemy''s mistakes. The military sphere
  rewards cunning, speed, and unpredictability.


  The passage continues: "以未治而攻人之亂，是猶以火應火，以水應水也，同莫足以相治，故以異為奇。" To attack another''s
  chaos while your own governance is not yet ordered is like responding to fire with
  fire, water with water. The same cannot govern the same, therefore one uses strangeness
  (奇) to achieve victory.


  This principle cautions against applying peacetime ethics to wartime or vice versa.

周西伯陰謀修德以傾商政，其事多兵權與奇計，故後世之言兵及周之陰權皆宗太公為本謀。以政治國、以奇用兵，太公兼之也。

以政治國，以奇用兵。先為不可勝之政，而後求勝於敵，以未治而攻人之亂，是猶以火應火，以水應水也，同莫足以相治，故以異為奇。

**所出**：《文子 · 卷十二》 — https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/文子/卷十二

治國貴乎正。 正者，不偏不倚，合於道法者也。以正道蒞民，則民有所準而風俗淳。以奇用兵。 奇者，詭譎權變，因時制宜，不拘常法者也。兵者不祥之器，聖人不得已而用之，故以奇用之。以無為取天下。 無為者，因其自然，不擾民也。聖人無為而天下自化，何必用力哉。
