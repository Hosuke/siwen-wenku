## English

The chapter "Preparing Against Water" (備水) from the Mozi presents a systematic approach to defending fortified cities against water-based siege attacks. The text outlines several key defensive measures:

1. **Elevation Survey**: Before constructing city defenses, one must carefully measure the height of the surrounding terrain on all four sides. The city itself should be positioned in a naturally lower area, with channels dug to direct water flow inward.

2. **Underground Drainage**: Deep trenches should be dug beneath the city, creating a network that allows water to seep away. Wells should be fitted with drainage channels (瓦井) positioned to monitor external water levels.

3. **水位 Monitoring**: When external water levels rise to a height of one zhang (approximately 3.3 meters) or more above the city floor, workers should凿穿 (bore through) the inner walls to release pressure and prevent catastrophic flooding.

4. **Naval Counter-Offensive**: Ten attack platforms (臨) should be prepared, each manned by 30 archers skilled with crossbows. These platforms are organized into fleets of twenty vessels, commanded by thirty selected warriors who are also proficient in close combat with spears, armor, and halberds.

5. **Strategic Positioning**: Families of these elite soldiers are housed separately and provided for, ensuring their loyalty and commitment during the siege.

6. **Combined Arms Defense**: Archers positioned on the city walls provide covering fire to support the naval assault launched from the water defense platforms (轒轀), which breach external embankments.

This passage demonstrates Mozi's sophisticated understanding of siege warfare and his emphasis on both defensive engineering and offensive counter-measures when facing technologically advanced attacks.

## 中文

《備水》為《墨子》城守諸篇之一，述城邑防水攻之術。其法有備內外二途：

**城內之備**：城外周道廣八尺，塹水以防。城內地勢宜俾下，令水歸於城中窪地。又穿地深隧，引水泄漏。城中置瓦井以測水位，若外水高丈以上，則鑿城內水道以泄之。

**城外之備**：則以決水攻敵為要。並船十艘為一臨，臨三十人，人皆擅弩。二十船為一隊，選材士有力者三十人共船。其二十人各擅有方，執劍甲鞮瞀；又十人擅長操持禾苗。先養材士，別舍以居，食其父母妻子以為質。視水可決，則以臨船載轒轀而出，決外堤以灌敵。城上為射檥，疾佐之。

此篇與 [[備梯]] [[備城門]] 諸篇相類，皆墨子守城之學。[[五兵]] 之用，[[蛾傅]] 之法，於此皆可見其端倪。

## 日本語

『墨子・備水』篇は、水攻めに対する城郭防衛の術を詳述す。

一、地形測量。城の内側と外側の周囲的道路の幅は八尺とし、四方の地形の高低を慎重に測定す。城の地は周囲より低く構え、水を引き込む溝を設けておく。

二、地下排水。城下深く穴を穿ち、漏泉（浸透井戸）を設けて水を逃がす。外壁には水位監視用の井戸を配置し、外水が一丈（約三・三メートル）以上になったとき、内壁に穴を開けて泄水す。

三、水上反撃。十の臨（攻撃用舟艇）を備え、各臨に弩使い三十人を配置す。二十舟を一隊とし、材士有力者三十人で操船す。其の中に矛・戟・剣甲・鞮瞀に長ずる者二十人、苗矛の使い手十人を置く。

四、水攻の好機到来せば、轒轀（攻城車両）を以て決隄し、外敵を水没させる。城上より射檥（弓兵拠点）が援護射撃を加う。

本篇は[[守圍城之法]]と関連し、水攻という特殊な攻城法への対応を論じた墨家防衛思想の精髓たり。
