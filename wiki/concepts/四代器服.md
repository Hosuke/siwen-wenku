## English

The *Mingtang Wei* chapter provides an extensive comparative catalog of ritual vessels (禮器) and ceremonial garments (祭服) across the four legendary and historic dynasties: Youyu (有虞氏, the Yao-Shun period), Xia (夏), Shang (殷), and Zhou (周). This systematic comparison illustrates the Confucian principle that the sages "added and subtracted according to the times" (聖人因時損益).

**Chariots and Banners:**
- Youyu: Luan carriages (鸞車) and banner (綏)
- Xia: Gou carriages (鉤車) and banner (綏)
- Shang: Great carriages (大路)
- Zhou: Cheng carriages (乘路)
- Colors: Youyu preferred black; Shang used white with black heads; Zhou employed yellow with red manes

**Wine Vessels:**
- Youyu: Tai vessels (泰)
- Xia: Mountain lei (山罍)
- Shang: Zhu vessels (著)
- Zhou: Xi xiang (犧象)

**Drumming Implements:**
- Xia: Foot drums (足鼓)
- Shang: Pillar drums (楹鼓)
- Zhou: Suspended drums (縣鼓)

**Sacrificial Vessels (俎):**
- Youyu: Kuan (梡)
- Xia: Jue (嶡)
- Shang: Ju (椇)
- Zhou: Fang zu (房俎)

**Ceremonial Garments:**
- Youyu: Wei bindings (韍)
- Xia: Mountain patterns (山)
- Shang: Fire patterns (火)
- Zhou: Dragon patterns (龍章)
