## English

The **Liji** (Book of Rites) contains detailed regulations regarding the handling of torches during rituals, particularly in nighttime ceremonies or when guests arrive after dark. The text states: "為獻主者，執燭抱燋，客作而辭，然後以授人。執燭不讓，不辭，不歌。"

This passage describes the duties of the **獻主** (host-official) who presents offerings. When a guest arrives during evening hours, the host-official carries the torch (**燭**) and holds the torch-stand (**燋**). When the guest rises to decline, the host-official then hands these over to the guest. However, the regulations specify that when carrying a torch, one should not yield the right of way (**不讓**), should not make excuses or decline offers (**不辭**), and should not sing (**不歌**).

These prohibitions likely served practical purposes: a torch-bearer needed full attention on the flame and their path, making yielding or elaborate social courtesies dangerous. The prohibition against singing while holding a torch preserved the solemnity of the ritual context. This demonstrates how **少儀** (Minor Rituals) governed even the most practical aspects of ceremonial life.

The passage continues with related instructions: "洗盥執食飲者勿氣，有問焉，則辟咡而對" — those who have washed and are handling food and drink should not release their breath (at the offerings), and if asked a question, they should answer while turning away slightly.

## 中文

《[[禮記]] · [[少儀]]》記執燭之禮，其文云：「為獻主者，執燭抱燋，客作而辭，然後以授人。執燭不讓，不辭，不歌。」

此節所言者，夜間祭祀或宴饗之時，主人之執事者持燭之禮也。

所謂「獻主」者，代人行祭、主人未至而代替行禮之人也。獻主之禮，與主人略同，然亦有差異。客人至而天晚，獻主當執燭抱燋以迎之。客人起立而辭讓，則獻主以其燭燋授之。

然持燭之時有三禁：
一曰「不讓」——持燭之時，不得讓道讓人。蓋燭火易熄，持者須專心視燭，若讓道恐有失也。
二曰「不辭」——持燭之時，人有以物相惠者，不得辭謝。蓋辭讓必多言，多言則分心，分心則燭危也。
三曰「不歌」——持燭之時，不可歌詠。蓋歌詠必忘神，神分則燭滅，且歌聲喧嘩，非所以敬神明也。

又有云：「洗盥執食飲者勿氣，有問焉，則辟咡而對。」言執事者在行祭之前，必先盥洗。及其執飲食以待神也，當屏息以向神，不敢以氣觸之。若有人問之，則辟咡而對——辟咡者，側口以對，恐氣及人也。

此皆《[[中庸-概論]]》所言「戒慎乎其所不睹，恐懼乎其所不聞」之意，蓋祭祀之時，無一舉動可以輕忽也。
