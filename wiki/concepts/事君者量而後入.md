  ## English


  The **"Minor Rules of Deportment"** (少儀) articulates a fundamental principle of
  ministerial conduct:


  > **事君者量而後入，不入而後量；凡乞假於人，為人從事者亦然。然，故上無怨，而下遠罪也。**


  This passage, though brief, addresses one of the most critical issues in Confucian
  political philosophy: the relationship between personal judgment and institutional
  obligation.
