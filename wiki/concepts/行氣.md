## English

**行氣** (circulating qi) is a fundamental Daoist cultivation practice discussed extensively in *Baopuzi*. According to this text, the practice can be pursued alone for a lifespan of several hundred years, or combined with alchemical elixirs for accelerated effects. The practice is described as essential for those who wish to cultivate immortality, as sexual energy techniques alone cannot be effective if the body is depleted through exhaustion.

The text catalogs numerous extraordinary abilities demonstrated by accomplished practitioners of *xingqi*: they could enter plague-stricken areas without contracting illness, sit beside infected patients without being harmed, and even walk among dozens of sick people without fear. They could use their qi to drive away malevolent spirits and mountain demons, repel venomous creatures, and even prevent blades from cutting flesh. Some could make water flow upstream, prevent thatch-roofed houses from catching fire, eject nails from wooden pillars, cool boiling water so that hands remained unharmed, or freeze water solid even in bitter cold.

These demonstrations serve as evidence that the human body, when properly cultivated, can transcend ordinary limitations. The text connects *xingqi* to earlier figures like Zhang Liang, who practiced it alongside fasting and dietary restrictions, ultimately achieving supernatural powers.

## 中文

**行氣**為抱朴子所述之修仙要術。其書云：「服藥雖為長生之本，若能兼行氣者，其益甚速，若不能得藥，但行氣而盡其理者，亦得數百歲。」又論及房中之術與行氣之關係：「不知陰陽之術，屢為勞損，則行氣難得力也。」

行氣之妙用，載於書中者甚詳。善行氣者，內以養身，外以卻惡。其效驗包括：
- **防疫避病**：能入大疫之中，與病人同床而不染。
- **禁制鬼神**：以炁禁之，皆即絕。
- **辟卻毒蟲**：入山林溪毒蝮蛇之地，以炁禁之，能辟方數十里。
- **蹈刃不傷**：以炁禁白刃，可蹈之不傷，刺之不入。
- **水火之變**：禁水使逆流，禁火使不燃。

吳遣賀將軍征山賊，賊中有善禁者，官軍刀劍皆不得拔。賀將軍悟及「金有刃者可禁，蟲有毒者可禁，其無刃之物，無毒之蟲，則不可禁」，乃作白棒以擊之，大破賊眾。此例見行氣禁術之局限，亦見其效用之真實。

仲長公理論行氣之效，雲「始者未之信，至於為之者，盡乃然矣」，皆謂修此術者確能不饑不病。

## 日本語

**行氣**（気功）は、抱朴子が詳述する道教修煉法の一つ。『抱朴子』によれば、仙薬配合得更生又能令不死者復活，此乃由上药之力所致。

扁鵲救虢太子、胡醫活蘇武、淳于擅腦外科、華佗行剖腹洗胃、文摯延期治病、仲景穿胸納赤餅，皆尋常醫技，猶能起死回生，況神仙之道，何所不為？
