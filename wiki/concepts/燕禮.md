## English

The Banquet Ritual (燕禮, Yàn Lǐ) constitutes one of the most significant ceremonial occasions in the classical Chinese ritual system, documented primarily in the *Book of Rites* (禮記) and the *Rites of Zhou* (周禮). This ritual was performed after formal diplomatic exchanges between feudal lords, or during celebrations of victories, to entertain officials and demonstrate the harmonious relationship between ruler and ministers.

The *Yàn Yì* (燕義, "The Meaning of the Banquet") explains that the fundamental purpose of the banquet ritual lies in **illuminating the righteousness between ruler and minister** (明君臣之義). According to this text, when the ruler responds to his ministers' expressions of loyalty with appropriate rewards and recognition, the realm achieves tranquility and the sovereign maintains peace. The ritual embodies a reciprocal relationship wherein ministers devote their utmost abilities to serve the state, and rulers reciprocate with noble ranks and stipends. This mutual exchange forms the essential bond that sustains proper governance.

The spatial arrangement of the banquet hall reveals the hierarchical structure of the political order. The ruler occupies a special position on the **eastern stairs** (阼階) of the main hall, symbolizing his unique authority as the pivot of the ritual space. Unlike his ministers who stand in orderly ranks, the ruler stands alone, facing west—a posture that expresses both his solitary dignity and his readiness to receive submissions from those facing east. This solitary standing embodies the principle that "none dares to compete" (莫敢適) with the sovereign's position.

The ritual proceeds through systematic rounds of formal drinking (旅酬), each round moving from ruler to high ministers (卿), then to senior officials (大夫), then to junior officials (士), and finally to the sons of officials (庶子). This graduated progression represents the transmission of grace downward through the hierarchy, while the reciprocal bows and acknowledgments ensure that each level receives proper recognition.

Crucially, the banquet ritual distinguishes between the roles of **minister** and **guest**. The steward (宰夫) serves as the "offering host" (獻主), not the ruler's ministers, for "no minister dares to be on equal terms with the ruler" (臣莫�敢與君亢禮). Furthermore, high officials (公卿) do not serve as formal guests to avoid suggesting inappropriate equality with the sovereign. Instead,大夫 (senior officials) are received as guests, thereby maintaining the proper graduated distinctions.

The final section of the ritual emphasizes material expressions of hierarchy through the arrangement of **ritual vessels, sacrificial animals, and offerings** (俎豆牲體荐羞). Each rank receives vessels and portions appropriate to their station, making visible the distinctions of noble and base that structure the political community. This material differentiation, far from being mere ceremony, constitutes the visible manifestation of the moral-political order.

The *Yàn Yì* thus presents the banquet not as mere conviviality but as a ritual enactment of the fundamental relationships that constitute political society. Through the precise ordering of space, the graduated transmission of honor, and the material differentiation of rank, the banquet ritual demonstrates and reinforces the principles of ruler-minister righteousness that sustain good governance.

## 中文

《燕義》為《禮記》之篇，闡釋諸侯朝聘後與群臣宴飲之禮要義。其開篇先述庶子官之職，繼而詳論燕禮之義，以為「燕禮者，所以明君臣之義也」。

燕禮之設，非僅娛賓遣興，乃所以別貴賤、明尊卑、序朝廷。蓋君立於阼階之上，獨升席位，西面而立，莫敢有與君抗禮者。宰夫為獻主，臣無敢與君亢禮。公卿不為賓，而以大夫為賓者，為疑也，明嫌之義也。

禮之要義，在於上下相報：臣竭力盡能以立功於國，君必報之以爵祿；上明正道以道民，民道之而有功，然後取其什一。如此，則上下和親而不相怨，和寧之義備矣。故曰：「燕禮者，所以明君臣之義也。」

其旅酬之序，自君而卿、而大夫、而士、而庶子，等級分明。俎豆牲體荐羞皆有差等，所以明貴賤也。此禮行則君臣之分正，朝廷之序定，治國之道彰矣。

## 日本語

燕禮（宴礼）は『礼記』中所収の重要な儀礼篇章であり、諸侯の朝聘後の群臣との宴席における礼の意味を解説する。『燕義』一篇は、燕禮の根本的意義が「君臣の義を明らかにする」ことにあると明言する。

燕禮の空間配置は階層秩序を象徴する。君主は阼階（祖階の東側）に立ち、席に独り昇り、西方を向いて立つ。これは君主的威厳の象徴であり、群臣が君に抗することを禁じる原則を表現する。

重要な点は、宰夫が「献主」（供物の主催者）として振る舞い、臣下が君主と対等の礼を取らないことである。公卿は賓客とせず、問題を回避し疑念を避けるため大夫を賓客とする。

旅酬の順序は君主から卿、大夫、士、庶子へと段階的に進められ、位の序列を示す。俎豆・牲体・荐羞の配置にも厳密な等級差があり、貴賤の分別を可視化する。燕禮はこのように、宴飲を通じて君臣関係の正しいあり方を示す政治的根本儀礼である。
