  The Banquet Ritual (燕禮, Yàn Lǐ) constitutes one of the most significant ceremonial
  occasions in the classical Chinese ritual system, documented primarily in the *Book
  of Rites* (禮記) and the *Rites of Zhou* (周禮). This ritual was performed after formal
  diplomatic exchanges between feudal lords, or during celebrations of victories,
  to entertain officials and demonstrate the harmonious relationship between ruler
  and ministers.


  The *Yàn Yì* (燕義, "The Meaning of the Banquet") explains that the fundamental purpose
  of the banquet ritual lies in **illuminating the righteousness between ruler and
  minister** (明君臣之義). According to this text, when the ruler responds to his ministers''
  expressions of loyalty with appropriate rewards and recognition, the realm achieves
  tranquility and the sovereign maintains peace. The ritual embodies a reciprocal
  relationship wherein ministers devote their utmost abilities to serve the state,
  and rulers reciprocate with noble ranks and stipends. This mutual exchange forms
  the essential bond that sustains proper governance.


  The spatial arrangement of the banquet hall reveals the hierarchical structure of
  the political order. The ruler occupies a special position on the **eastern stairs**
  (阼階) of the main hall, symbolizing his unique authority as the pivot of the ritual
  space. Unlike his ministers who stand in orderly ranks, the ruler stands alone,
  facing west—a posture that expresses both his solitary dignity and his readiness
  to receive submissions from those facing east. This solitary standing embodies the
  principle that "none dares to compete" (莫敢適) with the sovereign''s position.


  The ritual proceeds through systematic rounds of formal drinking (旅酬), each round
  moving from ruler to high ministers (卿), then to senior officials (大夫), then to
  junior officials (士), and finally to the sons of officials (庶子). This graduated
  progression represents the transmission of grace downward through the hierarchy,
  while the reciprocal bows and acknowledgments ensure that each level receives proper
  recognition.


  Crucially, the banquet ritual distinguishes between the roles of **minister** and
  **guest**. The steward (宰夫) serves as the "offering host" (獻主), not the ruler''s
  ministers, for "no minister dares to be on equal terms with the ruler" (臣莫�敢與君亢禮).
  Furthermore, high officials (公卿) do not serve as formal guests to avoid suggesting
  inappropriate equality with the sovereign. Instead,大夫 (senior officials) are received
  as guests, thereby maintaining the proper graduated distinctions.


  The final section of the ritual emphasizes material expressions of hierarchy through
  the arrangement of **ritual vessels, sacrificial animals, and offerings** (俎豆牲體荐羞).
  Each rank receives vessels and portions appropriate to their station, making visible
  the distinctions of noble and base that structure the political community. This
  material differentiation, far from being mere ceremony, constitutes the visible
  manifestation of the moral-political order.


  The *Yàn Yì* thus presents the banquet not as mere conviviality but as a ritual
  enactment of the fundamental relationships that constitute political society. Through
  the precise ordering of space, the graduated transmission of honor, and the material
  differentiation of rank, the banquet ritual demonstrates and reinforces the principles
  of ruler-minister righteousness that sustain good governance.



  ## 日本語


  燕禮（宴礼）は『礼記』中所収の重要な儀礼篇章であり、諸侯の朝聘後の群臣との宴席における礼の意味を解説する。『燕義』一篇は、燕禮の根本的意義が「君臣の義を明らかにする」ことにあると明言する。


  燕禮の空間配置は階層秩序を象徴する。君主は阼階（祖階の東側）に立ち、席に独り昇り、西方を向いて立つ。これは君主的威厳の象徴であり、群臣が君に抗することを禁じる原則を表現する。


  重要な点は、宰夫が「献主」（供物の主催者）として振る舞い、臣下が君主と対等の礼を取らないことである。公卿は賓客とせず、問題を回避し疑念を避けるため大夫を賓客とする。


  旅酬の順序は君主から卿、大夫、士、庶子へと段階的に進められ、位の序列を示す。俎豆・牲体・荐羞の配置にも厳密な等級差があり、貴賤の分別を可視化する。燕禮はこのように、宴飲を通じて君臣関係の正しいあり方を示す政治的根本儀礼である。

燕禮者，天子、諸侯與臣下燕飲之禮也。[[昏禮]]之終[[鄉飲酒]]之別燕。[[士冠禮]]有[[士相見禮]]之屬，[[儀禮]]所存十七篇，此篇第十六。鄭玄云：「燕，息也。序長幼、養老命燕，某適四方，聘問餙賔，[[聘禮]]之儀。」

燕義之要，在於「安」與「酬」二字。安者，[[君臣]]之情也；酬者，[[爵]][[觶]]之儀也。公尊[[瓦大]]兩，有[[冪]]，[[玄酒]]南上，[[膳篚]]在北，[[司宮]]兼卷重席。[[射人]]告具，[[小臣]]納[[卿大夫]]，[[祝史]]立門東，[[士旅食者]]立門西。公[[阼階]]升，即席西鄉。

賓出，射人請立司正。司正洗角觶，南面坐奠于中庭，升受命。命卿大夫：「君曰以我安！」卿大夫皆對曰：「諾！敢不安？」司正降自西階，取觶升降，[[無算爵]]之節始此。

無算爵者，[[士]]有[[執膳爵]]者，有執散爵者。[[公]]坐取[[大夫]]所媵觶，[[媵爵者]]洗象觶，升實之，坐奠于[[荐]]南。公行一爵若[[賓]]若長，唯公所酬，以旅于西階。卒受者以虛觶奠于篚。

若射，則[[大射正]]為[[司射]]，如[[鄉射]]之禮。[[君]]與射，則為下射，[[袒朱襦]]，[[小臣]]以巾授矢，稍屬。不以樂志。既發，則小臣受弓以授弓人。

宵，則[[庶子]]執燭[[阼階]]上，[[司宮]]執燭[[西階]]上，[[甸人]]執大燭于庭，[[闉人]]為大燭於門外。[[賓]]醉，北面坐取其荐[[脯]]以降。奏《[[陔]]》。[[公]]不送。

記云：燕，朝服，於寢。其牲狗也，亨于門外東方。若與四方之賓燕，則公迎之于大門內，揖讓升。賓為[[苟敬]]，席于[[阼階]]之西，北面，有臠，不嚌肺，不啐酒。其介為賓。無膳尊，無膳爵。與卿燕，則大夫為賓。羞膳者與執冪者，皆士也。羞卿者，[[小膳宰]]也。

有[[房中之樂]]。
