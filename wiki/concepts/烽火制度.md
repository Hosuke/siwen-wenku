## English

The beacon fire system described in "Mozi · Miscellaneous Defenses" (雜守) constitutes a comprehensive day-and-night signal protocol for city wall defense. The system establishes graduated alert levels based on the proximity and nature of enemy incursion.

The signal hierarchy operates as follows:

**Daytime Signals (based on torch count):**
- Single beacon: enemy forces sighted in the distance
- Two beacons: enemy has entered the territory
- Three beacons plus drum: enemy forces engaged in archery combat
- Four beacons plus two drums: outer city garrison engaged
- Five beacons plus five drums: inner city under direct attack

**Nighttime Signals (based on fire patterns):**
The same numerical progression applies, with fire取代 torches as the primary medium. Each level corresponds to increasing proximity and severity of the siege threat.

The text specifies that signals should progress systematically from the perimeter to the capital city (至主國止), with the fastest courier relays maintaining continuous communication. In emergencies, signals may be raised or lowered as circumstances demand.

The beacon keepers (守烽者) must remain stationed at their posts throughout the siege, prepared to emit subtle signals at a moment's notice. The system requires coordination between multiple watchtowers and signals must not be discontinued until the danger passes (烽勿罷).

This hierarchical warning system enabled rapid mobilization of defenders while conserving resources during false alarms. The graduated response prevented both over-reaction and delayed preparation.

## 中文

《墨子·雜守》所載烽火制度，乃被圍之城晝夜傳警之法。其要義在據敵寇遠近，分級燃火，以達調兵禦敵之效。

制度之詳情如下：

**晝間信號：**
- 望見寇寇蹤，舉一烽
- 寇已入境，舉二烽
- 敵射妻（接戰），舉三烽並一鼓
- 郭（即外城）有事，舉四烽二鼓
- 城（即內城）被圍，舉五烽五鼓

**夜間信號：**
- 以火代烽，數目與晝間相應

文中又載：「烽火以舉，輒五鼓傳，又以火屬之，言寇所從來者少多，旦弇還。去來屬次，烽勿罷。」蓋言烽火一舉，五鼓相傳，更以火光續之，報告敵寇多寡、天明乃止，且往來相繼，不可間斷。

守烽者「事急，日暮出之，令皆爲微職」，皆需隱蔽待命，機變迅速。此制與 [[墨子旗幟制度]] 相須為用，共成城守預警之完密體系。

## 日本語

『墨子・雑守』に記された烽火制度は、包囲都市の昼夜両用の警報体系である。敵の接近度と攻撃の深刻度に応じて段階的に烽火を焚き、城内へ警急を伝達する仕組みを完备している。

昼間の信号は次の五段階による：
- 一烽：敵発見
- 二烽：敵入境
- 三烽＋一鼓：戦闘中
- 四烽＋二鼓：外城の戦い
- 五烽＋五鼓：内城包囲

夜間は火を以って烽火に代人り、同一の段階制を適用する。伝令は五鼓を伝えてから火光で敵の人数・動静を報告し、夜明けまで継続する。

守烽者は常に最前線に待機し、微かな信号も発し得る態勢を維持する。この体系は [[墨子旗幟制度]] と相まって、完璧な都市防御警報システムを形成する。
