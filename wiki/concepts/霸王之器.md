  centered on four cardinal virtues as the indispensable \"vessels\" (器) for rulers
  who would achieve hegemonic or kingly rule. The text states: **\"義與信、和與仁，霸王之器也。有治民之意而無其器，則不成。\"**
  These four—righteousness (義), trust (信), harmony (和), and benevolence (仁)—are not
  merely moral qualities but functional instruments of political order.\n\nThe text
  defines each: a ruler who issues commands and the people respond with joy practices
  *harmony* (和); when those above and those below share mutual affection, this is
  *benevolence* (仁); when the people obtain what they desire without having to seek
  it, this is *trust* (信); when the ruler removes the harms of heaven and earth, this
  is *righteousness* (義). \n\nThis hierarchical structure—from harmony through benevolence,
  trust, to righteousness—represents ascending levels of political achievement, with
  *hegemony* (霸) requiring the first three and *true kingship* (王) requiring all four.

《禮記·經解》提出治國之要道，以義、信、和、仁四德為霸王之器。孔子明言：「義與信、和與仁，霸王之器也。有治民之意而無其器，則不成。」此四者非僅道德品質，乃治國之實用器具，無之則意圖雖善，終不能成治。

其義各有分疏：發號出令而民悅，謂之和；上下相親，謂之仁；民不求所欲而得之，謂之信；除去天地之害，謂之義。四者之間，和為治之淺者，仁次之，信又次之，義為最深。霸者得其一二，王者兼備四者。此說揭示儒家政治哲學之本旨：治道在乎器用，器用備則王道可成，器用闕則雖有愛民之心，終歸無功。
