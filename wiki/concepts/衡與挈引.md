content_legacy: '## English


  The mechanical passages in "Canon and Explaining the Canon" examine the principles
  of balance (衡) and mechanical advantage through levers and pulley systems (挈與引).
  Mozi''s analysis distinguishes between lifting (挈) and pulling (引) as different
  mechanical operations with distinct force requirements.


  The balance scale (衡木) operates on the principle that equilibrium is maintained
  when equal weights are placed at equal distances from the fulcrum. When weight is
  added to one side, that side descends unless counterbalanced. The text analyzes
  situations where the "root" (本, closer to fulcrum) and "tip" (標, farther from fulcrum)
  have different lengths, explaining how leverage multiplies force.


  For lifting mechanisms, the analysis describes how a rope system can raise heavy
  objects: pulling upward requires less force than the object''s weight due to mechanical
  advantage. The text examines the condition where "the rope is vertical and weight
  is at the end" — when the pulling force and weight align optimally, the system is
  in equilibrium and motion can occur with minimal additional force.

《墨子·經說下》論衡與挈引之理，闡述槓桿、滑車之機械原理。衡木如重焉而不撓，極勝重也。衡之要在於「權重相若」，本短標長則標下，此機械利益之要義也。

挈與引有別：挈有力，引無力。挈長重者下，短輕者上。繩直權重相若，則正矣。收者，上者愈喪，下者愈得。此論述滑車組之原理，繩之引軲，猶自舟中引橫，倚、倍、拒、堅，各有其用。

更有車梯之制：兩輪高為輲，重其前弦，縣重於前，是梯挈且挈則行。此乃早期機械裝置之記載，見墨家重實用之學風。
