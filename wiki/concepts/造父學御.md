## English

"Zaofu Learns Driving" is a parable from the Tang Wen chapter of the Liezi, illustrating how mastery of any skill requires concentration, proper posture, and harmonious coordination between the self and external instruments.

The story opens with the master craftsman Taidou teaching Zaofu the art of chariot driving. Taidou demanded that Zaofu observe his movements with extreme humility before teaching any technique. Only after three years of respectful observation did Taidou reveal his method: "Observe my movements. Once you understand them as I do, then you may hold the six reins and control the six horses."

Taidou then set up a wooden track barely wide enough for one foot, marking positions along it. He demonstrated walking back and forth without stumbling or falling. Zaofu mastered this in three days. Impressed, Taidou explained the deeper principles: "What you achieved through your feet, you must now apply through your heart. For driving, achieve harmony in the reins and bit, coordinate the speed with the lips' utterance, maintain correct posture in the chest and belly, and hold the rhythm with the palms."

The key insight is that true mastery arises from internal-external coordination: "Internally, achieve it in the center of your heart; externally, harmonize with the horses' intent." This allows the driver to "advance and retreat following the line, spin and turn meeting the ruler's curves, travel far while retaining strength—indeed, the technique is obtained."

The culmination is effortless mastery: "Grasp it from the bit, respond through the reins; grasp it from the reins, respond through the hands; grasp it from the hands, respond through the heart. Then without using the eyes, without using the whip, with a tranquil heart and proper body, the six reins remain unconfused, the twenty-four hooves land without error."

This parable illustrates the Confucian principle that all skills—from archery to governance—require devoted practice and the unity of mind, body, and instrument. [[梓匠規矩]] addresses similar themes of craft mastery.

## 中文

造父之師曰泰豆氏，此《湯問篇》所載者也。造父始從習御，執禮甚卑，泰豆三年不告。造父執禮愈謹，乃告之曰：「古詩言：『良弓之子，必先為箕；良冶之子，必先為裘。』汝先觀吾趣。」

泰豆乃立木為塗，僅可容足；計步而置，履之而行。趣走往還，無跌失也。造父學之，三日盡其巧。

泰豆歎曰：「子何其敏也？凡所御者，亦如此也。曩汝之行，得之於足，應之於心。推於御也，齊輯乎轡銜之際，而急緩乎脣吻之和，正度乎胷臆之中，而執節乎掌握之間。」

其要義在於內外相合：「內得於中心，而外合於馬志，是故能進退履繩而旋曲中規矩，取道致遠而氣力有餘。」

終而至於無為之境：「得之於銜，應之於轡；得之於轡，應之於手；得之於手，應之於心，則不以目視，不以策驅；心閑體正，六轡不亂，而二十四蹄所投無差。」

此篇所載，與[[善學者師逸功倍]]相通，皆論技藝之道在於專心致志、內外兼修。造父之術，可通於治國之道，以心御馬，以心御民，其理一也。

## 日本語

造父が御を嗜む故事は『列子』湯問篇に収められている。造父の師匠泰豆性は、三年間も礼を尽くすことを求め、漸くその教えを告げた。

泰豆性は木で足幅ほどの道を作り、歩調を合わせて歩かせた。造父は三日でこれを会得，泰豆性は歎じた：「子の敏にして捷なること、甚だ佳い。凡そ御する者も亦此の如し。曩には足を得て之に應じ、心に應じた。今やこれを御に推すには轡銜の合いに齊輯し、脣吻の和に急緩し、胸臆の中に正度を保ち、掌握の間に節を執る」

その要は内外の合一にある：「内に於いて中心得其，外に於いて馬志に合えば、是以て進退は繩に履み、旋曲は规矩に中る」

最高境地は「心глазを要せず、策を馭せず、心閑かに體正しく、六轡乱れず、二十四蹄投ずる所差なし」となる。

此の寓話は[[梓匠規矩]]と共に、技芸精通が專心致志と内外兼修を要することを説く。

造父以善御幸於周繆王，得驥、溫驪、驊騮、騄耳之駟，西巡狩，樂而忘歸。徐偃王作亂，造父為繆王御，長驅歸周，一日千里以救亂。繆王以趙城封造父，造父族由此為趙氏。
