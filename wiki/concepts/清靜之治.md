content_legacy: '## English


  **清靜之治** (Qīngjìng zhī Zhì, "Quietist Governance") is a core concept from *Wenzi*,
  Book 9, describing the highest form of governance through the ruler''s inner purity
  and stillness. Unlike Confucian ritual-based governance or Legalist law-based control,
  this Daoist approach holds that when the ruler achieves complete mental tranquility
  and freedom from personal desires, the entire realm naturally falls into harmony
  without conscious effort.


  The text outlines several essential qualities of this governance: the ruler who
  embodies 清靜之治 maintains "虛無寂寞，不見可欲" (emptiness and stillness, never encountering
  desirable things), achieving a state where "心與神處，形與性調" (the heart dwells with the
  spirit, and the form harmonizes with nature). In stillness they embody virtue; in
  motion they follow pattern; they walk the natural path and act according to necessity.


  The practical effects are profound: "漠然無為而天下和，淡然無欲而民自樸" — with impartial non-action,
  the world is harmonious; with detached desirelessness, the people are natural. Since
  there is no contention, resources are abundant; those who seek gain nothing, and
  those who receive do not refuse. Virtue returns to its source effortlessly.


  This concept connects to the related ideas of [[天府]] (the Heavenly Storehouse) and
  [[搖光]] (Rotating Light), which describe the inexhaustible nature of the Way that
  sustains all things. The ruler who practices 清靜之治 draws from these sources without
  depleting them, achieving "不言之道" — the Way that needs no words.'

**清靜之治**者，《文子·卷九》所載道家治國之上乘也。其要在上位者虛無寂寞，內心澄澈，無所誘欲，則天下自然歸化焉。

夫清靜之治，非以法度約束萬民，乃返求諸己，修養心神，使心與神居，形與性調。靜而體德，動而理通，循自然之道，緣不得已而為。是故漠然無為而天下和，淡然無欲而民自樸。不忿爭而財足，求者不得，受者不讓，德反歸焉，而莫之惠焉。

此治道之所以為上也。視之不見名曰夷，聽之不聞名曰希，搏之不得名曰微。此三者不可致詰，故混而為一。上德不德，是以有德；下德不失德，是以無德。清靜者，道之別名也；無為者，德之具象也。聖人體道，故能不言而善應，不召而自來，繹然而坦蕩，歸根而復命。

[[天府]]者，不言之辨、不道之道也。[[搖光]]者，資糧萬物者也。清靜之治，通於此二者，而達於無為之境

**《太上老君說常清靜經》**論清靜之義尤詳：

  人神好清，而心擾之；人心好靜，而慾牽之。常能遣其慾而心自靜，澄其心而神自清。自然六慾不生，三毒消滅。

  清者濁之源，動者靜之基。人能常清靜，天地悉皆歸。

  慾既不生，即是真靜。真常應物，真常得性；常應常靜，常清靜矣。如此清靜，漸入真道；既入真道，名謂得道。

  真常之道，悟者自得。得悟道者，常清靜矣。

  又清靜者，修煉之宗極，[[與道合真]]之階梯也。
