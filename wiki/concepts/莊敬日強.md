content_legacy: '## English


  The concept of **莊敬日強** (Dignified Respect Strengthens Daily) appears in the 表記
  chapter of the Book of Rites as a fundamental principle of self-cultivation. Confucius
  states: "君子莊敬日強，安肆日偷。君子不以一日使其躬儳焉，如不終日。" — "The superior person is dignified and
  respectful, and thus grows stronger each day; the one who is indulgent and careless
  becomes daily more degenerate. The superior person never allows himself for even
  a single day to be in a state of disorder, as if he cannot last through the day."


  This passage establishes a direct correlation between external conduct and internal
  moral development. "莊敬" (dignified respect) refers to a state of solemn reverence
  in one''s bearing, speech, and actions—a quality that paradoxically generates strength
  rather than constraining it. The counterpart "安肆" (complacent indulgence) represents
  the laxity that comes from abandoning ritual propriety, which leads to moral "偷"
  (degeneracy/lightness).


  The principle extends beyond personal cultivation to political implications. Earlier
  in the same chapter, Confucius advises that one should "慎以辟禍，篤以不掩，恭以遠恥" — "be cautious
  to avoid disaster, be sincere to avoid concealment, be respectful to keep distant
  from shame." The connection between 恭 (respect) and 遠恥 (distance from disgrace)
  demonstrates how outward ritual behavior protects both personal reputation and moral
  integrity.


  The doctrine also connects to the broader 禮記 teaching about ritual as a transformative
  practice. Just as [[禮樂不可斯須去身]] emphasizes that ritual and music must never be absent
  even for a moment, 莊敬日強 insists on continuous self-monitoring through ceremonial
  mindfulness.

「莊敬日強」出自《禮記·表記》，為孔子所言修身之要義。其文曰：「君子莊敬日強，安肆日偷。君子不以一日使其躬儳焉，如不終日。」

此章之義，在明恭敬與放肆對人心性之不同影響。「莊敬」者，容貌端肅、處事謹慎之謂也；「日強」者，德行日益增進之謂也。君子以莊敬存心，故能日日長進。反之，「安肆」者，安於放縱、肆無忌憚之謂；「日偷」者，德行日漸衰薄之謂也。

「不以一日使其躬儳焉」之語，尤見警切。「儳」者，雜亂不整之貌。君子修身，不敢有一日之懈怠；若使形骸有一刻之放逸，則終日惶惶，如不能保其終日。此乃以一日之放肆為莫大之恥，非待祸患之來而後懼也。

又觀前文所言「慎以辟禍，篤以不掩，恭以遠恥」，可知恭之一字，乃遠恥之方。恭敬非徒為外飾，乃內外交養之道。外有莊敬之容，內有堅強之德，表裏相須，缺一不可。此與《論語》所謂「君子不重則不威」之義相通，皆言威儀與德行之關係。
