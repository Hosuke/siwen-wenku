content_legacy: '## English


  **三仙說** — the theory of the three immortals — is Ge Hong''s systematic classification
  of those who achieve transcendence. According to 仙經 cited in *Baopuzi*:


  1. **天仙** (Celestial Immortals): 上士舉形昇虛，謂之天仙 — The superior person who raises the
  physical form into the void is called a Celestial Immortal. This represents the
  highest achievement: bodily ascension into the heavens while still alive.


  2. **地仙** (Earth Immortals): 中士游於名山，謂之地仙 — The middling person who wanders among
  the famous mountains is called an Earth Immortal. These immortals have achieved
  longevity but remain bound to the earth, unable to ascend bodily.


  3. **尸解仙** (Corpse-Releasing Immortals): 下士先死後蛻，謂之屍解仙 — The lower person who first
  dies, then sheds [the body], is called a Corpse-Releasing Immortal. This category
  includes those who appear to die (sometimes leaving only clothing in the coffin),
  then continue their existence elsewhere.


  The text provides historical examples of 尸解仙:

  - **李少君** (Li Shaojun): Appeared ill, "died," but when Emperor Wu had his coffin
  opened, only his garments were inside.

  - **壺公** (Hugong) and **費長房** (Fei Changfang): Hugong took Fei Changfang away; when
  both later "died," their coffins contained only a bamboo staff inscribed with cinnabar
  characters.

  - **李意期** (Liyi Qi) and his two disciples: Appeared to die, but years later were
  seen alive in Qixian, and their coffins contained only bamboo staves.


  This threefold classification allowed Ge Hong to account for various accounts of
  immortals without requiring that all of them achieve the highest form of bodily
  ascension. Many historical "deaths" of famous practitioners could be explained as
  尸解 — the shedding of a temporary physical form.


  See also: [[神仙不死]], [[學仙之法]], [[真人]], [[至人]]



  ## 日本語


  **三仙說**とは、葛洪の神仙分類体系である。『抱朴子』が引く仙経によれば、仙は三等ある：


  一、**天仙**：上士は形体共に虚空に昇る。これを天仙と謂う。最高の成就である。


  二、**地仙**：中士は名山を巡る。これを地仙と謂う。長生は得たれども、地上に留まる。


  三、**尸解仙**：下士はまず死に、しかる後に躯を脱ぐ。これを尸解仙と謂う。


  葛洪は尸解の説をもって、古代の仙人のさまざまな「死」を解釈する。李少君の死後に棺に衣冠のみがあったこと、壺公と費長房が死んだかに見えながら数年後に長房が帰ってきたことなどは、すべて尸解として説明される。

**三仙說**者，葛洪對神仙之分級也。據仙經所雲，仙有三等：

一、**天仙**：上士舉形昇虛，謂之天仙。上智之人，形體飛昇，入於虛無，此為神仙之上乘。

二、**地仙**：中士游於名山，謂之地仙。中材之人，長生不死，然不能飛昇，僅漫游名山而已。

三、**尸解仙**：下士先死後蛻，謂之尸解仙。下愚之人，先假死以脫形，而後蛻去故躯，別更生存。

葛洪以尸解之說，釋古來仙人種種「死亡」之跡：
- 李少君病死，棺中唯衣冠在焉。
- 壺公將費長房去，相繼死，殯埋數年，而長房來歸，見李意期將兩弟子皆在郫縣。發棺視之，三棺有竹杖一枚，以丹書之。

如此，則凡載籍所傳「仙去」之跡，皆可得解。天仙則舉形昇虛，地仙則游於名山，尸解仙則蛻形而去，其實一也。
