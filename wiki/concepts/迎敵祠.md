## English

The "Welcoming Enemy Ritual" (迎敵祠) is a defensive ritual ceremony described in the Mozi chapter "Ying Di Ci," designed to fortify a besieged city against incoming enemy forces. Unlike aggressive military operations, this ritual represents a form of spiritual and psychological preparation for defense.

The ceremony is structured according to the cardinal direction from which the enemy approaches:

**Eastern Attack**: An eastern altar (八尺 high) is constructed, staffed by eight men aged eighty. They bear blue banners, with eight blue deities (each eight chi tall). Eight crossbows are prepared, firing eight volleys. The commander's attire is blue, and the sacrificial victim is a chicken.

**Southern Attack**: A seven chi altar, seven men aged seventy, red banners, seven red deities (seven chi tall), seven crossbows firing seven volleys, red attire, dog sacrifice.

**Western Attack**: A nine chi altar, nine men aged ninety, white banners, nine white deities (nine chi tall), nine crossbows firing nine volleys, white attire, sheep sacrifice.

**Northern Attack**: A six chi altar, six men aged sixty, black banners, six black deities (six chi tall), six crossbows firing six volleys, black attire, pig sacrifice.

The numerical correspondences—altar height, age, deity height, crossbow volleys, and cardinal directions—demonstrate the Mozi school's emphasis on systematic organization and proportional relationships in defensive planning. The choice of sacrificial animals follows traditional auspicious associations: chickens for sunrise (east), dogs for southern heat, sheep for western dryness, and pigs for northern cold.

Beyond the altar ceremony, shamans (巫) are dispatched to major external shrines to offer prayers and sacrifices. This integration of ritual and defense reflects Mozi's broader philosophy that spiritual forces, properly invoked, can assist in protecting the state.

## 中文

迎敵祠者，墨子所述之城防祭儀也。敵人來犯之時，必先備祭以禦之。其法以敵來之方位為準，分設四壇：

東來則祀青帝。壇高八尺，堂密八，選年八十者八人主祭，青旗，神長八尺者八，弩八，八發而止，將服必青，牲以雞。蓋日出於東，雞鳴應之，故用焉。

南來則祀赤帝。壇高七尺，堂密七，選年七十者七人主祭，赤旗，神長七尺者七，弩七，七發而止，將服必赤，牲以狗。南方屬火，狗陽獸也，故用之。

西來則祀白帝。壇高九尺，堂密九，選年九十者九人主祭，白旗，神長九尺者九，弩九，九發而止，將服必白，牲以羊。西方肅殺，羊性溫和，以柔克剛之意。

北來則祀黑帝。壇高六尺，堂密六，選年六十者六人主祭，黑旗，神長六尺者六，弩六，六發而止，將服必黑，牲以彘。北方屬水，彘性濡潤，故用焉。

又令巫祝遍祠外宅諸大祠，給禱牲焉。凡望氣之術，有大將氣、小將氣、往氣、來氣、敗氣，能明此者，可知成敗吉凶。此祭儀與望氣之術相表裏，共為城防之要。

## 日本語

迎敵祠とは、『墨子』「迎敵祠」篇に記される城防祭祀の儀式である。敵が四方の方角から攻撃してくる際に、それに応じて適切な祭祀を行い、城市を守るための儀礼である。

東方より来たる時は青色の方策により、北方は黑色、南方は赤色、西方は白色と、それぞれの方角に対応した色の旗、祭神人数、弩の発射回数、指揮官の服装、犠牲となる動物が定められている。これらの数値的対応は墨家の組織的・系統的な思考を示している。

城外の大祠においても巫祝が祈祷と犠牲を捧げ、望気術によって敵軍の大将気・小将気・往気・来気・敗気を観察し、勝敗予見に活用する。この儀式は祭祀と軍事防御、精神的心地を融合させた墨家の実践的思索の結晶である。
