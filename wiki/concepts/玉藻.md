content_legacy: '## English


  **玉藻** (Yùzǎo, "Jade Ornament") refers to the jade decorations affixed to the front
  of the Son of Heaven''s ceremonial crown (冕, miǎn), specifically the hanging tassels
  (旒, liú) that frame the face. This term appears in the title and opening passage
  of the *Book of Rites* chapter "Jade Ornaments" (玉藻), which establishes the foundational
  regulations governing court dress, ritual procedures, and ceremonial conduct for
  rulers and officials.


  The *玉藻* describes the Son of Heaven wearing "玉藻十有二旒" (jade ornaments with twelve
  tassels), representing the twelve months of the year. Each tassel is made of colored珠子
  (pearls or beads), hanging down to obscure the ruler''s features during sacrifice,
  thereby emphasizing the solemnity and impersonality of the ritual role rather than
  the individual person. The text states: "天子玉藻，十有二旒，前後邃延，龍卷以祭" — the ruler wears
  jade ornaments of twelve tassels, hanging long before and behind, with dragon-embroidery
  robes for sacrifice.


  This passage establishes the principle that **ritual dress manifests cosmic order**.
  The number twelve corresponds to lunar months, and the obscuring of the ruler''s
  face signifies that the monarch acts as an instrument of heaven rather than as a
  private individual. The jade ornament thus functions as both aesthetic adornment
  and metaphysical symbol.


  The chapter proceeds to regulate the Son of Heaven''s daily rituals: observing the
  sun at the eastern gate, hearing political affairs at the southern gate, and during
  leap months, standing with the left door closed. The wearing of jade ornaments marks
  the highest levels of ritual activity, while more mundane affairs are conducted
  in simpler dress.

《禮記·玉藻》為《禮記》第十九篇，詳述天子、諸侯、卿大夫、士各級貴族之服飾制度與朝儀規制，乃古代中國禮制之重要文獻。篇名「玉藻」取首句「天子玉藻」之意，蓋玉為禮之象徵，藻為水草之屬，取其文飾之義。

開篇即定天子祭服之制：「天子玉藻，十有二旒，前後邃延，龍卷以祭。」言天子祭天地祖先之時，垂十二旒之冕，旒飾以玉，前後深邃延展，身服龍卷之圖。此制所以象天數、明天命也。

其後所記規制甚繁，要而言之：

**朝祭之服**：玄端以朝日，聽朔于南門之外；皮弁以視朝，日中而餕；朔月大牢，平日少牢。

**史官之職**：動則左史書之，言則右史書之，御瞽幾聲之上下。此言史官記事之責，樂師審音之任。

**庖廚之禮**：君無故不殺牛，大夫無故不殺羊，士無故不殺犬豕。君子遠庖廚，凡有血氣之類，弗身踐也。此制所以養仁心、崇德教也。

**儀容之度**：行容惕惕，廟中齊齊，朝廷濟濟翔翔。足容重，手容恭，目容端，口容止，聲容靜，頭容直，氣容肅，立容德，色容莊。

**佩玉之節**：「君子必佩玉，右徵角，左宮羽。」趨以《采齊》，行以《肆夏》，玉鏘鳴焉。此以聲音諧和喻德行中正，故君子在車則聞鸞和之聲，行則鳴佩玉，是以非辟之心無自入也。

《玉藻》之義，在於以服飾儀容明貴賤之分，以聲音步趨養中和之德，誠修身治國之要典也。
