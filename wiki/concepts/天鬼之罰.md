content_legacy: "## English\n\nMozi's doctrine of Heavenly Punishment (天鬼之罰) establishes
  a cosmic mechanism to enforce the principle of upward conformity (尚同). In \" Shangtong
  Zhong,\" Mozi argues that while the hierarchical system of Son of Heaven → Three
  Dukes → feudal lords → officials → village elders can unify human governance, true
  order requires conformity to Heaven's will as well.\n\nAccording to Mozi, even if
  the realm conforms to the Son of Heaven, if it does not conform to Heaven, natural
  disasters will befall the people. These punishments include: uncontrolled cold and
  heat, unseasonable frost and snow, untimely rain, failed grain harvests, unsuccessful
  livestock, plagues, and devastating storms. Mozi describes these as \"Heaven's descending
  punishment upon those below who do not conform upward to Heaven.\"\n\nThe solution
  lies in the ancient sage-kings' reverence for Heaven's will. They clearly understood
  what the spirits and ghosts desired and avoided what they despised, thereby promoting
  benefit for the world and eliminating harm. They led the myriad people in purification
  rites, bathing, and preparing pure offerings and sacrificial grain to serve Heaven
  and the spirits. They ensured that wine and offerings were cleanly prepared, sacrificial
  animals were well-fattened, jade tablets and silks met proper measurements, and
  spring and autumn sacrifices were offered at the correct times. Their administration
  was neither biased nor negligent.\n\nMozi teaches that if those above (Heaven and
  the spirits) favor the virtuous administration of their subordinates, and those
  below (the people) benefit from it, then by vigorously pursuing such endeavors,
  the blessings of Heaven and the spirits can be attained. The people's affection
  can be gained, and the ruler will succeed in all endeavors—defense secured, victories
  achieved in campaigns, and counsel that proves correct.\n\nThis doctrine connects
  the political philosophy of [[尚同]] with the religious philosophy of [[天志]], establishing
  that [[賞譽毀罰]] operates not only through human institutions but also through cosmic
  enforcement.\n\n\n## 日本語\n\n天鬼の罰とは、天下の刑罰の最終的根拠を天鬼に求める墨子の教説である。〈尚同中〉において、天下が天子に尚同しても、天に尚同しなければ天災がくだると論じた。\n\n天鬼の罰の内容は次の通りである。寒熱の節度なく、雪霜雨露が時節を失い、五穀が熟せず、六畜が遂げられず、疾菑疫病がはやり、暴風苦雨が繰り返し訪れる，这都是天の降罰であり、天に尚同しない人々の罰である。\n\n古の聖王はこの道理を明知し、天鬼の欲する所を明らかにし、天鬼の憎む所を避けて、天下の利を興し、天下の害を除いた。因此天下の万民を率いて斎戒沐浴し、酒醴粢盛を絜めて、以って天鬼を祭祀した。\n\n祭祀にあたっては、酒醴粢盛は蠲潔に不敢ず、犧牲は腯肥に不敢ず、珪璧幣帛は度量に中るに不敢ず、春秋祭祀は時幾を失うに不敢ず、聴獄は中不得不中、分財は均らに不敢ず、住処は怠慢に不敢ずとした。\n\n墨子はまた、上なる天鬼は政長を厚いものと為し、下なる万民は政長の便益あるものとする。天鬼の深厚的ものと為りて彊く従事せば、天鬼の福可得；万民便益有るものにして彊く从事せば、万民之親可得と論じた。\n\n如此為政すれば、謀事得、舉事成、入守固、出誅勝となる。この教説は[[天志]]の宗教哲学と[[尚同]]の政治哲学を結びつけ、天地人神之間の調和を求めたものである。\n\n---\n\n##

《墨子·非命下》又引《仲虺之告》以明天鬼賞罰之義。仲虺戒湯曰：「我聞有夏人矯天命于下，帝式是增，用爽厥師。」墨子注云：「彼用無為有，故謂之神。若有而謂有，夫豈謂神哉？」此言桀以虛無為有，妄稱天命以誑民，故帝將加之以咎。

  又引《太誓》之言云：「上帝不常，九有以亡；上帝不順，祝降其喪。」此明上天之賞罰，非盲目之命運，乃鑒察人行為之報應。墨子以為，此皆天鬼賞賢罰暴之明證，非命定之說所能否認者。
