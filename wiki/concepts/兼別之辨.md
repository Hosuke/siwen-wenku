content_legacy: '## English


  The Mohist distinction between *jian* (兼, universality/wholeness) and *bie* (別,
  partiality/division) represents the central axis of Mozi''s political philosophy.
  In *Tianzhi Zhong*, Mozi explicitly frames the choice between these two approaches
  as the determining factor between order and chaos.


  Those who practice *jian*—the sage-kings Yao, Shun, Yu, Tang, Wen, and Wu—govern
  by universal care: "When residing in a great state, they do not attack small states;
  when residing in a great clan, they do not disorder small clans; the strong do not
  rob the weak; the numerous do not tyrannize the few; the cunning do not deceive
  the simple; the honored do not despise the humble." This yields what Mozi terms
  *tian de* (天德, heavenly virtue): benefit to Heaven above, to spirits in the middle,
  and to people below.


  Those who practice *bie*—the tyrant-kings Jie, Zhou, You, and Li—govern by partiality
  and self-interest, creating division and conflict. This yields what Mozi terms *tian
  zei* (天賊, heavenly wrongdoing): benefiting neither Heaven, spirits, nor people.


  The text notes that those who practice *jian* accumulate beautiful names through
  history, recorded in bamboo and silk, inscribed in bronze and stone, transmitted
  to descendants. Those who practice *bie* accumulate ugly names, similarly recorded
  for posterity as warnings.


  The *jian-bie* distinction directly parallels Mozi''s doctrine of [[兼愛]] (universal
  love), but in this passage serves as a practical criterion for evaluating governance.
  Following *jian* brings [[天德與天賊|heavenly virtue]] and reward; practicing *bie* brings
  heavenly punishment.

「兼」與「別」之辨，為墨子政治哲學之核心對立範疇。《天志中》以為，治亂之別、興衰之分，全在於此。

**兼者**，以兼愛為尚，「處大國不攻小國，處大家不亂小家，强不劫弱，衆不暴寡，詐不謀愚，貴不傲賤」。觀其事，則「上利乎天，中利乎鬼，下利乎人，三利無所不利」，是謂[[天志|天德]]。三代聖王堯舜禹湯文武皆從事於兼，故得天的賞賜，「使貴為天子，富有天下，名譽至今不息」。

**別者**，以親疏為別，「處大國則攻小國，處大家則亂小家，强劫弱，衆暴寡，詐謀愚，貴傲賤」。觀其事，則「上不利乎天，中不利乎鬼，下不利乎人」，是謂天賊。三代暴王桀紂幽厲皆從事於別，故得天之罰，「天下天亦縱棄紂而不葆」。

墨子以為，行兼者「書於竹帛，鏤之金石，琢之槃盂，傳遺後世子孫」，美名流芳；行別者亦書之金石，「傳遺後世子孫」，醜名永鑒。此以歷史為天志之佐證，示人取捨之要。

兼別之辨，實為[[順天之意]]與反天之意之踐行層面。行兼即順天，行別即逆天。
