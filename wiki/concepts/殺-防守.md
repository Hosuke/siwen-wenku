## English

The **Defensive Killing Station** (殺) is a fortified barrier system with killing positions described in the Mozi's defensive warfare chapter. According to the text, these killing stations (殺) are positioned every twenty paces along the outer defenses, featuring a wall thickness of ten chi with two gates each five paces wide.

The structure combines with the outer wooden barrier (薄) system: at regular intervals, openings are left in the barrier wall to serve as killing stations. The barrier gates are fitted with wooden barricades that can be easily removed (not cemented), allowing defenders to make rapid counterattacks while remaining capable of restoration. The wall atop the city also has viewing platforms positioned above these barrier gates, with signal posts (楬) for communication.

The killing stations serve as strongpoints where defenders can concentrate fire against swarming enemies while maintaining protected positions. The design reflects Mozi's principle of layered defense—the killing stations extend the effective defense line beyond the city wall itself, forcing attackers to expend resources breaching multiple barriers before reaching the main wall.

## 中文

**殺**者，墨子所述城外防守之殺敵所也。《備蛾傅》篇云：「二十步一殺，有𡑾，厚十尺，殺有兩門，門廣五步，薄門板梯貍之，勿築，令易拔。」

其制：二十步置一殺，有𡑾焉，厚十尺。殺有兩門，門廣五步。薄之門以板為梯，貍附之，勿築，令易拔而復設也。

城外之備，殺居其一。殺與薄相表裡，薄在外以阻敵，殺在內以殲敵。敵攻城者，先薄而遇殺，必被殲焉。城上之人希薄門而置楬，以望敵人之所在。殺之所以名為殺者，取其殺敵之意也。此守城者之要地，不可忽也。

## 日本語

**殺卒（さっそつ）**は、『墨子』防守戦術篇に記述された城外防御施設である。二十歩ごとに配置され、厚さ十尺の壁と幅五歩の門を二つ 갖춘構造物である。薄（障壁）外部と連携し、門には木製の堰板を取り付けて迅速な反撃を可能にする。城壁上部からはこれらの殺卒を見下ろす監視台が置かれ、楬（信号柱）で敵の動態を伝令する。蛾傅攻城の対策として、複数層の防御線を城外に展開し、攻撃側に消耗戦を強いさせる設計思想が認められる。
