## English

"昏禮者，禮之本也" (the wedding ceremony is the root of all rites) is a central philosophical proposition from the *Book of Rites* (《禮記·昏義》). This statement asserts that the wedding ceremony (昏禮) is not merely one ritual among many, but rather serves as the fundamental foundation from which the entire system of ritual (禮) derives its meaning and validity.
