content_legacy: '## English


  Mozi''s doctrine of "太盛難守" (The Superior Perishes) articulates a sobering principle:
  those who possess exceptional qualities—whether in virtue, beauty, courage, or ability—invariably
  face destruction. This is not a moral judgment but a sociological observation about
  the fate of the extraordinary.


  The text provides four vivid examples:


  1. **比干 (Bi Gan)** — Known for his moral courage and frankness, he was put to death
  by King Zhou of Shang for remonstrating against the ruler''s misrule. His death
  demonstrates that moral superiority invites retaliation.


  2. **孟賁 (Meng Ben)** — The legendary strongman of the Warring States period, famed
  for his unparalleled bravery, was ultimately killed. His fate shows that raw physical
  superiority cannot protect one from violence.


  3. **西施 (Xi Shi)** — Celebrated as the most beautiful woman in history, she was
  cast into a river and drowned (or suffered other fates depending on accounts). Her
  beauty, which brought ruin to the state of Yue, also brought ruin to herself.


  4. **吳起 (Wu Qi)** — The brilliant military strategist and political reformer served
  Chu but was later assassinated by nobles who opposed his reforms. His abilities,
  which made him enemies among the ruling elite, led to his death.


  Mozi''s point is that excessive excellence in any domain attracts envy, opposition,
  and ultimately destruction. The sage or talented individual must therefore exercise
  caution and humility, for "太盛難守"—those who shine too brightly will be extinguished.
  This counsel complements the [[不召之臣]] principle of maintaining one''s integrity
  regardless of consequences, and the [[求賢之道]] discussion of how rulers should treat
  the worthy.

「太盛難守」為[[墨子]]所提出的重要命題，出自〈親士〉篇。其核心義理乃言：凡才德出眾、容貌過人、勇力绝倫者，鮮有不遭橫禍者矣。

文中列舉四事以證之：

其一，**比干**。比干以忠諫聞名，見紂王無道，屢次進諫，終被剖心而死。比干之「抗」，謂其節操剛直，不肯阿附暴君。然正直如比干，終不免於難。

其二，**孟賁**。孟賁為戰國時勇士，相傳其力能拔牛角、舉千鈞，以勇著名天下。然如此勇力，終竟被殺，不能自保。

其三，**西施**。西施為越國美女，越王句踐用以亂吳，吳果亡。然西施之美，反為其禍，或沉於江，或謂吳亡後不知所終。

其四，**吳起**。吳起為衛國人，先後仕於魯、魏、楚諸國，以兵法、政治變法聞名。其才智過人，然大功既成，反為楚國舊貴族所嫉，竟遭車裂之刑。

墨子之意，不在責怪賢者之盛，而在警示治國者：過盛之物難以長保，恰如[[甘井近竭，招木近伐]]之喻。此說與[[求賢之道]]相表裡，亦可與[[材士]]條互參。
