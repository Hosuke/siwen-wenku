content_legacy: '## English


  In the chapter "Valuing Righteousness" (貴義), Mozi presents a striking analogy comparing
  incompetent gentlemen of the world to blind people who cannot distinguish black
  from white. While a blind person may learn the words "white is white" and "black
  is black," and even the most sharp-sighted person could not dispute these names,
  when presented with both white and black objects together, the blind person cannot
  identify them. The one who knows white and black is not the one who can distinguish
  them in practice.


  Mozi draws the parallel: "Now the gentlemen of the world name benevolence, yet even
  Yu and Tang could not change this name. But when presented with both benevolent
  and non-benevolent persons, the gentlemen of the world cannot distinguish them."
  The key insight is that merely knowing the name of something is not the same as
  being able to discriminate it in reality. True knowledge requires practical judgment,
  not just verbal familiarity.


  This argument reflects Mozi''s broader logical framework, particularly his emphasis
  on [[兼與別]] (universal care versus partiality) and the [[小取]] (Minor Selection) method
  of distinguishing cases. It criticizes those who speak of benevolence without possessing
  the practical wisdom to recognize its presence or absence in the world. The analogy
  exposes a fundamental disconnect between theoretical knowledge and practical discernment—a
  theme that appears throughout Mozi''s critique of Confucian learning.

《墨子·貴義》篇中，墨子以瞽者喻世之君子，指出認識之名與取象之實的根本區別。瞽者雖知白黑之名，明目者無以易之，然而兼取白黑之物使瞽者辨之，則不能知也。以此諷刺當世之君子雖能言仁義之名，卻不能取仁與不仁而辨之。

墨子以為，仁之名雖禹湯不能易，然兼取仁與不仁，使天下之君子取焉，不能知也。此非以其名不知，乃以其取不知。故墨子斷言：「天下之君子不知仁者，非以其名也，亦以其取也。」

此論反映了墨子對認識論的深刻洞察。在[[小取]]篇中，墨子系統闡述了「以類取，以類予」的邏輯方法，強調分類識別的重要性。瞽者之喻，正是對那種空言仁義而無實踐辨別能力者的尖銳批評。此說與[[兼愛]]之義相通——真正的仁者，不僅要知仁之名，更要能於行事中取仁而去不仁，方為真知。
