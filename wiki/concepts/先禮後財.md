## English

The principle of "先禮後財" (xiān lǐ hòu cái) — prioritizing ritual propriety over material wealth — constitutes a fundamental theme in the "Xiang Yin Jiu Yi" chapter and reflects a broader Confucian doctrine regarding the proper ordering of social values. This principle states that ritual (禮) must take precedence over financial or material considerations in all civilized endeavors.

In the context of the village drinking ceremony, this principle manifests in several concrete ritual prescriptions. When wine is offered as a preliminary sacrifice (祭酒), or when sacrificial meats are presented (祭薦), these actions constitute expressions of "敬禮" (reverent ritual), demonstrating that the ceremony exists primarily to honor proper social relations and moral obligations. The tasting of wine (啐酒) and the completion of the ritual toast (成禮) further reinforce this prioritization. The text explicitly states that such actions occur "於席末" (at the seat's edge), indicating that these ritual elements are not the central focus of the gathering but rather peripheral expressions of a deeper value orientation.

The "卒觶" (final toast) ceremony, where cups are raised one last time and the wine is formally offered at the western steps, provides the most explicit statement of this principle. The text clarifies: "言是席之上，非專為飲食也，此先禮而後財之義也" — meaning that the seat's elevation and the ceremony's elaboration are not fundamentally about eating and drinking, but rather embody the principle of placing ritual before material concerns.

The social consequences of embracing this principle are profound. When "先禮後財" becomes the norm, the people naturally develop "敬讓" (respect and yielding), and conflict becomes rare. Conversely, societies that prioritize material wealth over proper ritual inevitably fall into strife and disorder, as the natural human inclinations toward greed and self-advancement go unchecked by moral education and proper ritual forms.

This principle connects to the broader Confucian teaching that proper ritual creates a " humanity" (仁) society where material abundance serves moral ends rather than becoming ends in themselves. It also relates to the Mencian distinction between "先義而後利" and "後義而先利," where the former leads to social harmony and the latter to chaos.

## 中文

「先禮後財」之義，出於《禮記·鄉飲酒義》。

祭祀之時，祭薦、祭酒，以表敬禮。嚌肺以嚐禮，啐酒以成禮。此皆非專為飲食也，乃所以明禮之重於食也。於席末行之，正所以見禮之非專主乎席之中也。

及其卒觶，致實於西階上，主人之言曰：「言是席之上，非專為飲食也，此先禮而後財之義也。」

**禮與財之辨**，此其要也。禮者，義之節文也；財者，養生之物也。聖人制禮，必使民知禮之為先，財之為後。何哉？蓋禮行則敬讓生，敬讓生則爭奪息；財先則貪慾熾，貪慾熾則鬥亂起。

是故《鄉飲酒義》明言：「先禮而後財，則民作敬讓而不爭矣。」教化之本，莫先於此。

## 日本語

「先禮後財」の原理は、『禮記·鄉飲酒義』において明示される。

郷饮酒の儀礼において、祭薦・祭酒は「敬禮」の表出であり、食物それ自体よりも社会関係と道徳的義務の尊重が本質であることを示す。肺を嚌して礼を嘗め、酒を啐して礼を成す。これらはすべて「席末」で行われる。席の端において行われたのは、飲食が席の中心ではなく、その周辺的価値の表出に過ぎないことを示すためである。

「卒觶」の儀において、杯を西階上に捧げて「席の上は飲食のためばかりではない。先禮而后財の義である」と述べる。ここにおいて、礼と財の優劣が明示される。礼は義の節文であり、財は生存の物的条件である。聖人が礼を定めたのは、民に礼の優先性を認識させ、財の追求が礼の実践に服従させるためであった。

この原理を採用する社会の帰結は深遠である。先禮后財が規範となれけば、民は自然と敬讓を涵養し、争いは稀となる。逆に、財を先に置いて礼を後にする社会は、欲と利己主義が道徳教育と適切な儀礼形態によって抑制されることなく、必然的に葛藤と紊乱に陥る。
