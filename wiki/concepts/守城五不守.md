content_legacy: '## English


  The "Five Conditions Where Defense Is Impossible" (五不守) represent Mohist strategic
  wisdom about the material and human factors that determine siege outcomes. The concluding
  passage of "Mozi · Miscellaneous Defenses" states:


  "凡不守者有五：城大人少，一不守也。城小人衆，二不守也。人衆食寡，三不守也。市去城遠，四不守也。畜積在外，富人在虛，五不守也。"


  These five conditions reveal Mohist realism about urban defense:


  **1. Large city, few defenders** — Overextended garrison cannot adequately cover
  all wall sections.


  **2. Small city, excessive population** — Crowding strains supplies and limits defensive
  mobility.


  **3. Many people, insufficient food** — Provisions deplete before relief arrives.


  **4. Market distant from the city** — Supply lines become vulnerable to interdiction.


  **5. Wealth accumulated outside, wealthy residents within** — Creates internal instability
  as wealthy insiders may negotiate surrender.


  The final statement—"率萬家而城方三里"—suggests optimal defensive scaling: approximately
  10,000 households per three-li (roughly one mile) city circumference. This proportional
  relationship between population, wall perimeter, and defensive capacity reflects
  Mohist engineering principles applied to urban planning.


  These conditions systematize the practical wisdom that even the best fortifications
  fail when human and material factors are misaligned. The "Twelve Siege Techniques"
  (攻城十二術) attack these vulnerabilities; proper defense requires eliminating them beforehand.

《墨子·雜守》末節，墨子總結守城之要：「凡不守者有五：城大人少，一不守也。城小人衆，二不守也。人衆食寡，三不守也。市去城遠，四不守也。畜積在外，富人在虛，五不守也。」

茲分述之：

**一、城大人少** — 守兵分佈於廣大之城牆，每處皆薄弱，易被各個擊破。

**二、城小人衆** — 人口過多則擁擠，物資消耗加劇，守城機械施展不開。

**三、人衆食寡** — 粮秣不給，未待援兵已自困乏。此即 [[城下食寡]] 之義。

**四、市去城遠** — 市場遠離城池，補給線一旦被截，物資無從運入。

**五、畜積在外、富人在虛** — 財寶積於城外，富人居于城中，則城內之心不一，難免有通敵之患。

末云「率萬家而城方三里」，示城郭之制當有常度，不可過大過小。此與 [[攻城十二術]] 相對——攻城者攻此五隙，守城者當先去此五患，方可固守。墨家之學，貴實而去虛，於茲益驗。
