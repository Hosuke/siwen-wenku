## English

**The Three Corpses** (三屍, also written 三尸 or 三殸) are malevolent spiritual entities described in Taoist cosmology as residing within the human body. According to *Baopuzi*, these beings, though intangible, belong to the category of "spirit, ghost, and deity" (魂靈鬼神之屬). Their sole purpose is to bring about the practitioner's premature death, after which they wander freely and enjoy the offerings of sacrificial rites.

The *Baopuzi* describes a specific mechanism: on every *gengshen* day (庚申日), the Three Corpses ascend to heaven to report all of their host's transgressions to the Lord of Fate (司命). Similarly, on the last day of each lunar month (月晦之夜), the stove god (灶神) also reports the household's sins to the celestial authorities. These定时 reports create a periodic accounting system where accumulated sins are formally submitted for divine judgment.

The concept of body-dwelling deities who serve as both guardians and potential adversaries appears throughout Taoist literature. The Three Corpses represent the internal obstacles to immortality—temptations and moral failings that work from within. Successful cultivation requires not only external virtue but also internal transformation of these entities. Related concepts include [[形者生之舍]] (the body as dwelling) and [[真人]] (the perfected person who has overcome such obstacles).

The text notes Ge Hong's own epistemic caution ("吾亦未能審此事之有無也"), acknowledging uncertainty while still presenting the doctrine as practically significant for cultivation.

## 中文

**三屍神**，簡稱三屍，又稱三虫，道教中人體內住之神。其形無形，實為魂靈鬼神之屬。抱朴子稱此三神常欲令人早死，死後乃得離開人體，自行遊行，享受人世祭酹。

三屍每月庚申日上天，向司命稟奏人道人之過失。月晦之夜，灶神亦上天白人罪狀。大者奪紀，小者奪算。此為人體內之神與天上監察系統相呼應之說。

修道者欲登仙籍，必先遣除三屍。相關修煉法門見[[服丹守一]]、 [[學仙之法]]。抱朴子亦稱「吾亦未能審此事之有無」，可見當時对此说尚有存疑，然以天道邈遠、鬼神難明為誡，仍勉人積善立功。

## 日本語

**三屍神**（さんししん）とは、人体内に住みるとされる三つの邪悪な霊的存在を指す。『抱朴子』によれば、これらの存在は形を持たないが、「魂霊鬼神」の一種であり、人間の早期の死を招き、死後に自由に歩き回り、供物を楽しむことを望むという。

毎月庚申日の夜、三屍は天に昇って人間の罪を司命神に報告する。同様に、每月の晦日の夜には、竈神も人間の罪を報告する。この定期的な報告システムにより、蓄積された罪是天によって裁かれる。三屍の克服は道教修煉の重要な目標の一つであり、仙道達成の前提条件とされる。
