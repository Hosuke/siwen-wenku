content_legacy: "---\n\n## English\n\nThe \"Way of Hegemon-Kings\" (霸王之道) represents
  the philosophical framework for righteous warfare and legitimate rule as articulated
  in the *Wenzi*. According to this text, the hegemon's approach combines strategic
  planning with moral purpose—using wisdom to deliberate, policies to chart, and acting
  according to righteousness rather than for selfish preservation.\n\nThe core principle
  states: \"The Way of hegemon-kings uses wisdom for deliberation, strategy for planning,
  and moves according to righteousness, not to secure survival, but to preserve the
  dying and rescue the perishing.\" This distinguishes legitimate military action
  from mere aggression. When encountering a hostile ruler who oppresses the people,
  the righteous leader mobilizes forces to intervene, denouncing the ruler's injustice
  and exposing their faults.\n\nUpon arrival, strict military discipline is maintained:
  no felling of trees, no excavation of graves, no destruction of crops, no burning
  of stockpiles, no capture of civilians, no slaughter of livestock. The proclamation
  declares the enemy ruler guilty of \"going against Heaven and Earth, insulting the
  gods and spirits, rendering unfair judgments, and slaughtering the innocent\"—thus
  becoming an object of heavenly punishment and popular vengeance.\n\nThe key distinction
  lies in the moral framework: \"A righteous army arrives at the border but stops
  without fighting; an unrighteous army creates corpses and bloodshed.\" The hegemon
  wages war to eliminate unrighteousness and bestow virtue, not to conquer or dominate.
  Those who fight for territorial gain cannot achieve kingship; those who seek personal
  advantage cannot establish merit. Only those who act for the sake of others receive
  the support of the many.\n\n[[義兵]] | [[上義]] | [[治國有常]]\n\n---\n\n《商君書 · 卷五》[[慎法]]篇云：千乘能以守者，自存也；萬乘能以戰者，自完也；雖桀為主，不肯詘半辭以下其敵。外不能戰，內不能守，雖堯為主，不能以不臣諧所謂不若之國。國之所以重，主之所以尊者，力也。耕戰二者，力本。能行耕戰二者於境內，則霸王之道畢矣。\n\n
