content_legacy: '## English


  **The First Harvester** (先嗇) represents one of the most ancient and venerated concepts
  in Chinese agricultural ritual, serving as the divine patron of grain and cultivation.
  The term combines 先 (first/prior) with 嗇 (harvest/reaping), signifying the original
  source from which all subsequent harvests derive.


  In the "Record of Rites" chapter "Jiao Te Sheng" (郊特牲), the Eightfold Year-End Sacrifice
  (大蜡八) is described with the First Harvester as its central focus: "蜡之祭也：主先嗇，而祭司嗇也"
  — "In the year-end sacrifice, the First Harvester is the chief object of worship,
  while the Minister of Agriculture is also sacrificed to."


  The concept embodies several layers of meaning:


  **Cosmic Origin**: The First Harvester represents the primordial force or deity
  who first brought grain to humanity. In the agricultural worldview of ancient China,
  cultivated crops were not merely natural phenomena but divine gifts requiring acknowledgment
  and gratitude.


  **Agricultural Success**: As the spirit presiding over the origin of grain cultivation,
  the First Harvester embodies the hope for continued agricultural productivity. The
  phrase "主先嗇" (chief First Harvester) indicates this deity''s supreme position in
  the ritual hierarchy of agricultural spirits.


  **Complementary to the Minister of Agriculture (司嗇)**: While the First Harvester
  represents the divine origin of grain, the Minister of Agriculture represents the
  human institutional apparatus that manages cultivation—the officials, the calendrical
  calculations, and the distribution of seeds. Together, they form a complete picture
  of agricultural causation, from divine gift to human management.


  The worship of 先嗇 reflects the ancient Chinese understanding that human prosperity
  depends upon forces beyond human control. The First Harvester is neither purely
  celestial nor purely agricultural but occupies an intermediate position—divine yet
  intimately connected to the earth, transcendent yet immanent in every grain of rice.

**先嗇**者，古祭祀中供奉之穀神，乃百穀始生之神，其義為「最初之收穫者」也。

《禮記·郊特牲》述大蜡八之祭曰：「蜡之祭也，主先嗇，而祭司嗇也。」是先嗇為蜡祭之主祭对象，司嗇次之。

先嗇之義涵有三：

**宇宙本源之意**：先嗇代表宇宙間最初赐予人类穀物之神靈。在古人眼中，栽培作物非自然現象，乃神聖恩賜，當以祭祀報答之。

**農業豐收之意**：先嗇為穀物種植之守護神，象徵對農業持續豐收之期盼。「主先嗇」表明此神靈在農業祭祀神靈體系中之至尊地位。

**與司嗇相配之意**：先嗇代表穀物之神圣起源，司嗇代表管理種植之人類制度——官員、曆法計算、種子分配等。二者合而言之，構成農業因果之完整理論：神聖恩賜與人類管理相輔相成。

祭祀先嗇，反映古人認知：人類繁盛有賴人力以外之力量。先嗇既非純然天界，亦非純然農業，而是居於中間位置——神聖而親近大地，超然又內在於每一粒稻穀之中。

蜡祭又有「祭百種」之禮，以報嗇也。百種者，黍、稷、稻、粱、麥、菽、麻等百穀也。先嗇、司嗇、百種，此三者為蜡祭核心之祭焉。
