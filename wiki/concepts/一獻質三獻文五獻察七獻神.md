## English

The passage "一獻質，三獻文，五獻察，七獻神" presents a graduated hierarchy of ritual offerings in ancient Chinese ceremonial practice. Each level represents an increasing degree of spiritual refinement and ceremonial elaboration.

**一獻質 (One offering: simplicity):** The most basic level of ritual, characterized by straightforward sincerity without elaborate form. This represents the foundation of all ritual practice—genuine intention expressed through minimal external ornamentation.

**三獻文 (Three offerings: cultural refinement):** The intermediate level introduces cultural and aesthetic elements. The ritual incorporates music, speech, and decorative forms that express the community's shared values and civilized order.

**五獻察 (Five offerings: careful observation):** At this level, the ritual becomes more discriminating and hierarchical. The offerings are carefully arranged according to rank and merit, reflecting the cosmic order of heaven and the graduated responsibilities of social roles.

**七獻神 (Seven offerings: spiritual):** The highest level transcends material offerings to achieve communion with the divine. At this level, the ritual participant experiences a kind of spiritual transformation where the boundary between human and heavenly diminishes.

This hierarchy illustrates a fundamental principle of Confucian ritual: genuine reverence cannot be manufactured through elaborate ceremony alone. Rather, sincere intention (質) must precede and inform cultural expression (文), careful discrimination (察), and ultimately spiritual communion (神). The progression suggests that true spirituality in ritual emerges naturally from proper foundation rather than being imposed by external grandeur.

## 中文

「一獻質，三獻文，五獻察，七獻神」見於《禮記·禮器》篇，表述古代祭祀禮儀之等級結構。此七獻之分，非徒等級之分別，乃心性修養之階梯也。

**一獻之質：** 質者，誠也。鄭玄注云：「質，誠也。」一獻之禮，貴在誠心，不假文飾。祭祀之初，求諸心之誠摯，非求諸器之繁縟。是故至敬不壇，埽地而祭；大圭不琢，大羹不和。此皆一獻質之意也。
