content_legacy: '## English


  **"Loving the People Without Delay"** is Mozi''s directive concerning the proper
  manner in which those in authority must govern, emphasizing that genuine love for
  the people must be accompanied by swift and sincere action, ultimately winning their
  trust.


  In the concluding section of the chapter, Mozi addresses the ideal of [[尚同]] governance:
  "What enables the people to practice Unity with Superiors? Love the people ardently,
  and they will not be unmovable; establish trust and hold to it, enrich and honor
  them in the present, clarify punishments to lead them thereafter. Governing in this
  way, even if you wished to prevent them from unifying with you, it would be impossible."


  The key elements are: (1) **"疾愛"** — loving the people with urgency and intensity,
  not superficially or halfheartedly; (2) **"致信"** — establishing genuine trust through
  consistent and reliable conduct; (3) **"富貴以道其前"** — providing material prosperity
  and social advancement as incentives; and (4) **"明罰以率其後"** — making punishments
  clear and certain as deterrents.


  Mozi emphasizes that [[賞罰得下之情]] (reward and punishment based on understanding the
  people''s actual situation) is effective only when the ruler genuinely loves the
  people. If the people do not trust or feel loved by the ruler, even clear and fair
  rewards and punishments will not motivate them to report good and evil as the [[尚同]]
  system requires.


  This principle reveals the integration of Mozi''s political thought with his ethical
  foundation of [[兼愛]] (Universal Love). Political unity cannot be achieved through
  coercion alone; it must be grounded in the ruler''s genuine concern for the people''s
  welfare.

愛民不疾，乃墨子論執政者治民之道的教誨，強調上位者愛民當出於至誠而又急速，切不可延緩怠慢，務使民信之而後可以共圖治理。

篇末云：「凡使民尚同者，愛民不疾，民無可使。」此言欲使民行尚同之道，則執政者必先以愛民為急務。若愛民不誠不速，則民不見信，亦不肯為上所用，尚同之政無由而成也。

墨子具體陳說治民之法曰：「必疾愛而使之，致信而持之，富貴以道其前，明罰以率其後。」蓋謂：

一、疾愛：愛民當以迅速為要，不可遲疑觀望；
二、致信：務使民信於上，上亦當信於民，上下相得；
三、富貴以道其前：以財利祿位導之於前，使民知所趨向；
四、明罰以率其後：以賞罰分明驅之於後，使民知所戒懼。

有此四者，則民自然稟承上意，以善相告，以惡相聞，尚同之道可得矣。

此說可與[[賞罰得下之情]]相發明。蓋賞罰之所以能勸善沮暴者，以民信上之賞罰為公，而賞罰之所以能使民相信者，以其愛民之誠也。
