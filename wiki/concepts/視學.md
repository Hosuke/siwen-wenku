content_legacy: '## English


  The "視學" (Royal Inspection of Education) ceremony documented in *Liji · Wenwang
  Shizi* represents a comprehensive ritual through which the ruler demonstrates respect
  for education and the elderly, thereby establishing moral authority over the realm.


  The ceremony begins at dawn: "天子視學，大昕鼓徵，所以警眾也。眾至，然後天子至。" The great bell summons
  the people to assembly, and only after they gather does the ruler arrive, symbolizing
  that the ruler comes to serve the people, not the reverse.


  The ritual proceeds with sacrifices: "乃命有司行事。興秩節，祭先師先聖焉。有司卒事，反命。" The officials
  perform the sacrifice to former teachers and sages, and report back to the ruler
  upon completion.


  The "養老" (veneration of the elderly) portion follows: "始之養也：適東序，釋奠於先老，遂設三老五更群老之席位焉。"
  The ruler personally oversees the arrangements, examining the food and wine, ensuring
  that precious provisions are prepared for the elderly.


  After the ceremony, the ruler ascends to sing at the Qingmiao Temple: "反，登歌清廟，既歌而語，以成之也。言父子、君臣、長幼之道，合德音之致，禮之大者也。"
  Through song and discourse, the ruler completes the teaching of proper relationships.


  The ceremony concludes with the king ordering officials: "下管《象》，舞《大武》...有司告以樂闋，王乃命公侯伯子男及群吏曰：『反！養老幼於東序。』終之以仁也。"
  The ceremony thus culminates in benevolence, with the ruler returning to care for
  the elderly and young at the Easter Hall.

《禮記·文王世子》記天子視學之禮甚詳。天子至學宮，先擊鼓以徵眾：「大昕鼓徵，所以警眾也。眾至，然後天子至。」此天子先虛己以敬事也。

既至，命有司行事：「興秩節，祭先師先聖焉。有司卒事，反命。」

其養老之儀：「始之養也：適東序，釋奠於先老，遂設三老五更群老之席位焉。適饌省醴，養老之珍，具；遂發詠焉，退修之以孝養也。」

登歌清廟，乃下管象舞，歌罷而語，以成父子、君臣、長幼之道。[[樂正司業]]，[[父師司成]]，[[一有元良]]，萬國以貞，[[樂正]]之司[[學]]之[[業]]，[[父師]]之司[[成德]]，皆於此時見之。

末乃命公侯伯子男及群吏曰：「反！養老幼於東序。」終之以[[仁]]也。
