content_legacy: '## English


  The *Ji Tong* chapter systematically discusses the four seasonal sacrifices (*si
  shi zhi ji*, 四時之祭), which form a crucial component of the sacrificial calendar in
  classical Chinese ritual systems. These are: *yue* (礿, spring sacrifice), *di* (禘,
  summer sacrifice), *chang* (嘗, autumn sacrifice), and *zheng* (烝, winter sacrifice).


  The text states: 「凡祭有四時：春祭曰礿，夏祭曰禘，秋祭曰嘗，冬祭曰烝。礿禘，陽義也；嘗烝，陰義也。禘者，陽之盛也；嘗者，陰之盛也。故曰：『莫重於禘嘗。』」


  This cosmological framework assigns the four sacrifices to the yin-yang and Five
  Phases system:

  - *Yue* (礿) and *di* (禘) are associated with *yang* (陽), representing the expansive,
  active principle of the universe.

  - *Chang* (嘗) and *zheng* (烝) are associated with *yin* (陰), representing the contracting,
  receptive principle.


  The *di* (禘) and *chang* (嘗) are designated as particularly weighty (*莫重於禘嘗*), a
  doctrine that had significant political and ritual implications for imperial sacrifice.
  The *di* sacrifice, as the supreme expression of *yang*, was traditionally the most
  magnificent of all state sacrifices.


  The text further connects seasonal sacrifices to governance: 「古者：於禘也，發爵賜服，順陽義也；於嘗也，出田邑，發秋政，順陰義也。故《記》曰：『嘗之日，發公室，示賞也。』」
  This indicates that the timing of governmental acts—appointments, land grants, military
  campaigns—was calibrated to align with the cosmological qualities of each seasonal
  sacrifice.

《禮記·祭統》詳論四時祭祀之制，此為古代中國祭祀曆法之核心。其文云：「凡祭有四時：春祭曰礿，夏祭曰禘，秋祭曰嘗，冬祭曰烝。」

四時祭祀與陰陽義理相配：礿、禘屬陽，嘗、烝屬陰。禘為陽之盛，嘗為陰之盛，故古人有「莫重於禘嘗」之說，強調夏祭之禘與秋祭之嘗尤為重要。禘者，報本反始之大祭，示陽氣之盛大；嘗者，秋成之後，品嘗新穀，亦陰氣漸盛之象。

祭祀與政事相應：於禘之時，發爵賜服，順陽義也；於嘗之時，出田邑，發秋政，順陰義也。嘗之日，發公室，示賞也。又言「草艾則墨，未發秋政，則民弗敢草」，明祭祀之時令與農事、政事皆相配合。此以祭祀為治國之本之義也。

四時之祭，實貫通天人之道，使人間秩序與自然節律相諧。故古之明主，必敬慎四時之祭，以成陰陽之和，而致天下之治。
