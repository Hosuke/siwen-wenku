## English

**Seasonal Hunting** (歲時田獵) was a regulated system of hunting conducted according to the agricultural calendar, serving both as military training and as ritual observance. According to [[禮記·王制]], the Son of Heaven and the vassal lords were required to engage in three hunts per year, provided there were no major state affairs requiring their attention.

The purposes of these hunts were strictly ordered: first, to provide offerings for the ancestral temples (乾豆); second, to supply provisions for guests (賓客); third, to furnish the ruler's kitchen (充君之庖). To hunt without reason was considered disrespectful (不敬); to hunt without observing proper ritual was deemed a violation of heavenly creatures (暴天物).

The text established detailed ecological prohibitions: the Son of Heaven shall not surround game on all sides; vassal lords shall not slaughter whole herds; when the Son of Heaven kills, the great banner is lowered; when a vassal kills, the small banner is lowered; when a great officer kills, the assisting carriage stops. Once the assisting carriage stops, the common people may hunt.

The seasonal sequence was strictly observed: after otters offer fish (獺祭魚), the wetland officers may enter to construct fishing weirs; after wolves offer beasts (豺祭獸), the hunt may begin; after hawks transform (鳩化為鷹), then the nets may be set; only after plants wither and leaves fall (草木零落) may one enter the mountain forests; insects that have not yet hibernated shall not be burned for cultivation; young deer shall not be taken, eggs shall not be raided, pregnant animals shall not be killed, and nests shall not be overturned.

These regulations reflected the ancient wisdom of sustainable resource management, integrated with ritual propriety and military readiness.

## 中文

**歲時田獵**者，依時節而行田獵之禮也。《禮記·王制》曰：「天子諸侯无事則歲三田。」蓋歲三田者，非徒娛樂之為，實有深意存焉。

三田之用，各有次第：一為乾豆，以供祭祀；二為賓客，以待賢士；三為充君之庖，以備膳羞。無故而不田，曰不敬；田不以禮，曰暴天物。可見田獵之事，非可輕動，必合乎義，循乎禮也。

其田也有節：天子的諸侯不合圍，恐竭澤而漁也；諸侯不掩群，恐盡取之也。天子殺則下大綏，諸侯殺則下小綏，大夫殺則止佐車，佐車止而後百姓田獵。此皆所以明等級、慎殺伐之意也。

又有四時之禁：獺祭魚，然後虞人入澤梁；豺祭獸，然後田獵；鳩化為鷹，然後設罻羅；草木零落，然後入山林；昆蟲未蟄，不以火田。不麛，不卵，不殺胎，不殀夭，不覆巢。凡此皆本天地自然之理，取物以時，不違其性，可謂仁之至、義之盡也。

歲時田獵之制，合[[籍田禮]]觀之，可見周人重農而不忘武事，講禮而兼明刑罰，治國之道備矣。
