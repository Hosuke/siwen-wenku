## English

The "Distinction Between Spirits and Ghosts" (神鬼之辨) constitutes a fundamental category in classical Chinese religious thought, as elaborated in the *Book of Rites*. This conceptual framework organized the supernatural world according to human benefit, establishing criteria for veneration and worship.
