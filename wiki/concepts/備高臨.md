  to Mozi's defensive strategies for countering enemies who construct elevated earthworks—known
  as *yangqian* (羊黔)—to overlook and assault city walls. This chapter in the *Mozi*
  text details both the inadequacy of such siege techniques and the specific countermeasures
  defenders should employ.\n\nAccording to Mozi, the *yangqian* technique—where enemies
  pile earth and firewood to create a raised platform level with or above the city
  wall—is a \"clumsy\" siege method that exhausts the attackers' forces without posing
  a serious threat to a well-prepared defense. The key defensive structures include
  building observation platforms (*tai cheng*) on the city walls, positioning large
  crossbow mechanisms, and maintaining sufficient supplies of projectiles.\n\nThe
  text emphasizes that a properly defended city with skilled crossbow operators and
  coordinated defensive measures can easily defeat attackers using elevated siege
  tactics. The chapter concludes with specifications for a *continuous-fire crossbow
  vehicle* (連弩之車), a massive defensive weapon capable of firing sixty arrows in succession,
  designed to dominate attackers on elevated positions.\n\nCross-references: [[備梯]],

此篇所述攻城之法，敵人積土為高臺，居高臨下以攻吾城。墨子以為，羊黔之守不過「將之拙者」，足以勞卒，不足以害城。防禦之法，在於行城之制——左右出巨各二十尺，行城三十尺，配以強弩、技機，足以敗敵之攻。
  
  尤其關鍵者，乃**連弩之車**之製：
  
  - **車體**：杖大方一方一尺，長稱城之薄厚。兩軸三輪，輪居筐中，重下上筐
  - **結構**：左右㫄二植，左右有衡植，衡植皆圜內，內徑四寸
  - **弩臂**：長六尺，橫臂齊筐外，蚤尺五寸，有距博六寸
  - **機括**：筐大三圍半，左右有鉤距，方三寸，輪厚尺二寸，銅距臂博尺四寸
  - **射具**：矢長十尺，以繩□□矢端，如戈射，以𢉳卷收
  - **威力**：矢高弩臂三尺，用弩無數，出人六十枚（小矢無留）
  
  此車配以高樓，敵來則以射道、城上荅羅矢禦之。十人主此車，遂具寇。
  
  ### 羊黔之守
  
  羊黔者，敵人積土為高臺之術。土薪俱上，蒙櫓俱前，遂屬之城，兵弩俱上，此攻城之要術也。然墨子以為，守方若能善用行城、連弩之車，則羊黔之攻可敗。
  
  ### 行城之法
  
  守爲臺城，以臨羊黔。行城三十尺，左右出巨各二十尺，配以強弩、技機、奇器，足以殄敵。
