## English

The **Diplomatic Mission** system (聘問) constituted the formal diplomatic intercourse between the Son of Heaven and the feudal lords, as well as among the lords themselves. The *Book of Rites: Royal Regulations* establishes a graduated schedule of diplomatic contact: "Regarding the relationship between feudal lords and the Son of Heaven, there shall be a minor embassy (小聘) in the second year, a major embassy (大聘) in the third year, and a personal audience (朝) in the fifth year."

This three-tiered system of diplomatic missions served multiple functions. The minor embassy involved lower-ranking officials delivering messages and gifts, maintaining communication without requiring the lord's personal presence. The major embassy was a more substantial delegation, typically led by a senior minister, symbolizing deeper commitment to the relationship. The personal audience represented the highest form of diplomatic contact, requiring the feudal lord to appear before the Son of Heaven in person.
