content_legacy: "---\n\n## English\n\nThe **Diplomatic Mission** system (聘問) constituted
  the formal diplomatic intercourse between the Son of Heaven and the feudal lords,
  as well as among the lords themselves. The *Book of Rites: Royal Regulations* establishes
  a graduated schedule of diplomatic contact: \"Regarding the relationship between
  feudal lords and the Son of Heaven, there shall be a minor embassy (小聘) in the second
  year, a major embassy (大聘) in the third year, and a personal audience (朝) in the
  fifth year.\"\n\nThis three-tiered system of diplomatic missions served multiple
  functions. The minor embassy involved lower-ranking officials delivering messages
  and gifts, maintaining communication without requiring the lord's personal presence.
  The major embassy was a more substantial delegation, typically led by a senior minister,
  symbolizing deeper commitment to the relationship. The personal audience represented
  the highest form of diplomatic contact, requiring the feudal lord to appear before
  the Son of Heaven in person.\n\nThe text further elaborates that when the Son of
  Heaven and feudal lords meet without special circumstances\n\n---\n\n來盟，前定也。不言及者，以國與之；不言其人，亦以國與之。不日，前定之盟不日。蓋盟之先期約定者，非臨時之會，故從簡也。\n\n

天王使南季來聘，《穀梁傳》曰：聘，問也。聘諸侯，非正也。聘者，天子遣使問候於諸侯之禮。然聘諸侯而非問天王，是以下問上，故《春秋》以為非正。蓋聘問之禮，當以上逮下為正，以下聘上則非禮之正也。

**所出**：《春秋穀梁傳 · 隱公》 — https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/春秋穀梁傳/隱公

又有諸侯外交之禮曰[[聘問會誓盟]]，亦見於《禮記》本文。聘者，遣使問訊鄰國也；問疾問喪，皆聘之義。會者，郤地相見也；遇者，不及期偶逢也。誓者，約信以言也；盟者，蒞牲出血而約也。五者輕重之序，在本文中各有其義，學者當合觀而參考之。
