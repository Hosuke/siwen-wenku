content_legacy: '## English


  The prevention of internal saboteurs (姦民防制) represents one of the most distinctive
  and severe aspects of Mozi''s defensive command system. In the chaos of a siege,
  the greatest threat often comes not from without but from within—collaborators,
  spies, and defeatists who would open the gates to the enemy or sabotage defensive
  preparations from within the walls.


  The text establishes an extensive network of mutual surveillance. Every ten households
  on the city wall are organized into units that monitor each other. Family members
  living in the alleyways below the wall are similarly organized to maintain watch
  on their neighbors. Those who fail to report crimes within their unit are held collectively
  responsible and punished along with the actual offenders.


  The punishment for treasonous activity is extraordinarily severe. Those who conspire
  with the enemy or plan to harm their commanders are treated identically to those
  who openly rebel—the penalty being dismemberment by chariots (車裂), an ancient Chinese
  punishment in which the victim''s limbs are tied to separate chariots that are then
  driven in opposite directions. More shocking still, the families of deserters—parents,
  wives, children, and siblings—are all subjected to the same terrible fate.


  However, the system also incorporates incentives for betrayal of traitors. Those
  who inform on conspirators before the plot is carried out are not only exempted
  from punishment but receive rewards: two yi of gold for ordinary citizens who expose
  conspirators, and the promise of a thousand-household fief for those who capture
  and report enemies who have infiltrated the city.


  The system extends to preventing unauthorized communication with the enemy. Soldiers
  and civilians are forbidden from speaking with those across the walls, from using
  projectiles to send messages, or from any contact that might convey information
  to the besieging army. Violations of these prohibitions result in the criminal''s
  entire family being executed and their bodies displayed on the city walls.

姦民防制，乃墨子守城號令之要務也。攻城之時，姦細之患甚於外寇，故防制之法不得不嚴。

其法以什伍連坐為本。城下里中家人皆相葆，若城上之數。左右有罪而不智也，其次伍有罪。若能身捕罪人若告之吏，皆購之。若非伍而先知他伍之罪，皆倍其購賞。

姦民之所謀為外心者，罪車裂。𦈢與父老及吏主部者不得，皆斬；得之，除，又賞之黃金人二鎰。伍人不得，斬；得之，除。伍人踰城歸敵，伍人不得，斬；與伯歸敵，隊吏斬；與吏歸敵，隊將斬。

歸敵者，父母、妻子、同產皆車裂；先覺之，除。當術需敵，離地，斬。伍人不得，斬；得之，除。

禁約之條尤嚴。城中禁使卒民不欲寇微職和旌者，斷。非擅出令者，斷。失令者，斷。倚戟縣下城，上下不與衆等者，斷。無應而妄讙呼者，斷。總失者，斷。譽客內毀者，斷。離署而聚語者，斷。聞城鼓聲而伍，後上署者，斷。

又禁以矢書射敵，犯令者父母妻子皆斷，身梟城上。此法雖嚴，然賞罰相須，亦所以使人知所趨避也。
