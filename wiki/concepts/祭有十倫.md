content_legacy: '## English


  The text presents a systematic enumeration of the "ten categories of meaning" (十倫)
  inherent in the ritual of sacrifice. This classification demonstrates the Confucian
  conviction that sacrifice, far from being merely a religious observance, serves
  as a comprehensive model for understanding the entire order of human relationships
  and cosmic harmony.


  The ten categories are:


  1. **Seeing the way of serving ghosts and spirits** (見事鬼神之道) — Sacrifice is the
  paradigm for proper religious consciousness and reverence toward the supernatural
  order.


  2. **Seeing the principle between ruler and minister** (見君臣之義) — The hierarchical
  ordering modeled in the sacrificial context reflects political structure.


  3. **Seeing the relationship between father and son** (見父子之倫) — Sacrifice reenacts
  and reinforces generational bonds and filial obligations.


  4. **Seeing gradations of noble and base** (見貴賤之等) — Ritual properly distinguishes
  social stations, teaching each person their proper place.


  5. **Seeing the graduated intimacy of kinship** (見親疏之殺) — Mourning grades and sacrifice
  express the graduated nature of family relationships.


  6. **Seeing the施 of reward and punishment** (見爵賞之施) — Sacrifice incorporates principles
  of merit-based recognition and governmental administration.


  7. **Seeing the separation between husband and wife** (見夫婦之別) — The sexual division
  of labor and complementary roles are enacted and taught through ritual.


  8. **Seeing the equalization of political affairs** (見政事之均) — Just governance is
  reflected in the equal distribution of ritual attention and resources.


  9. **Seeing the proper sequence of elder and younger** (見長幼之序) — Age-based precedence
  is maintained and demonstrated in ceremonial contexts.


  10. **Seeing the junction between high and low** (見上下之際) — The vertical connections
  between ruler and ruled are expressed and affirmed.


  Each category serves both as a description of what sacrifice reveals and as a prescription
  for how society should be ordered. The sacrifice hall becomes a microcosm of the
  well-governed state.

《祭統》所言「祭有十倫」，乃儒家以祭祀為社會秩序藍圖之系統論述。此十倫者，實為人倫大義之全面闡發：

一倫、見事鬼神之道——祭祀鬼神有其道，修德以享神，非僅備物供奉而已。此與 [[明乎郊社之義]] 相關。

二倫、見君臣之義——祭祀之時，君臣位分赫然分明。此 [[霸王之道]] 之儀軌也。

三倫、見父子之倫——祖孫相承，父死子繼，祭祀正是此血脈延續之象徵。與 [[孝弟忠順]] 相發明。

四倫、見貴賤之等——俎豆之間，禮數各異，貴賤分明。此 [[辨貴賤長幼]] 之道也。

五倫、見親疏之殺——親者服重，疏者服輕，恩義厚薄，因倫而異。此 [[從輕而重]] 之義。

六倫、見爵賞之施——祭祀之日，亦行賞賜。此古者顯忠敬於先祖之義。

七倫、見夫婦之別——祭祀必夫婦親之，男先女後，各有其職。此 [[男女有別]] 之明證。

八倫、見政事之均——祭祀普施惠澤，無有偏私。此 [[絜矩之道]] 之施行也。

九倫、見長幼之序——昭穆齒敘，此 [[長幼有序]] 之義。

十倫、見上下之際——恩澤自上而下，流被百姓。此 [[惠下之道]] 之要義也。

十倫之說，可謂儒家社會倫理之全幅呈現。祭祀非僅祭祖祀神而已，乃整飭人倫、涵養風俗之大道也。
