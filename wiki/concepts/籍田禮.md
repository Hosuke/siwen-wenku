content_legacy: '## English


  The **Ritual Ploughing** (籍田禮, also 親耕 or 帝藉) was an important agricultural ceremony
  in ancient China in which the Son of Heaven personally guided a plough through a
  sacred field dedicated to the imperial ancestors and the Lord of Grain (后稷). This
  ritual, recorded in the *Monthly Ordinances* (月令) and other classical texts, symbolized
  the ruler''s direct involvement in and responsibility for agricultural production.


  According to the *Monthly Ordinances*, in the first month of spring (孟春之月), after
  the ceremonial welcoming of spring (迎春), the ruler undertook the ritual ploughing.
  The rites proceeded as follows:


  The Son of Heaven selected an auspicious day (元日) to pray for grain to the Supreme
  Lord (上帝), then chose a favorable date (元辰) for the ploughing itself. He personally
  loaded the plough (耒耜) onto the protective chariot (參保介之御間), and led the Three Dukes,
  Nine Ministers, and various lords to the sacred field (帝藉). The emperor pushed the
  plough three times (三推), the dukes five times (公五推), and the ministers and lords
  nine times (卿諸侯九推). Upon completion, a celebratory feast (勞酒) was held in the great
  sleeping hall (大寢), at which all officials attended.


  The *Zhou Li* records the existence of the Domain of the Imperial Domain (甸師), whose
  officers cultivated the imperial fields and supplied grain for sacrifices. The *Book
  of Documents* (尚書) mentions the "ploughing ceremony of Yu the Great" (禹親執耒耜以墾草萊),
  establishing the model''s antiquity.


  The ritual ploughing served multiple purposes: it demonstrated the ruler''s virtue
  through personal labor, invoked divine blessing upon the agricultural season, encouraged
  the populace to devote themselves to farming, and legitimized the ruling house through
  successful harvest.

**籍田禮**者，天子親執耒耜，耕於帝藉，以勸天下農事之禮也。亦稱「親耕」「帝藉之禮」。

據《禮記·月令》：「是月也，天子乃以元日祈穀于上帝。乃擇元辰，天子親載耒耜，措之于參保介之御間，帥三公九卿諸侯大夫，躬耕帝藉。天子三推，公五推，卿諸侯九推。」

其禮也，先祈穀上帝，後擇吉辰躬耕。耒耜者，古農具也，以木為耒、以金為耜。天子所乘有甲士護衛，曰「保介」。三推、五推、九推者，人數遞加，以別尊卑也。

《周禮·甸師》：「掌帥其屬而耕耨王藉，以時入之，以供齍盛。」甸師之官，主耕王籍，收其穀以供祭祀。

籍田之義有三：其一，天子身先天下，躬耕以勸農；其二，祈求上帝庇佑，風雨以時，嘉生蕃庶；其三，明君重農務本，以養兆民。《書》曰：「禹親執耒耜以墾草萊」，是先王之遺制也。
