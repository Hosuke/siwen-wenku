## English

**明器** (spirit vessels or tomb objects) represents one of the most philosophically rich concepts in *Liji* concerning mortuary practice. In *Tan Gong Shang*, the discussion of 明器 involves a debate between Zong Xian (仲宪) and Zengzi regarding how the Three Dynasties understood these objects.

Zong Xian articulated a progressive theory: "夏后氏用明器，示民無知也；殷人用祭器，示民有知也；周人兼用之，示民疑也。" — "The Xia used spirit vessels, showing the people were without knowledge; the Shang used ritual vessels, showing the people possessed knowledge; the Zhou used both, showing the people were in doubt."

Zengzi vigorously rejected this interpretation: "曾子曰：『其不然乎！其不然乎！夫明器，鬼器也；祭器，人器也；夫古之人，胡為而死其親乎？』" — "Nonsense! Spirit vessels are for ghosts; ritual vessels are for humans. Why would the ancients treat their departed relatives as lacking awareness?"

Confucius later elaborated the principle underlying 明器, stating: "之死而致死之，不仁而不可為也。之死而致生之，不知而不可為也。是故竹不成用，瓦不成味，木不成斫，琴瑟張而不平，竽笙備而不和，有鐘磬而無簨虡。其曰明器，神明之也。" — "To treat the dead as if utterly gone is inhuman; to treat them as if still living is unwise. Therefore [vessels] of bamboo are non-functional, clay pots lack flavor, wood is uncarved, stringed instruments are strung but untuned, wind instruments are complete but unharmonized, bells and chimes lack their frames. These are called spirit vessels — for the spirits."

The 明器 principle embodies the Confucian doctrine of the Mean in death rituals: providing for the deceased as they transition between states, acknowledging their altered nature while maintaining proper provision. Compare [[飯於牖下]] and [[小斂]] for related mortuary procedures.

## 中文

「明器」者，葬禮所用之器物，以供死者之魂用者也。《禮記·檀弓上》記仲憲與曾子之問答，論三代明器之義：

仲憲云：「夏后氏用明器，示民無知也；殷人用祭器，示民有知也；周人兼用之，示民疑也。」

曾子駁之曰：「其不然乎！夫明器，鬼器也；祭器，人器也；夫古之人，胡為而死其親乎？」

曾子以明器為鬼神之器，祭器為生人之器，古人厚待其親，非以民之無知有知為說。

至若孔子論明器之義則尤精：「是故竹不成用，瓦不成味，木不成斫，琴瑟張而不平，竽笙備而不和，有鐘磬而無簨虡。其曰明器，神明之也。」

明器之設，乃取「致生之不可，致死之亦不可」之中道，既非以死者為無知而忽之，亦非以為有生而過分之備。此乃[[禮記]]「[[禮所以制中]]」之義也。

明器與[[大斂]]、 [[小斂]]之附身器物，皆為葬禮中重要環節。
