content_legacy: "## English\n\n**學仙之法** (xué xiān zhī fǎ) — the methods of learning
  immortality — constitutes the practical teaching of *Baopuzi*. Ge Hong outlines
  specific practices that distinguish his Daoist approach from mere charlatanry.\n\n**Core
  Practices:**\n\n1. **Medicinal Elixirs** (藥物養身): The consumption of golden elixirs
  (金丹) and cinnabar preparations to transform the body. Ge Hong insists these must
  be prepared according to secret oral instructions (口傳心記), not mere written formulas.\n\n2.
  **Numerological Techniques** (術數延命): Practices for extending life through cosmic
  timing, breathing exercises, and calendar-based cultivation.\n\n3. **Internal Cultivation**:
  內視反聽 (looking inward and listening within), 屍居無心 (dwelling like a corpse without
  mind), achieving profound stillness and emptiness.\n\n4. **Dietary Abstinence**
  (辟穀/休糧): Abstaining from grains and ordinary food to purify the body. Even Han Wudi
  achieved \"small benefits\" from this practice.\n\n**Essential Conditions:**\n\nGe
  Hong emphasizes that these practices cannot be combined with worldly success:\n-
  The emperor cannot \"cover his intelligence and conceal his faculties\" while governing\n-
  The wealthy cannot abandon their attachments while maintaining their possessions\n-
  Those seeking immortality must give up 榮華 (glory), 厚玩 (rich treasures), 聲譽 (reputation)\n-
  They must treat 富貴 as 不幸 (misfortune), 榮華 as 穢汙 (filth)\n\n**The Three Types of
  Immortals:**\n\nGe Hong cites 仙經 (immortal scriptures) to classify practitioners:\n-
  **天仙** (Celestial Immortals): 上士舉形昇虛 — the superior person rises bodily into the
  void\n- **地仙** (Earth Immortals): 中士游於名山 — the middling person wanders among famous
  mountains\n- **尸解仙** (Corpse-Releasing Immortals): 下士先死後蛻 — the lower person dies
  first, then sheds the corpse\n\nThe case of Li Shaojun exemplifies 尸解: he appeared
  to die, was buried, but his coffin contained only clothing — his body had been \"released.\"\n\nSee

**學仙之法**者，抱朴子之實踐功夫也。葛洪詳述修仙之要道，以別於方術之妄。

**修煉之要：**

一、**服食藥物**：以金丹大藥變化身形。葛洪強調，此法必須口傳心記，不可僅據文字。

二、**術數延命**：通過吐納、存思、導引諸法，調理陰陽，以延年壽。

三、**內視反聽**：收视返聽，內觀己身，反求諸心。

四、**尸居無心**：如屍之居，無思無慮，達於虛靜。

五、**辟穀休糧**：絕粒清腸，滌除濁穢。

**世務之不可兼：**

葛洪反覆陳明，帝王之尊、功名之利、聲色之娛，皆與學仙相妨：
- 帝王撞千石之鐘、伐雷霆之鼓，神馳於宇宙，安能掩翳聰明、長齋久潔？
- 富貴之家高位厚祿，正為重累，非修仙之資。

**三仙之說：**

仙經所載，仙有三等：
- **天仙**：上士舉形昇虛
- **地仙**：中士游於名山
- **尸解仙**：下士先死後蛻

少君之去，正是尸解：棺中唯衣冠在焉，蓋已蛻形而去矣。
