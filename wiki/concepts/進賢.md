content_legacy: '## English


  **Promoting the Worthy** (進賢) constitutes a central doctrine in Mozi''s political
  philosophy as presented in "Exalting the Worthy (Middle)." The chapter defines the
  mechanism by which a wise ruler cultivates a society where the virtuous predominate
  and the unworthy diminish.


  The process operates through a systematic reward and punishment framework. When
  the sage-king elevates worthy individuals to positions of authority—making them
  wealthy and honored as officials—while demoting the unworthy to menial service,
  the result is that "the people are all encouraged by the prospect of reward and
  fear punishment, mutually urging each other to become worthy." Consequently, "the
  worthy become numerous while the unworthy grow few."


  This doctrine stands in stark contrast to contemporary practices (as Mozi critiqued
  them) where rulers appointed favorites based on familial ties or physical appearance
  rather than merit. Such appointments, Mozi argued, would lead to governmental dysfunction:
  officials who lack wisdom cannot govern wisely, and the realm descends into chaos.


  The subsequent passage elaborates that after identifying worthy individuals, the
  sage must "observe their conduct, examine their abilities, and carefully assign
  them to appropriate offices." Those capable of governing the state should govern
  the state; those suited for managing a department should manage a department; those
  fit for governing a city should govern a city. This differentiation ensures that
  all positions are filled by the worthy, establishing the foundation of proper governance.



  ## 日本語


  **進賢**とは、墨子が『尚賢中』で論じた、徳ある者を登用して徳のない者を退けるという政治原則である。


  墨子によれば、治国の本質は「賢者衆しくして不肖者寡な」状態を実現することにある。その方法は明確である。賢者は挙上げて高位に進め、富と名誉を与える。不肖者は廃して賤役に従事させる。


  この仕組みにより、民は獎賞を期待し罰を恐れて、互いに賢者たれと励まし合う。结果として「賢者衆しくして不肖者寡なし」となる。


  ただし、墨子は単に昇進させるだけでは不十分だと強調する。聖人は「其の言を聞き、其の行を迹り、其の能を察して官を慎む」必要がある。各人に応じた役職を与えることで、国家は正常に運営される。これは墨子の功利的政治哲学の核心をなす。'

**進賢**者，墨子尚賢說之要旨也。見於《墨子·尚賢中》。

墨子以為，治國之道，在於使賢者衆而不肖者寡。其法奈何？曰：「賢者舉而上之，富而貴之，以為官長；不肖者抑而廢之，貧而賤之，以為徒役。」

如此則民皆勸賞畏罰，相率而為賢。是以賢者衆而不肖者寡，此之謂進賢。

然進賢非徒舉之而已。墨子續云：「然後聖人聽其言，迹其行，察其所能而愼予官，此謂事能。」言聖人既得賢人，當察其言行，考其才能，而謹慎賦予官職。

使治國者治國，使長官者長官，使治邑者治邑，凡所使治國家、官府、邑里者，皆國之賢者也。

三代聖王堯舜禹湯文武之所以王天下、正諸侯者，正以此也。反是者，則賞不當賢、罰不當暴，為賢者不勸、為暴者不沮，國家亂亡無日矣。
