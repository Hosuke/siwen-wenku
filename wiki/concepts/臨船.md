## English

Línchuán (臨船) literally means "commanding boat" or "attack boat." In the Mozi's Water Defense chapter, it refers to a tactical unit of ten boats organized for offensive water operations during a siege.

Each 臨 consisted of ten boats carrying thirty skilled warriors, organized as follows: twenty soldiers skilled in crossbows (弩) and various weapons, and ten soldiers specializing in agricultural tools used as weapons (苗). The entire unit was coordinated as "並船以為十臨," meaning the boats were joined together to form these attack formations.

These formations were used offensively: when defenders sallied forth to breach enemy dikes upstream, the 臨 formations would carry [[轒轀]] vehicles and deliver soldiers directly to the target. The water itself became a weapon, flooding enemy positions surrounding the city.

The system required careful preparation: elite warriors (材士) were selected and housed with their families, trained in advance, and held accountable through the hostage system described in the text.

## 中文

臨船者攻城水戰之編制也。《墨子·備水》云：「並船以為十臨，臨三十人。」十船相連為一臨，其船三十人：二十人擅弩，十人擅苗。

此制之要義有數端：
一為船連而進，不若單船易散；
二為人多而有分工，弩射與操持各有所司；
三為 [[材士]] 為骨幹，挾 [[轒轀]] 以渡水。

視水可決之時，以臨船載兵出城，決外堤以灌敵圍師。城上 [[射檥]] 之兵佐之，矢石交加。此水攻之法，亦墨子「非攻」之義——以守為攻，使敵知難而退也。

《六韜》有「三軍渡水」之述，與此 [[備水]] 之法相參，可見先秦水戰編制之大略。

## 日本語

臨船は、攻城用水上作戦における戦術単位である。十艘の船を一隊とし、三十名の兵員が搭乗する。《墨子・備水》の記述によれば、弩の射手二十名と農器使い十名で編成され、 [[轒轀]] を載せて敵の堤防を突破し、水攻めを行う，城からの反撃手段として機能した。
