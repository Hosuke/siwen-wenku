content_legacy: '## English


  The concept "淳樸散而禮樂飾" articulates a fundamental Daoist critique of civilization,
  describing how humanity''s natural simplicity became scattered as artificial constructs
  of ritual, music, and moral systems emerged.


  In 《文子·卷十二》, this phrase appears in a passage discussing the decline of the golden
  age: "性失然後貴仁義，仁義立而道德廢，純樸散而禮樂飾，是非形而百姓眩，珠玉貴而天下爭。" When human nature was lost, benevolence
  and righteousness were esteemed; when benevolence and righteousness were established,
  the Way and virtue were abandoned; when simplicity scattered, ritual and music became
  ornamented; when right and wrong took form, the common people were confused; when
  pearls and jade were valued, the world competed.


  This represents a cyclical historical vision where:

  1. **Ancient simplicity (淳樸)**: In the beginning, humans lived in natural harmony
  with the Dao, possessing innate virtue without conscious effort

  2. **The emergence of morality**: As natural virtue declined, artificial moral categories
  (仁義) were created to regulate behavior

  3. **The proliferation of ritual**: When even morality failed, elaborate ritual
  systems (禮樂) emerged to control people externally

  4. **Social chaos**: Despite (or because of) these developments, conflicts over
  values, goods, and status emerged


  The Daoist position is not that ritual and music are inherently bad, but that they
  represent second-best solutions to the loss of original nature. When people no longer
  act naturally rightly, we must constrain them with external forms. The ideal remains
  the restoration of natural goodness rather than the proliferation of controls.

「淳樸散而禮樂飾」乃《文子·卷十二》所載之道家批判文明之要義，描述人類從自然純樸之世，墮入禮樂人為之世之歷程。

原文云：「性失然後貴仁義，仁義立而道德廢，純樸散而禮樂飾，是非形而百姓眩，珠玉貴而天下爭。」此言揭示文明發展之悖論：人為道德之興起，恰因自然本性之喪失；而禮樂之興盛，正是淳樸散亡之徵兆。

此說可分四層理解：

其一為上古淳樸之世。那時之人，呼吸陰陽，與道為一，莫不仰其德以和順，領理隱密，自成純樸，無需外力之約束，亦無仁義之名目。此乃道家理想之至德之世。

其二為道德衰微之始。及至衰世，嗜欲達於物，聰明誘於外，性命失其真，人不復能自然為善，於是聖人立仁義之名目，以獎善罰惡，此乃不得已而為之者也。

其三為禮樂修飾之世。仁義之道不行，姦偽萌生，於是聖人制禮作樂，琢飾詩書，以端風俗。然禮樂愈繁，淳樸愈遠，人之真情愈失。

其四為天下爭亂之世。珠玉貴而天下爭，智巧萌生而狙學以擬聖，禮義修而賢者隱處，是非眩而百姓惑。此非治世之象，乃衰世之徵也。

道家之意，非謂廢禮樂也，乃欲人知禮樂之所由生，當反本溯源，脩復性情，使民復歸於淳樸。若徒恃禮樂以為治，是猶揚湯止沸，不去其本也。
