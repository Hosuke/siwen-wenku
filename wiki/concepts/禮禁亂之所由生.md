  The *Book of Rites* develops a sophisticated theory of ritual''s preventive function
  in social governance. The text employs a striking hydraulic metaphor: **"夫禮，禁亂之所由生，猶坊止水之所自來也。"**
  Ritual prevents the very origins of chaos, just as a dam blocks the source of flooding.
  The logic is prospective rather than merely prohibitive—ritual addresses root causes
  before disorder manifests.


  The text illustrates this with concrete examples: if marriage rites (*昏姻之禮*) are
  abandoned, the marital relationship suffers and淫亂 proliferates; if village drinking
  ceremonies (*鄉飲酒之禮*) are neglected, the order of age hierarchy collapses and conflicts
  multiply; if mourning and sacrifice rites (*喪祭之禮*) are discarded, filial gratitude
  fades and disloyalty spreads; if diplomatic ceremonies (*聘覲之禮*) are abolished, the
  distinction between ruler and minister blurs and rebellious invasions arise.


  The conclusion is clear: destroying old dykes leads inevitably to flooding; abandoning
  old ritual inevitably produces chaos. This forward-looking theory of ritual as preventive
  medicine for society stands in contrast to purely punitive approaches to governance.

《禮記·經解》闡發禮之預防功能，創用水利之喻。孔子曰：「夫禮，禁亂之所由生，猶坊止水之所自來也。」禮之於亂，非待其已熾而後禁之，乃從源頭防遏之。此說明禮之治效在乎事先，而非只在事後補救。

其論以實例為證：昏姻之禮廢，則夫婦之道苦而淫辟之罪多；鄉飲酒之禮廢，則長幼之序失而爭闘之獄繁；喪祭之禮廢，則臣子之恩薄而倍死忘生者眾；聘覲之禮廢，則君臣之位失而倍畔侵陵之敗起。禮之存廢，與社會治亂之間，有必然之因果關聯。

故以舊坊為無所用而壞之者，必有水敗；以舊禮為無所用而去之者，必有亂患。禮之用廣矣，不止於繁文縟節，乃所以安上治民之本。此儒家禮治思想之精義，迥異於專恃刑罰之治道。
