## English

The "Sixfold Cultivation" (心小志大智圓行方能多事少) is a comprehensive ethical framework articulated in the *Wenzi*, synthesizing the paradoxes necessary for sagehood. Each of the six attributes contains a seemingly contradictory pair, revealing the Daoist insight that true excellence emerges from balancing opposites.

**心欲小** (Small Desire): The cultivated person exercises restraint over their smallest impulses and precautionary caution against the minutest dangers. They dare not indulge their desires even in细微之处. This echoes the Daoist emphasis on minimizing desires, comparable to the Confucian concept of [[修身]] but with distinctively Daoist inflections toward wu-wei (non-action).
