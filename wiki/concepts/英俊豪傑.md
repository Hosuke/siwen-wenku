content_legacy: '## English


  The concept of **英俊豪傑** represents a fourfold classification of talented individuals
  found in Wenzi''s philosophy of governance. This hierarchical framework assigns
  different virtues and capabilities to each grade, establishing a principle of matching
  talent to position.


  **Heroes (英)** possess wisdom surpassing ten thousand people. They understand the
  patterns of heaven and earth and comprehend the principles of human emotion. Their
  capacity for tolerance enables them to accommodate the multitude, their kindness
  allows them to embrace those at a distance, and their wisdom permits them to understand
  situational adaptation. These are the individuals fit to lead nations.


  **Talented Persons (俊)** possess virtue sufficient for teaching and transformation,
  conduct sufficient to hide deeper meanings, trustworthiness sufficient to win followers,
  and clarity sufficient to illuminate subordinates. They represent the backbone of
  administrative competence.


  **Outstanding Persons (傑)** demonstrate conduct that serves as a model, wisdom sufficient
  to resolve doubts and confusion, trustworthiness sufficient to maintain agreements,
  and integrity sufficient to handle shared finances. They execute matters according
  to principle and speak in accordance with the Way.


  **Great Persons (豪)** maintain their duties without neglect, uphold righteousness
  without partiality, refuse to shirk from difficulties, and do not pursue profit
  through inappropriate means. They embody steadfast integrity in the face of adversity.


  The text emphasizes that these four types should each occupy positions appropriate
  to their capacities, proceeding from the root to the branches, using the heavy to
  control the light. When the worthy govern the unworthy, order prevails; when the
  unworthy govern the worthy, chaos results—the natural principle of heaven and earth
  cannot be avoided.


  The sage-kings advance the worthy to establish merit, while unworthy rulers elevate
  those who share their flatteries. By observing what a ruler elevates, one can distinguish
  order from disorder and evaluate whether the ruler is worthy or unworthy.

「英俊豪傑」出自文子，乃治國用人之四等人材論。

**英**者，智過萬人，謂之英。明於天地之道，通於人情之理，大足以容眾，惠足以懷遠，智足以知權。此人中之英豪也。

**俊**者，千人者謂之俊。德足以教化，行足以隱義，信足以得眾，明足以照下。此治世之能臣也。

**傑**者，百人者謂之傑。行可以為儀表，智足以決嫌疑，信可以守約，廉可以使分財，作事可法，出言可道。此一方之表率也。

**豪**者，十人者謂之豪。守職不廢，處義不比，見難不苟免，見利不苟得。此特立獨行之士也。

文子強調：「英俊豪傑，各以大小之材處其位，由本流末，以重制輕，上唱下和，四海之內，一心同歸。」

用人之要，在於使賢者居上、不肖者居下。若使不肖臨賢，雖嚴刑不能禁其姦，小不能制大，弱不能使強，此天地之性也。
