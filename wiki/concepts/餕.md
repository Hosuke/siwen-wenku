## English

The text dedicates substantial attention to the practice of *jun* (餕), the ritual distribution of sacrificial remnants after the main ceremony has concluded. The passage opens with a reflection on the importance of this seemingly minor conclusion: "Sacrifice has its conclusion, and the餕 is its final stage—one must understand this."

The significance of the餕 lies in what it reveals about governance. As the text states through the voice of ancient worthies: "A good ending is like a good beginning" (善終者如始), and "the尸 also餕s the remnants of the spirits—this is a policy of kindness, from which one may observe governance." The ritual sharing of sacrificial remains demonstrates how governmental virtue flows downward through society.

The hierarchy of distribution follows strict social gradations:

- When the尸 (representative of the deceased) rises, the ruler and four ministers餕 first.
- When the ruler rises, six great officers餕 next.
- When the great officers rise, eight scholars餕.
- When the scholars rise, all officials receive portions.

Each transition follows the principle: "Each change by number is to distinguish the ranks of noble and base and to create an image of extending kindness." This systematic distribution demonstrates the Confucian ideal that governmental blessings should reach all levels of society proportionally.

The text emphasizes that when those above share with those below, the entire realm experiences the ruler's benevolent intention. "When there is a great marsh above, kindness will surely reach those below" (上有大澤，則惠必及下). Just as water flows naturally downhill, virtue should flow from the ruler through each successive level to the common people.

## 中文

《祭統》詳論「餕」禮，此乃祭祀末節分享祭餘之儀式，其義深遠，不可不知。

古語云：「善終者如始。」餕之義，在於善終。祭祀之主體雖已完成，而其惠澤當流布無盡，方為完善。

餕之分施，有嚴格之次第：

- 尸謖，則君與卿四人餕。
- 君起，則大夫六人餕。
- 大夫起，則士八人餕。
- 士起，百官進徹之。

每變以眾，所以別貴賤之等，興施惠之象。此與 [[推恩]] 之義相合，蓋恩義當由上及下，層層流布，不可壅閼。

「廟中者，竟內之象也。」宗廟之中，儼然一小型國家。餕禮所見之次第，正是政治秩序之縮影。 [[惠下之道]] 由此可見——在上者有餘，當與在下者共之，不可獨享。

《坊記》有言：「君子貴人而賤己，先人而後己。」餕禮正是此義之實踐。君享其份，大夫、士、百官各安其分，而下之民亦蒙其惠。此儒家理想的治道——恩澤下流，無有凍餒之民。

## 日本語

餕とは、祭祀の主要儀式が終わった後、供物の残り물을順に分配する儀式である。『祭統』はこれを「祭祀の最後の段階」と呼び、その意義を重視する。

重要なのは、分配に厳密な順序があることである。尸が起身하면国君と卿大夫がこれに続き、国君が起身하면大夫が、大夫が起身하면士が、最終的に百官に分配される。この段階的な分配により、上下の区別が示されながら同時に慈悲の精神も広められる。

「上に大沢有れば、則ち惠必ず下に及ぶ」という言葉が示すように、統治者の徳目は自然に民衆に流れなければならない。餕礼はちょうどその原理を実行に移すものであり、統治と社会の秩序についての儒教の理想を映し出している。
