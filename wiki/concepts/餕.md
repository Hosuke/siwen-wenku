content_legacy: '## English


  The text dedicates substantial attention to the practice of *jun* (餕), the ritual
  distribution of sacrificial remnants after the main ceremony has concluded. The
  passage opens with a reflection on the importance of this seemingly minor conclusion:
  "Sacrifice has its conclusion, and the餕 is its final stage—one must understand this."


  The significance of the餕 lies in what it reveals about governance. As the text states
  through the voice of ancient worthies: "A good ending is like a good beginning"
  (善終者如始), and "the尸 also餕s the remnants of the spirits—this is a policy of kindness,
  from which one may observe governance." The ritual sharing of sacrificial remains
  demonstrates how governmental virtue flows downward through society.


  The hierarchy of distribution follows strict social gradations:


  - When the尸 (representative of the deceased) rises, the ruler and four ministers餕
  first.

  - When the ruler rises, six great officers餕 next.

  - When the great officers rise, eight scholars餕.

  - When the scholars rise, all officials receive portions.


  Each transition follows the principle: "Each change by number is to distinguish
  the ranks of noble and base and to create an image of extending kindness." This
  systematic distribution demonstrates the Confucian ideal that governmental blessings
  should reach all levels of society proportionally.


  The text emphasizes that when those above share with those below, the entire realm
  experiences the ruler''s benevolent intention. "When there is a great marsh above,
  kindness will surely reach those below" (上有大澤，則惠必及下). Just as water flows naturally
  downhill, virtue should flow from the ruler through each successive level to the
  common people.

《祭統》詳論「餕」禮，此乃祭祀末節分享祭餘之儀式，其義深遠，不可不知。

古語云：「善終者如始。」餕之義，在於善終。祭祀之主體雖已完成，而其惠澤當流布無盡，方為完善。

餕之分施，有嚴格之次第：

- 尸謖，則君與卿四人餕。
- 君起，則大夫六人餕。
- 大夫起，則士八人餕。
- 士起，百官進徹之。

每變以眾，所以別貴賤之等，興施惠之象。此與 [[推恩]] 之義相合，蓋恩義當由上及下，層層流布，不可壅閼。

「廟中者，竟內之象也。」宗廟之中，儼然一小型國家。餕禮所見之次第，正是政治秩序之縮影。 [[惠下之道]] 由此可見——在上者有餘，當與在下者共之，不可獨享。

《坊記》有言：「君子貴人而賤己，先人而後己。」餕禮正是此義之實踐。君享其份，大夫、士、百官各安其分，而下之民亦蒙其惠。此儒家理想的治道——恩澤下流，無有凍餒之民。

馂者，祭畢均食祭餘之禮也。利成送尸後，祝命佐食徹肵俎降設于堂下阼階南，司宮設對食，乃四人共馂。上佐食盥升，下佐食對之，賓長二人備。司士進敦黍於二佐食，資黍於羊俎兩端。司士辯舉，馂者皆祭黍、祭舉。主人西面三拜馂者，馂者奠舉於俎皆答拜。乃皆食食舉，卒食。主 人洗爵酌以授上馂，贊者洗三爵以授次馂，皆不拜受。主人三拜，馂者奠爵祭酒卒爵。上馂親嘏曰：「主人受祭之福，胡壽保建家室。」主人興，坐奠爵拜，上馂答拜而興出，主人送乃退。

古者祭祀，餘胙不敢以味忘祖，故立馂禮以均惠。《禮記·禮器》曰「宗廟之祭，貴者獻以爵，賤者酬以觶」，此之遺意歟。
