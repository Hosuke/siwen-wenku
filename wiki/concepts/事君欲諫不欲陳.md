## English

**"In serving the ruler, one should remonstrate but not publicize"** (事君欲諫不欲陳) represents the Confucian ideal of ministerial conduct. According to the *Record of Standards*, proper service to authority requires discretion, humility, and a commitment to truth over self-promotion.

The text establishes fundamental principles of service:

**Initiating affairs through words:** "When serving the ruler, first establish your position through your words; when presenting yourself, complete your trustworthiness." The minister receives responsibility based on the credibility established through words spoken prior to appointment.

**Manner of remonstrance:** Confucius condemns both excessive caution and excessive boldness in approaching the ruler:
- "If one is distant yet remonstrates, this is flattery."
- "If one is near yet does not remonstrate, this is opportunism."

**The proper approach:** "The near ministers preserve harmony; the chief officials direct the various ministries; the great ministers consider the four quarters."

**On advancement and retreat:** "In serving the ruler, it is difficult to advance yet easy to retreat — thus the position remains orderly. If easy to advance yet difficult to retreat, chaos ensues. Therefore the exemplary person advances three times after bowing and retreats once, thereby avoiding disorder."

**Ultimate loyalty:** "In serving the ruler, one may be honored or humiliated, enriched or impoverished, given life or killed — but one must not cause disorder." This demonstrates that loyalty transcends personal fortune, subordinating all self-interest to the preservation of proper governance.

The *Book of Songs* (小雅) captures this: "I hold you in my heart — why not speak of it? Hidden in the heart's core, not a single day forgets."

## 中文

**「事君欲諫不欲陳」** 見於《禮記·表記》，為臣道之要旨。夫子論事君之道，要在直諫而在勿矜。

其言曰：「事君先資其言，拜自獻其身，以成其信。是故君有責於其臣，臣有死於其言。」蓋臣之事君，以言為階，故不可妄言，亦不可失言。言既出於口，身即隨之，當以死守之。

又論進諫之方：「事君遠而諫，則諂也；近而不諫，則屍利也。」疏遠之臣而強言，是諂媚也；親近之臣而不言，是尸位素餐也。二者皆非忠道。

故夫子曰：「邇臣守和，宰正百官，大臣慮四方。」各安其分，各盡其職，此君臣之義也。

又論進退之節：「事君難進而易退，則位有序；易進而難退，則亂也。故君子三揖而進，一辭而退，以遠亂也。」蓋進退有節，則朝廷不亂；躁進貪祿，則國事日非。

夫子又言：「事君慎始而敬終。」「事君可貴可賤，可富可貧，可生可殺，而不可使為亂。」此所以明忠之至也。富貴不能淫，貧賤不能移，威武不能屈，此之謂真臣。

《詩》雲：「心乎愛矣，瑕不謂矣；中心藏之，何日忘之。」此言忠臣愛君，藏之於心，不必形之於口，亦不當表之以求譽也。
