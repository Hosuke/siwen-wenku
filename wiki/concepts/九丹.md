content_legacy: '## English


  The Nine Elixirs (九丹) represent a hierarchical system of alchemical preparations
  for immortality described in Ge Hong''s *Baopuzi Neipian* (抱朴子內篇), specifically
  in the chapter "Using the Golden Elixir" (金丹). This system progresses from the first
  and most basic elixir (丹華) to the ninth and most supreme (寒丹), each offering progressively
  faster paths to immortality.


  The first elixir,丹華 (Elixir of Splendor), requires the preparation of a "six-one
  mud" (六一泥) using various minerals and calcining for thirty-six days. Consumption
  leads to immortality within seven days. The second elixir, 神符 (Divine Talisman),
  grants immortality in one hundred days and enables the practitioner to walk on water.
  The third provides protection from the five weapons of warfare. The fourth, 還丹 (Return
  Elixir), transforms mercury into gold upon contact, representing the principle of
  cyclical return. The fifth through ninth elixirs progressively reduce the time required—from
  thirty days to as little as three days—until the practitioner achieves the ability
  to fly without wings.


  Crucially, the text states that obtaining merely one of these nine elixirs suffices
  for immortality; one need not prepare all nine. The practitioner may ascend to heaven
  or remain among humans at will, and the elixirs grant the ability to pass through
  obstacles unharmed.

九丹者，抱朴子所言神仙長生之要方，分為九品，自丹華至寒丹，次第昇進，功用遞增。抱朴子詳述其煉製之法、服食之效，以明金丹之道為仙道之極。

第一丹名丹華，先作玄黃，用雄黃水、礬石水、戎鹽、礜石、牡蠣、赤石脂、滑石、胡粉諸物，以六一泥封之，煆煉三十六日而成。服之七日即可成仙。第二丹名神符，又稱神丹，服之百日仙，行度水火，以丹塗足下可步行水上，服三刀圭則三屍九蟲皆消，百病皆愈。第三丹功能辟五兵，仙人玉女皆來侍奉。第四丹名還丹，服之百日仙，朱鳥鳳凰翔覆其上，以一刀圭合水銀一斤火之，立成黃金，以此丹書凡人目上，百鬼走避。第五丹名餌丹，服之三十日仙；第六丹名煉丹，服之十日仙；第七丹名柔丹，服之一刀圭百日仙；第八丹名伏丹，服之即日仙；第九丹名寒丹，服之一刀圭百日仙，仙童仙女來侍，飛行輕舉，不用羽翼。

經云：「凡此九丹，但得一丹便仙，不在悉作之。」蓋九丹雖有高下之分，然皆能令人不老不死，超脫塵寰。服之欲昇天則去，欲且止人間亦任意，皆能出入無間，不可得之害矣。
