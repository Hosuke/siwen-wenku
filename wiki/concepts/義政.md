content_legacy: '## English


  **義政** (Righteous Governance) represents the political ideal in Mozi''s philosophy,
  characterized by alignment with Heaven''s will (天志) and the practice of **兼愛** (universal
  love). This governance model stands in direct opposition to **力政** (force governance),
  which Mozi condemns as违背天意 and destructive to human welfare.


  Righteous governance manifests through several concrete policies: large nations
  should not attack small ones; powerful families should not usurp weaker ones; the
  strong should not oppress the weak; the noble should not despise the humble; the
  cunning should not deceive the simple. These five principles embody the essence
  of universal love applied to political relations.


  Mozi argues that such governance benefits all three spheres simultaneously—above
  it pleases Heaven (上利於天), in the middle it satisfies the spirits (中利於鬼), and below
  it benefits the people (下利於人). When all three spheres receive benefit (三利無所不利),
  the ruler achieves universal praise and is honored as a **聖王** (sage-king).


  The philosophical foundation of義政 lies in Mozi''s doctrine of 天志. Since Heaven desires义
  and despises不义, and since the ruler who practices義政 receives Heaven''s blessing,
  political conduct becomes inseparable from cosmic moral order. The ruler who truly
  understands this relationship will govern not merely by human power but by aligning
  with Heaven''s purposeful will.


  Historical examples illustrate this distinction. The sage kings Yu, Tang, Wen, and
  Wu practiced義政 and received Heaven''s rewards—wealth, honor, and the eternal reputation
  of sage kingship. The tyrant kings Jie, Zhou, You, and Li practiced力政 and suffered
  Heaven''s punishments—destruction of their dynasties and eternal infamy as violent
  rulers.

**義政**者，墨子所標舉之政治理想也。順天意而行，以兼愛為本，與力政相對。

義政之要旨有五：處大國不攻小國，處大家不篡小家，强者不劫弱，貴者不傲賤，多詐者不欺愚。此五者，皆兼愛之道施之於政治者也。

義政之效，上利於天，中利於鬼，下利於人。三利無所不利，故舉天下美名加之，謂之聖王。反是而為力政者，則三不利無所利，舉天下惡名加之，謂之暴王。

義政之根柢，在於天志之說。天欲義而惡不義，行義政者得賞，行不義者得罰，此自然之理也。故聖王之治，非徒恃人力，必順天意而行。

觀乎自古聖王，禹湯文武是也。其事上尊天，中事鬼神，下愛人。天意曰：「此之我所愛，兼而愛之；我所利，兼而利之。」故使貴為天子，富有天下，業萬世子孫，傳稱其善，方施天下，至今稱之，謂之聖王。
