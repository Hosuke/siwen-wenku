content_legacy: '## English


  **Returning to Origins** (報本反始) constitutes one of the most essential principles
  of Chinese sacrificial ritual, as articulated in the "Record of Rites" chapter "Jiao
  Te Sheng" (郊特牲). The phrase encapsulates the dual movement of报答 (reciprocating benefits
  received) and returning to the原始 (original beginning).


  The text explicitly states: "萬物本乎天，人本乎祖，此所以配上帝也。郊之祭也，大報本反始也" — "All things originate
  from Heaven, and human beings originate from their ancestors. This is why they are
  paired with the Supreme Ruler. The suburban sacrifice greatly repays the source
  and returns to origins."


  This principle operates on multiple levels:


  **The Cosmic Level**: Just as all things emerge from Heaven (天), human beings must
  acknowledge their ultimate origin in the cosmos. The suburban sacrifice to Heaven
  (郊祭) at the winter solstice represents the greatest expression of this cosmic acknowledgment.


  **The Ancestral Level**: Human beings originate from their ancestors, making sacrificial
  offerings to deceased forebears an essential expression of filial gratitude and
  recognition of one''s source of existence.


  **The Agricultural Level**: The seasonal cycle of planting and harvesting, combined
  with year-end sacrifices, demonstrates how the principle of 報本反始 extends to gratitude
  for the earth''s bounty and the spirits of grain.


  The "Jiao Te Sheng" chapter further develops this theme through the communal sacrifice
  at the earth altar (社): "唯為社事，單出里。唯為社田，國人畢作。唯社，丘乘共粢盛，所以報本反始也" — "For the earth altar
  affairs, the entire village emerges. For the earth altar field, all the country''s
  people labor. For the earth altar, the hamlets jointly provide the ceremonial grain.
  This is to repay the source and return to beginnings."


  The principle connects to the broader ritual concept that proper sacrifice must
  flow from genuine feeling (誠) rather than mere external performance. One cannot
  truly "return to origins" through perfunctory ceremony; the inner disposition must
  align with the outward form.

**報本反始**者，《禮記·郊特牲》所論祭祀之大義，謂答報根本、返於初始之理也。

《郊特牲》明言：「萬物本乎天，人本乎祖，此所以配上帝也。郊之祭也，大報本反始也。」

此理有三層：

**宇宙之層次**：萬物本乎天，故郊天之祭，乃所以報宇宙生成之本。冬至之郊，陽氣始生，萬物復始，天子以盛禮祀天，以表對宇宙本原之敬意。

**祖宗之層次**：人本乎祖，故祭祀祖先，乃所以報祖宗生育之恩。慎終追遠，民德歸厚，此祭祀之本義也。

**農事之層次**：《郊特牲》又載：「唯為社事，單出里。唯為社田，國人畢作。唯社，丘乘共粢盛，所以報本反始也。」社祭土而主陰氣，報土地之功；田事既畢，共粢盛以祭，報本返始之義備矣。

他又云：「季春出火，為焚也。然後簡其車賦，而歷其卒伍，而君親誓社，以習軍旅。」此雖言田獵教戰，然其本亦在報本返始——以春田之獲，報土地養育之恩也。

「反」者，返也，歸也；「始」者，原始也，根本也。祭祀之道，非徒具文，必也誠於中而形於外，方為真實之報本返始。
