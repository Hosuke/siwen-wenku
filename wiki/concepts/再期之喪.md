content_legacy: '## English


  The *Book of Rites* establishes a comprehensive system for categorizing mourning
  periods based on the Three Seasons framework (三時). This system provides clear guidelines
  for the duration of mourning obligations, distinguishing between periods of varying
  length and intensity.


  The text states: "再期之喪，三年也；期之喪，二年也；九月七月之喪，三時也；五月之喪，二時也；三月之喪，一時也。" This creates a
  hierarchical structure: two years ( 再期) corresponds to the full three-year mourning;
  one year corresponds to two years of actual observance; nine or seven months equals
  three seasons; five months equals two seasons; and three months equals one season.


  The phrase "故期而祭，禮也；期而除喪，道也。祭不為除喪也" is particularly significant. It establishes
  that celebrating the seasonal sacrifice at the end of the mourning period is proper
  ritual, and removing mourning garments at the proper time is the proper way. Crucially,
  the sacrifice is not performed merely to end the mourning — it has its own independent
  ritual significance.


  The text also addresses exceptional circumstances: "三年而後葬者必再祭，其祭之間不同時而除喪" — those
  who bury after three years must perform the sacrifice twice, and the sacrifices
  and the removal of mourning must not occur simultaneously. This reveals the careful
  attention to ritual precision in classical Chinese ceremonial practice.

《禮記·喪服小記》詳論喪期之制度，以三時為綱，分別隆殺。其文云：「再期之喪，三年也；期之喪，二年也；九月七月之喪，三時也；五月之喪，二時也；三月之喪，一時也。」

期者，周年也。再期者，再周而三年也。期之喪降於再期一等，而其實止二年，蓋期而除服，期而再免也。三時者，三季也，即三九月也。二時者，兩季也，即六七月也。一時者，一季也，即三四月也。

「故期而祭，禮也；期而除喪，道也。祭不為除喪也」此句最為吃緊。期而祭者，祥祭也；期而除喪者，祥而釋縗麻也。然祭祀之設，非為除喪而設，除喪自有其時，不可混為一途也。此明喪服之禮與祭祀之禮各有所用，不可以彼代此。

「三年而後葬者必再祭」者，明葬之過期者，其除喪亦必有道也。葬既逾期，則祥祭不可以一祭畢之，必再行祭事而後可以除喪。其間不可以同時，亦可見古禮之精密矣。
