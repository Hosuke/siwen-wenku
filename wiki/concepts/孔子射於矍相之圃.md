content_legacy: '## English


  The story of **Confucius''s Archery at Juexiang Garden** (孔子射於矍相之圃) is a celebrated
  episode from *The Meaning of Archery* (射義) in the *Book of Rites*. It illustrates
  how archery served as a means of moral selection and character assessment.


  According to the text, Confucius held an archery demonstration in the garden of
  Juexiang (矍相). The spectators crowded around like a wall (觀者如堵牆), creating an atmosphere
  of public scrutiny. When the archery reached the point requiring a marshal (司馬),
  Confucius had his disciple Zilu (子路) hold a bow and arrows to invite participants,
  proclaiming:


  "Those who were defeated generals of armies (賁軍之將), officials who brought ruin to
  their states (亡國之大夫), and those who have become heirs to others (與為人後者) may not
  enter. All others may enter."


  As a result, half the audience left (蓋去者半), and half remained (入者半).


  Confucius then had Gongwang Zhiqiu (公罔之裘) and Xu Dian (序點) address the gathered
  participants with wine. Gongwang Zhiqiu declared that those who had been dutiful
  in youth and middle age (幼壯孝弟), respected propriety in old age (耆耋好禮), did not follow
  vulgar customs (不從流俗), and were willing to wait for death in dignity (身以俟死) should
  take their positions. Again, half departed and half stayed.


  Xu Dian further announced that those who loved learning without fatigue (好學不倦),
  were unwavering in their commitment to ritual (好禮不變), and maintained clear purpose
  in advanced age (旄期稱道不亂) were welcome. In the end, only a few remained (蓋㢙有存者).


  This episode demonstrates that the archery ground (射宮) served as a moral testing
  ground, where not only technical skill but also virtue determined one''s right to
  participate in the ritual.

「孔子射於矍相之圃」者，見載於《禮記·射義》，為[[孔子]]以射選士之著名事跡也。

《射義》載，孔子設射於矍相之圃，觀者如堵牆，場面之盛可想。射至於[[司馬]]之官，孔子使子路執弓矢出延射，而宣言曰：

「賁軍之將、亡國之大夫、與為人後者不入，其餘皆入。」

此言一出，蓋去者半，入者半。孔子以此淘汰品行有虧之人，與[[仁者如射|射者仁之道]]相合，射非僅較力藝，乃所以觀德行也。

既而孔子又使公罔之裘、序點揚觶而語，序點言曰：「幼壯孝弟，耆耋好禮，不從流俗，身以俟死者，不在此位也。」蓋去者半，處者半。

序點又語曰：「好學不倦，好禮不變，旄期稱道不亂者，不在此位也。」蓋㢙有存者。

由此可見，[[射侯|射宮]]非徒較射之所，乃道德考驗之場。射禮之設，所以別賢不肖[[身可危而志不可奪|觀人觀志]]。[[孔門四科]]以德行居首，於此射事可見一斑。此與[[聞善相告見善相示]]之義相通，皆儒家「[[成人之道]]」之所寄也。
