content_legacy: '## English


  **Following Heaven as the Standard (法天)** is the central normative solution proposed
  in the Mozi chapter "Fa Yi." After systematically rejecting parents, teachers, and
  rulers as proper standards because they often lack benevolence, Mozi concludes:
  "然則奚以為治法而可？故曰：莫若法天" (What then can be the standard for governance? The answer is:
  nothing surpasses following Heaven).


  Mozi describes Heaven''s attributes as the basis for this claim. Heaven''s Way (天之行)
  is broad and impartial (廣而無私), its beneficence is profound without claiming virtue
  (施厚而不德), and its brightness endures without decline (其明久而不衰). These three qualities—impartiality,
  selflessness, and eternal clarity—distinguish Heaven from flawed human authorities.
  The sage-kings took Heaven as their model (聖王法之), and Mozi urges rulers to measure
  their actions against Heaven''s standard: "動作有為，必度於天，天之所欲則為之，天所不欲則止" (In every action
  and undertaking, one must measure against Heaven; what Heaven desires, do; what
  Heaven does not desire, cease).


  This concept forms a crucial link between Mozi''s political philosophy and his religious
  thought. The Will of Heaven (天志) operates as both a moral standard and a cosmic
  force that rewards the virtuous and punishes the wicked. The concept is closely
  related to [[天志]] in the Mozi canon.

「法天」者，以天為法度之謂也。墨子既破父母、學、君三者不可為法，乃提出終極解決之道：「莫若法天。」

墨子何以尊天？蓋天有三重德性：

一曰「行廣而無私」——天之道，廣大普遍，無有偏私。凡日月之照，風雨之施，雨露之潤，無問華夷，不別親疏。此所以異於父母之私愛、君主之偏袒也。

二曰「施厚而不德」——天之恩澤，深厚廣大，然不自以為有德。《老子》雲「生而不有，為而不恃，長而不宰」，正此義也。此所以異於學者之矜己、君主之邀功也。

三曰「明久而不衰」——天之光明，恒久不滅，不似人壽之有終、權位之難保。此所以異於凡人之易朽、君權之更迭也。

有此三德，故聖王法之。墨子更進而言：「動作有為，必度於天，天之所欲則為之，天所不欲則止。」——凡人行事，當以天意為準則。天所欲者為之，天所不欲者止之。此乃「法天」之精義也。

「法天」之說，為墨子「天志」思想之根本，與[[天志]]篇相發明。
