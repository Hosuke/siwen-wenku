## English

**Major Encoffining** (大斂, dà liàn) represents the solemn second stage of preparing the deceased, when the body is placed into the coffin itself. The "Record of Major Mourning" (喪大記) provides elaborate regulations distinguishing the procedures for rulers, ministers, and commoners.

### Location and Procedure

Unlike minor encoffining conducted *hù nèi* (戶內), major encoffining takes place at the eastern steps (*zuò*, 阼). The text states: "小斂於戶內，大斂於阼."
