  **Zilu** (子路) was a disciple of the Confucian sage [[孔子]]. In the account "Confucius
  Archery at the Garden of Juexiang" (孔子射於矍相之圃) from the *Book of Rites: Meaning of
  Archery* (禮記·射義), Zilu was tasked by Confucius with distributing bows and arrows
  to participants and proclaiming the entry qualifications: "None shall enter who
  are generals of doomed armies, ministers of perished states, or those who follow
  in others'' footsteps. All others may enter." This demonstration showed how archery
  rituals served as a means of observing moral character rather than mere physical
  competition.

子路之死見[[子路結纓而死]]。衞蒯聵作亂，孔悝被劫，出公輒奔魯。子路聞亂而入，欲救孔悝，曰：「太子無勇，若燔臺，必舍孔叔。」及門，公孫敢拒之，子路曰：「利其祿，必救其患。」遂入。石乞、盂黶以戈擊之，割其纓。子路曰：「君子死，冠不免。」結纓而死。孔子聞亂，歎曰：「嗟乎！柴也其來乎？由也其死矣。」
