## English

**Zilu** (子路) was a disciple of the Confucian sage [[孔子]]. In the account "Confucius Archery at the Garden of Juexiang" (孔子射於矍相之圃) from the *Book of Rites: Meaning of Archery* (禮記·射義), Zilu was tasked by Confucius with distributing bows and arrows to participants and proclaiming the entry qualifications: "None shall enter who are generals of doomed armies, ministers of perished states, or those who follow in others' footsteps. All others may enter." This demonstration showed how archery rituals served as a means of observing moral character rather than mere physical competition.

## 中文

**子路**，孔子弟子，見於《[[孔子射於矍相之圃]]》所述之射禮。

《禮記·射義》載，孔子於矍相之圃演示射禮，使子路執弓矢以邀賓客，而宣言曰：「賁軍之將、亡國之大夫、求人之跡者不可入，其餘皆可入。」 audience departed while the other half remained. This ritual demonstration showed that archery served as a means to observe moral character rather than mere physical competition. The passage reveals the Confucian approach to evaluating individuals through ceremonial practice.
