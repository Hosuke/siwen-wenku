content_legacy: '## English


  The concept of **精誠** (literally "essence-sincerity" or "refined sincerity") constitutes
  a core teaching in Volume 2 of the *Wenzi*. The text presents it as the essential
  mechanism through which governance achieves its effects:


  > 令雖明不能獨行，必待精誠，故摠道以被民弗從者，精誠弗包也。


  Translation: "Even with clear orders, they cannot be executed alone; they must await
  精誠. Thus, even those who broadly disseminate the Way among the people will not be
  followed if their 精誠 fails to encompass them."


  The *Wenzi* argues that 精誠 is the indispensable condition for effective governance.
  Without inner sincerity, even the most clearly articulated laws and orders fail
  to move the people. The text states that the power to shift customs and transform
  habits cannot come from laws and rewards alone; these require 精誠 as their spiritual
  foundation.


  Furthermore, the doctrine holds that 精誠 has cosmic dimensions. When 精誠 arises within
  the ruler, it activates cosmic forces:


  > 精誠內形，氣動於天，景星見，黃龍下，鳳皇至，醴泉出，嘉穀生，河不滿溢，海不波湧。


  Translation: "When 精誠 takes shape within, the qi stirs Heaven. Then the Gazing Star
  appears, the Yellow Dragon descends, the phoenix arrives, the sweet spring emerges,
  the excellent grain grows, rivers do not overflow, and seas do not surge."


  This passage illustrates the Wenzian concept that the ruler''s inner moral cultivation
  produces visible cosmic responses—a form of cosmological moralism that connects
  human sincerity to natural phenomena.


  The *Wenzi* also connects 精誠 to the concept of 神化 (divine transformation), arguing
  that the "不言之教" (teaching through non-speech) operates precisely through 精誠. The
  sage''s silent influence is effective because it emanates from genuine inner sincerity
  rather than calculated manipulation.

「精誠」為[[文子]]卷二之要義，乃治道生效之關鍵所在。其言：

「令雖明不能獨行，必待精誠，故摠道以被民弗從者，精誠弗包也。」

此言明示：法令雖明，苟無精誠以輔之，終不能行於天下。[[文子]]以為，賞善罰暴，正令也；而其所以能行者，精誠也。令與誠相須而成，缺一不可。

精誠之效，不僅及於人事，更通於天道。[[文子]]曰：

「精誠內形，氣動於天，景星見，黃龍下，鳳皇至，醴泉出，嘉穀生，河不滿溢，海不波湧。」

此言人君心存精誠，則天象為之變異，祥瑞自至。反之，若「逆天暴物」，則有日月薄蝕、五星失行、山崩川涸之災。此[[天人感應]]之說，見於[[文子]]者如此。

精誠與[[不言之教]]相表裡。聖人之所以不言而教，以其精誠發於內，神氣動於天故也。非言說之功，乃感化之力。此為道家治道之要妙。
