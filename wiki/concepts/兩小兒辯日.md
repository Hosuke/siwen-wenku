## English

"The Two Children Debate About the Sun" is a celebrated anecdote from the Liezi's Tang Wen chapter, used to illustrate the limitations of human perception and knowledge.

The story recounts: Confucius was traveling east when he encountered two children engaged in an argument. Upon inquiry, they explained their dispute: one child believed the sun is closer to the earth when it first rises and farther at noon; the other held the opposite view.

The first child argued: "The sun at first rising is as large as a carriage canopy, while at noon it is like a dish or bowl—is this not because what is distant appears small while what is near appears large?"

The second child countered: "The sun at first rising is cold and cool, while at noon it is like reaching into hot water—is this not because what is near feels warm while what is distant feels cool?"

Confucius could not decide between them. The two children laughed: "Who told you that you are so knowledgeable?"

This anecdote has been interpreted as demonstrating that even the wisest have limits to their knowledge. It also illustrates the relativity of perception: the same object can appear different depending on atmospheric conditions, time of day, and the observer's position. The children represent empirical observation while Confucius, unable to adjudicate, represents the limits of rational inquiry when direct measurement is impossible.

Some interpret this as a critique of Confucian emphasis on moral knowledge over physical science; others see it as merely a humorous anecdote about the nature of expertise and humility.

## 中文

《湯問篇》載：「孔子東游，見兩小兒辯鬬，問其故。」

兩兒之辯，一曰日始出時近、日中時遠，一曰日初出遠、日中時近。

第一兒曰：「日初出大如車蓋，及日中則如盤盂，此不為遠者小而近者大乎？」

第二兒曰：「日初出滄滄涼涼，及其日中如探湯，此不為近者熱而遠者涼乎？」

孔子不能決。兩小兒笑曰：「孰為汝多知乎？」

此寓言之意，論者異説。一以為明聖人亦有所不知，故[[非助我者]]之義存焉；一以為諷儒者之蔽，專注人倫而忽於天象。然其要，在使人知認識之有限，不可執一端而自矜也。

夫日出之遠近，非目力可決，須驗之以儀，證之以理。兩兒各執一端，皆有所見，亦皆有所遺。聖人闕疑，不強作答，正是[[知言]]之道。

## 日本語

『湯問篇』にある有名な逸話である。孔子が東遊の間、二子の辯論に遇った。

一子は「日の出の時は人に近く、昼には遠い」とし、一子は「日の出は遠く、昼には近い」とした。

第一の子は「日の出には車蓋の如く大きく、昼には盤盂の如く小さい。是れ遠き者は小さく、近き者は大きいにあらずや」と問い、第二の子は「日の出には滄滄涼たく、昼には探湯の如し。是れ近き者は熱く、遠き者は涼しいにあらずや」と反論した。

孔子は決断できなかった。二子は笑って「孰れか汝に多き智を知らせたるや」と言った。

此の話は聖人にも知の限界があることを示し、あるいは儒者の人倫偏重を諷刺するものとも解される。要は[[非助我者]]の如く、知識の謙抑を教えるにある。
