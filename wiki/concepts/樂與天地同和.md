## English

**「大樂與天地同和，大禮與天地同節」** — "Great music harmonizes with Heaven and Earth; great ritual corresponds with the principles of Heaven and Earth." This proposition from the *Yueji* establishes music as a participation in cosmic order, linking the human art of music to the fundamental harmonies of nature.
