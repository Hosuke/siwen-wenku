  **司馬** (Sima) was one of the six ministers (*六卿*) in the Zhou dynasty, serving as
  the minister of war and the chief director of all military affairs. According to
  the *Zhouli* (《周禮》), the Sima held authority over military matters including the
  organization of troops, the administration of military law, and the final judgment
  in military command.


  In the context of the archery ritual (*射*) described in *The Book of Rites* (《禮記》),
  the Sima was the official to whom the archers were subject. During the ceremony
  at Juexiang Archery Ground (矍相之圃), [[孔子]] conducted his selection of worthy men
  through archery, with the Sima''s officials overseeing the proceedings. The Sima''s
  office thus played a ceremonial role in the moral evaluation of participants, reflecting
  the Zhou belief that martial skills must be coupled with virtue.


  Beyond military command, the Sima also participated in state governance and the
  cultivation of the aristocratic class. The position represented the intersection
  of martial authority and ritual propriety in the Zhou system of *junzi* education.

**司馬**，[[周禮]]六卿之一，掌軍政馬政，為周代最高軍事长官。

《周禮》載，司馬主治軍旅之政，詰誅有罪，誅伐叛逆。其屬有軍司馬、輿司馬、行司馬等，分掌軍中各務。軍法既定，賞罰從之，司馬主之。

[[孔子射於矍相之圃]]之事可見其禮制功能：射至司馬之官，孔子使子路延射，以觀德行。射非徒較力藝，乃所以選士，司馬之官掌其事，亦寓教化之意。

是故司馬之職，非僅征戰，亦涉禮儀，于周代貴族教育中，與[[射侯]]、孝弟、好禮並重焉。

道士司馬郊，一名凝正，一名守中，常遊江上，披冠褐躡屐，日可千里，衣服不改而常新，物觸竟無敢近之者。能詐死，以至青腫臭腐，俄而復活。嘗止池州開元觀，自宣之歙，召店主飲而辱之，店主擊之，忽踏於地，視之己死，體玲色變，一市聚觀。集鄉里縛主人，將送於州。夜半後，聞店中曰已失司馬矣。脩默明日行百里問之，皆曰昨早已過。每往來江上諸州皆然。
