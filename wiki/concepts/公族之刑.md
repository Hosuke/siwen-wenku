content_legacy: '## English


  The concept of "公族之刑" (Criminal Justice for the Ducal Clan) in the *Liji* establishes
  a distinctive legal framework for members of the ruling clan facing capital punishment.
  The text in *Wenwang Shizi* provides detailed procedures that demonstrate both the
  principle of equality before law and the preservation of familial dignity.


  When a member of the ducal clan commits a capital offense, the procedure unfolds
  as follows: "公族其有死罪，則磬于甸人。其刑罪，則纖剸，亦告于甸人。公族無宮刑。" The phrase "公族無宮刑" and "不翦其類也" indicates
  that members of the ruling lineage were exempt from castration, reflecting the principle
  that the ruler should not destroy his own kin.


  The judicial process maintains the dignity of the accused while upholding legal
  procedure: "公族無宮刑，不翦其類也" — the ruler does not cut off his own kind. This demonstrates
  the Confucian principle of maintaining proper relations even in the administration
  of justice.


  For those not yet having established separate households, even when degraded to
  commoner status, they must still follow proper rituals: "五廟之孫，祖廟未毀，雖及庶人，冠、取妻必告，死必赴，不忘親也。"

《禮記·文王世子》詳述公族刑罰之制。公族犯死罪，則「磬于甸人」；刑罪則「纖剸」，亦告于甸人。然公族無宮刑，蓋「不翦其類也」。

其司法之序曰：「獄成，有司讞於公。其死罪，則曰『某之罪在大辟』；其刑罪，則曰『某之罪在小辟』。公曰：『宥之。』有司又曰：『在辟。』公又曰：『宥之。』有司又曰：『在辟。』及三宥，不對，走出，致刑於甸人。」

雖三宥而不可得赦，公使人追之，有司對曰：「無及也！」然公仍素服不舉，為之變，如其倫之喪，無服而親哭之。

此所謂「公族之罪，雖親不以犯有司，正術也，所以體百姓也。」蓋刑於隱者，不與國人慮兄弟也。[[弗吊弗為服]]，[[哭於異姓之廟]]，為忝祖遠之也。
