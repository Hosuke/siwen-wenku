content_legacy: "## English\n\nThe beacon and torch signal system (烽燧制度) described
  in \"Haoling\" represents a sophisticated early Chinese military communications
  network. The system uses visual signals — torches by night, smoke beacons by day
  — to convey detailed information about approaching enemy forces.\n\nThe core mechanism
  involves \"表\" (signal markers) placed at regular intervals along roads leading
  to the city: \"士諜無過十里。居高便所樹表，表三人守之，比至城者三表，與城上烽燧相望，晝則舉烽，夜則舉火\" — scouts place signal
  markers no more than ten li apart, with three persons stationed at each marker,
  positioned so that all three markers can be seen from the city beacon tower. During
  the day, smoke signals are used; at night, fires.\n\nThe five-tier alert system
  is particularly precise: \"望見寇，舉一垂；入竟，舉二垂；狎郭，舉三垂；入郭，舉四垂；狎城，舉五垂。夜以火，皆如此\" — when
  enemies are spotted, raise one flag; when they enter the border, raise two; when
  they approach the outer wall, raise three; when they enter the outer wall, raise
  four; when they approach the inner city, raise five. At night, fire signals are
  used in the same manner.\n\nThis system requires complete environmental control:
  \"去郭百步，墻垣、樹木小大盡伐除之，外空井盡窒之，無可得汲也，外室屋盡發之，木盡伐之\" — within one hundred paces of the
  outer wall, all walls, trees (large and small) must be cleared, open wells must
  be filled, external structures must be demolished, and all wood must be removed.\n\n\n##

《墨子·雜守》篇所載烽燧之法，較他篇為詳。其文曰：

  > 寇烽、驚烽、亂烽，傳火以次應之，至主國止。

  是分烽為三等：寇烽、驚烽、亂烽。敵至則傳火以聞，次第達於主國。

  又有晝夜之別，制度井然：

  > 望見寇，舉一烽；入境，舉二烽；射妻，舉三烽、一鼓；郭會，舉四烽、二鼓；城會，舉五烽、五鼓。夜以火，如此數。

  晝則舉烽，夜則燃火。敵遠見者一烽，近入境者二烽，及射而來者三烽而一鼓，四面皆會者四烽而二鼓，直抵城下者五烽而五鼓。夜中則以火光代之，數亦如之。

  又有急事應對之則：

  > 其事急者，引而上下之。

  烽火之時，上下搖動，以示緊急之勢。
