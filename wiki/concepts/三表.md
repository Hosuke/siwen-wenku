content_legacy: '## English


  The **Three Standards** (三表) constitute Mozi''s systematic methodology for evaluating
  the truth or falsity of any proposition. In "Against Fate Part I," Mozi establishes
  this framework to refute fatalistic claims and demonstrate that human affairs are
  determined by effort, not predetermined destiny.


  The three standards are:


  1. **上本之於古者聖王之事** — "Based above on the deeds of the ancient sage-kings": Proposals
  must accord with historical precedent, particularly the successful governance of
  sage rulers like Tang and Wu Wang.


  2. **下原察百姓耳目之實** — "Examining below what the common people hear and see": Claims
  must be grounded in the direct sensory experience and practical knowledge of ordinary
  people.


  3. **廢以爲刑政，觀其中國家百姓人民之利** — "Putting into practice as policy, and observing whether
  it benefits the state and common people": The ultimate test is practical application
  — does it produce beneficial results for society?


  Mozi applies these standards rigorously to disprove fatalism. He examines historical
  records of sage kings (the first standard), considers the shared experience of the
  people (the second), and evaluates the destructive consequences of believing in
  fate — chaos, poverty, and social disintegration (the third).


  This tripartite method represents Mozi''s attempt to establish objective, verifiable
  criteria for knowledge, distinguishing valid arguments from empty rhetoric. It reflects
  his pragmatic, anti-speculative approach to philosophy.

**三表**，又稱三表法，乃墨子認識論之核心方法，用以鑒別言論是非真偽。《墨子·非命上》云：「言必立儀」，以為言語若無標準，譬如「運鈞之上而立朝夕」，不可得而明知也。

三表之義：

一曰**上本之於古者聖王之事**。凡立言論，必考之古聖先王之行事，以之為本。湯誅桀、武王伐紂，皆因勤政愛民而王天下，非由命定。此表旨在以歷史經驗為論據。

二曰**下原察百姓耳目之實**。言論之是非，當原察百姓耳目之所聞見，以實際經驗為準則。墨子以為，真理非高深莫測，必落實於人心之所共感。

三曰**廢以爲刑政，觀其中國家百姓人民之利**。立說之後，當付諸實行，設為刑政，觀其是否有利於國家百姓。若有害於民，則為非是。

墨子以此三表駁斥命定論：就古聖之事而觀，湯、武未嘗委之於命；就百姓耳目而察，命定之說於民無益；就政令施行而驗，信命則刑政亂、財用匱。是故「執有命者之言不可不非」。

三表之法，體現墨學重實踐、重功效之精神，為先秦哲學中較為系統之認識論架構。
