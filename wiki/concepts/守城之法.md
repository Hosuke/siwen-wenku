  The "Methods of City Defense" (守城之法) excerpted from Mozi''s "Ying Di Ci" represents
  a comprehensive administrative and military system for maintaining a besieged city''s
  functionality and resistance capacity.


  **Organizational Hierarchy**: The text establishes a precise chain of command. County
  inspectors (縣師) receive assignments and inspect ditches, fortifications, and roads.
  All officials pool resources; craftsmen report for duty. The military marshal (司馬)
  surveys the walls and organizes soldiers into units. At the gates, two guards manage
  the right door, two the left, four manage the closing mechanism, and a full contingent
  of armored soldiers maintains watch.


  **Unit Organization**: On the wall, each soldier with armor and halberd is supported
  by three assistants. Military leadership is distributed at every level: a leader
  for every five paces, a ten-soldier commander (什長) for every ten paces, and a hundred-soldier
  commander (百長) for every hundred paces. The flanks are monitored by senior officers,
  with a supreme commander (大將) overseeing all.


  **Resource Management**: Beyond thirty li, all firewood and water must be brought
  inside. Dogs, pigs, chickens, and other animals feed on husks and scraps; their
  remains are collected for sauce (醢) making—a grim provision against food shortage
  and a form of recycling for the war effort. The text even addresses health concerns:
  those suffering abdominal illness (腹病) are treated from these provisions.


  **Night Watch**: During quiet nights, drums are beaten and watchmen whistle to maintain
  alertness and deter infiltrators. The rhythm "slanders" (謲) the enemy''s qi, maintaining
  morale among the populace while psychological pressure on the besiegers.


  **Walls and Defense**: Arrows reaching the wall require covering (涂菌). Fire watch
  is maintained; supplies are stocked. Every measure ensures the city''s defensive
  integrity against both external assault and internal panic.

  **指揮系統**：県師が任務を受けて溝・堤防・道路を巡回檢査し、補裝・通路の整備、城壁の修理を担当する。全役人が資源を統合し、職人は出仕し、軍事元帅（司马）が城壁を検分して部隊を編成する。城門では右側二名、左側二名が管理し、四名が閉鎖装置を操作、百名の鎧兵が警備につく。

守城之法者，墨子所述籠城之組織管理要務也。其法甚備，條列於左：

**官制與調度**：縣師受事，出葆循溝防，築薦通塗，脩城。百官共財，百工即事，司馬視城脩卒伍。城門之守，二人掌右閹，二人掌左閹，四人掌閉，百甲坐之。

**部隊編制**：城上步一甲、一戟，其讚三人。五步有五長，十步有什長，百步有百長，㫄有大率，中有大將，皆有司吏卒長。城上當階，有司守之，移中中處，澤急而奏之。士皆有職。

**物資儲備**：三十里之內，薪蒸水皆入內。狗彘豚鷄食其𡧢，斂其骸以為醢。此蓋備乏食之時，取於舊物也。腹病者以起，蓋用醢以治病歟。

**城防設施**：城之內，薪蒸廬室，矢之所逮，皆為之涂菌以防火攻。城之外，矢之所逮，壞其墻，無以為客菌。

**夜守之法**：令命昏緯狗纂馬掔緯，靜夜聞鼓聲而謲，所以閹客之氣也，所以固民之意也，故時謲則民不疾矣。

凡此諸法，皆以確保城中秩序、士氣、物資、防務之万全，使敵不能攻而自困，此墨家守城之要道也。
