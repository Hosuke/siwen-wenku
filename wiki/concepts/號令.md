content_legacy: '## English


  Mozi''s "Commands and Orders" (號令) constitutes a comprehensive military legal code
  for the defense of besieged cities. Unlike purely technical siege manuals, this
  text addresses the human dimension of warfare—the management of defenders, prevention
  of internal betrayal, and the maintenance of discipline through a transparent system
  of rewards and punishments.


  The text opens with a fundamental principle: the city defender must act swiftly
  to harm the enemy. Prolonged defense awaiting relief is explicitly condemned as
  a failure to understand the art of defense. When the city comes under attack, the
  commander must immediately mobilize all five regional officials and hundred-household
  leaders, summoning those from wealthy families to sheltered official quarters for
  their protection.


  Central to the command system is the division of labor. Each of the eight districts
  within the city is assigned its own officer with four subordinates, tasked with
  patrolling the streets and monitoring the population. Family heads who fail in their
  defensive duties or accounting responsibilities are replaced. Any information about
  suspicious activity must be reported immediately—failure to do so carries the death
  penalty, while successful apprehension of traitors earns a reward of two yi of gold.


  The beacon and fire signal system provides communication across distances. Intelligence
  agents (諜者) are dispatched in groups of no more than three hundred, returning before
  nightfall. They establish signal posts every ten li, with three posts between themselves
  and the city walls. During the day, beacons indicate enemy approach; at night, fires
  serve the same purpose. The response protocol varies by distance: one beacon for
  distant enemies, two when they enter the border, three when they pass the outer
  walls, four when they enter the inner walls, and five when they approach the city
  itself.


  Punishments under the command system are severe and collective. A single soldier''s
  misconduct may result in the execution of their entire five-person squad. Those
  who desert to the enemy doom their families to the terrible punishment of being
  torn apart by chariots. However, the system also provides for amnesty to those who
  inform on traitors before they act. Officers who fail to halt deserters or who themselves
  assist in escape face execution.

墨子所述之號令，乃城防軍事紀律之總綱。其要在乎賞罰分明、人心歸附也。

號令之道，首重賞罰之公。「賞譽毀罰」必出於一心，不可有私人恩怨之偏。守城之法，以亟傷敵為上，延日持久以待救至者，非善守者也。城中姦民有謀為外心者，罪車裂；諸卒民居城上者，各葆其左右，伍中有罪不相智則連坐。

號令之要，又有姦細之防。凡有謀殺傷其將長者，與謀反同罪。有能捕告者，賜黃金二十斤。若非其分職而擅取，若非其所當治而擅治為之，皆斷。城下里中家人皆相葆，若城上之數。有能捕告之者，封之以千家之邑。

葆宮之制，亦號令之要務也。葆宮之墻必三重，守者皆累瓦釜墻上。門有吏，主者門里，筦閉必須太守之節。守樓臨質宮而善周，必密塗樓，令下無見上，上見下，下無知上有人無人而後可。

烽燧之事，亦號令所寄。士諜無過十里，居高便所樹表，表三人守之，比至城者三表，與城上烽燧相望，晝則舉烽，夜則舉火。聞寇所從來，審知寇形必攻，乃論小城不自守通者，盡葆其老弱、粟米、畜產。
