## English

**Mutual Disregard (不相愛)** is the pathological condition identified by Mozi as the root cause of all social chaos. Where [[兼愛]] prescribes universal mutual care, 不相愛 describes the failure thereof—the disposition of individuals who love only themselves and their own families, seeking benefit at others' expense.

The text enumerates a hierarchy of disorder stemming from 不相愛:

1. **Within the family**: Children fail to love their fathers, younger brothers fail to love elder brothers, ministers fail to love their rulers—each selfishly pursuing personal gain at their elders' expense. Even when parents fail to love children or elder brothers fail to love younger brothers, the same principle applies: each loves only the self.

2. **Among thieves and bandits**: Thieves love their own homes but not others' homes, so they steal from others' households for their own benefit. Robbers love their own bodies but not others' persons, so they harm others for their own gain.

3. **Among the nobility**: Lords of families love only their own families, causing them to disrupt other families for self-benefit. Rulers love only their own states, causing them to attack neighboring states.

The text concludes that "天下之亂物，具此而已矣"—all the chaotic phenomena in the world are reducible to this fundamental failure of mutual love. The physician analogy reinforces the point: just as a doctor who does not know the origin of disease cannot treat it, governance that ignores the origin of disorder cannot remedy it.

## 中文

**不相愛**者，墨子所指為天下亂之本源也。夫人之有亂，非天之降殃，乃人之自致。其致之之道，在於不相愛。

墨子歷數其狀：於家則子不愛父，弟不愛兄，臣不愛君，各虧其所愛者以自利；於盜賊則竊異室以利其室，賊人以利其身；於大夫則亂異家以利其家；於諸侯則攻異國以利其國。凡此種種，皆起於人唯知自愛，而不知愛人。

墨子明言：「天下之亂物，具此而已矣。」謂天下之亂象，盡於此矣，無他故也。其救治之道，亦惟在使人兼相愛而已。

## 日本語

**不相愛**とは，天下の混乱の根本原因として墨子が名指した病理的状態である。[[兼愛]]が普遍的な相互の愛を説くのに対し，不相愛はその失敗であり，自己と自分の家族だけを愛し，他者を犠牲にして利益を追求する傾向を指す。

本文は以下の階層的な乱れを列举する：
- 家族内：子は父を愛さず，弟は兄を愛さず，臣は君を愛さず
- 盗賊間：彼は自分の家を爱他人の家を爱他ない
- 貴族間：大夫は他家を守り，君主は自国だけを愛する

「天下の混乱は全て此れによる」——不相愛の排除だけが真の秩序をもたらし得る。
