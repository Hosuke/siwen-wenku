  The term *ru* (儒, Confucian) refers to the tradition of scholars who uphold ritual
  propriety (禮) and moral cultivation. *Ruxue* (儒學, Confucianism) centers on self-cultivation
  (修身) as its foundation, emphasizing both external rites and internal cultivation
  of character. In the context of the *Juli Chengtiao* narrative, the Taoist master
  criticizes the Confucian followers as those who "only know external rituals but
  do not understand internal工夫" — a classic Taoist critique of Confucianism. While
  Confucianism holds that self-cultigation precedes service to the world, Taoism argues
  that such cultivation may not achieve the realm of spiritual mastery (神).

儒者，言修身之學、崇禮儀之道的學者之稱。儒學者，以修身為本，注重內外兼修之工夫。[[痀僂承蜩]]所記[[道-道家-之]]家批判[[儒-儒學-之]]家，謂世儒但知外在之禮儀，不知內在之工夫。此[[道-道家-之]]對[[儒-儒學-之]]之批判，正指出儒家之修己，未必臻於「神」之境界。修己而後言上，乃[[儒-儒學-之]]固有之義；而[[道-道家-之]]之意，則在指出其未足也。
