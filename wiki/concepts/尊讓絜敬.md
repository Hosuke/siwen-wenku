## English

The concept of "尊讓絜敬" (zūnràng jìngjìng) — variously translated as "respect, yielding, purity, and reverence" or "deference, restraint, purity, and solemnity" — represents the four cardinal virtues that should govern interactions among the junzi (gentlemen/wise individuals) in classical Confucian thought. This tetradic formula appears in the "Xiang Yin Jiu Yi" chapter of the Book of Rites and encapsulates the moral foundations of ritual propriety.

The four elements function as an integrated system. "尊" (respect/deference) and "讓" (yielding/ceding) form a pair relating to proper social positioning, wherein one acknowledges the worth of others and willingly yields precedence to those of higher virtue or status. "絜" (purity/cleanliness) and "敬" (reverence/earnestness) form a complementary pair relating to inner spiritual disposition, wherein one maintains moral purity and approaches both ritual and persons with sincere solemnity.

The text explicates the social consequences of embodying these virtues: when gentlemen practice "尊讓," they do not dispute; when they practice "絜敬," they do not show disrespect. The absence of dispute and disrespect removes the foundations of "鬬辨" (contention and argumentation), which in turn prevents "暴亂之禍" (the disasters of violence and disorder). Thus, the ritual practice of these virtues serves not merely aesthetic or ceremonial purposes but functions as a bulwark against social chaos. The sage-kings who established these rituals understood that human nature, left unregulated, tends toward conflict, and that proper ritual education channels these tendencies toward harmonious cooperation.

The cultivation of "尊讓絜敬" connects to broader Confucian concerns with self-cultivation and social harmony. "尊讓" relates to the practice of "遜" (humility) and "辭讓" (deferential speech), while "絜敬" relates to the practice of "慎獨" (vigilance in solitude) and proper inner moral cleanliness. Together, they constitute the minimum requirements for moral social intercourse and form the psychological basis upon which more elaborate ritual forms are built.

## 中文

「尊讓絜敬」者，君子相接之道的四要素也，出於《禮記·鄉飲酒義》。

**尊讓**者，待人之道也。尊，敬之也；讓，遜之也。主人迎賓，三揖而後至階，三讓而後升，致敬於初見之時，致讓於升降之際。賓主相見，以尊讓為先，則無爭奪之心。

**絜敬**者，自修之道也。絜，通「潔」，清淨之義；敬，肅穆之義。盥洗揚觶，所以致絜也；拜至、拜洗、拜受、拜送、拜既，所以致敬也。內絜而外敬，則無怠慢之失。

《鄉飲酒義》云：「尊讓絜敬也者，君子之所以相接也。君子尊讓則不爭，絜敬則不慢，不慢不爭，則遠於鬬辨矣，不鬬辨則無暴亂之禍矣。」

是故聖人製禮，以尊讓絜敬為根本。非為美觀也，乃所以防爭奪、遠暴亂者也。先王之道，蓋本於此。

## 日本語

「尊讓絜敬」は、君子が之交わる際の四つの基本徳目であり、『禮記·鄉飲酒義』に見える。

「尊」は他者を敬い重んじることであり、「讓」は先を進んで人に譲るということである。主人 Dale賓を迎える際、三度の揖で階に至り、三度の譲りで昇る。これは初対面の敬意と、挙動における控えめの精神を表象する。「絜」は清浄を意味し、「敬」は厳粛・謹厳を意味する。顔を洗い觶を挙げるのは清浄を示すためであり，拜至・拜洗・拜受・拜送・拜既は敬意を表出する。

孔子の學において、この四要素は統合的に機能する。「尊讓」は社會的な位置取りに関するものであり、「絜敬」は内面的な精神状態に関するものである。彼此两組が結びつきて、争いのない社会の基盤を形成する。君子 Dale尊讓すれば争わず、絜敬すれば慢らない。不争不慢なれば、闘争と弁論の芽が摘まれ、暴力と紊乱の災いから遠ざかる。聖人 Daleこの儀礼を制定したのは、まさに人心的 Dale自然发生的争いを徳性教育によって調和的合作へと疏导しようとしたからである。
