## English

**Human Emotions** (人情) constitute one of the central topics in the *Yunyun* chapter, where Confucius provides a systematic analysis of human nature and its relationship to social order through ritual.

Confucius identifies seven emotions that humans possess without needing to learn: joy (喜), anger (怒), sorrow (哀), fear (懼), love (愛), hatred (惡), and desire (欲). These innate emotions form the basis of human personality and social interaction. Alongside the emotions, he delineates ten principles of human duty (人義): parental kindness and filial piety, fraternal goodness and submission, conjugal righteousness and compliance, elder beneficence and younger obedience, and ruler benevolence and minister loyalty.

Confucius explains that ritual (禮) exists precisely to regulate these emotions and desires. The "Great Way" of the [[大同|Great Unity]] emerges when people channel their desires properly and maintain harmonious relationships through ritual propriety. When ritual breaks down, desires lead to conflict and ultimately to the "calamities" (患) of mutual destruction and killing.

The famous formulation appears: "Food and sex are the great desires of humans; death and poverty are the great dislikes of humans. Thus desire and aversion constitute the great beginnings of the heart-mind. The heart-mind harbors intentions that cannot be fathomed; both the beautiful and the ugly reside within it. To exhaust these desires through ritual propriety—that is the Way."

This analysis grounds Confucian political philosophy in human psychology, arguing that good governance must acknowledge and channel human emotions rather than suppress them.

## 中文

**人情**為《禮記·禮運》篇之重要議題。孔子系統闡述人情與禮之關係，為儒家政治哲學之基礎。

孔子明言：「何謂人情？喜、怒、哀、懼、愛、惡、欲，七者，弗學而能。」此七情為人之本性，不待學而知。又有十義：「父慈、子孝、兄良、弟弟、夫義、婦聽、長惠、幼順、君仁、臣忠，十者，謂之人義。」

孔子以為，聖人之所以治人七情，修十義，講信脩睦，尚辭讓，去爭奪，舍禮何以治之？「飲食男女，人之大欲存焉。死亡貧苦，人之大惡存焉。故欲惡者，心之大端也。」

又言：「人藏其心，不可測度也，美惡皆在其心，不見其色也，欲一以窮之，舍禮何以哉？」

是以聖人制禮，正所以節人情之中節者也。情得其節，則家和國治；情失其節，則爭奪相殺。禮之於人，猶酒麴之於酒，君子以厚，小人以薄。可見人情為禮之本，亦治道之原。

## 日本語

**人情**は『礼記』「運」篇の中心的テーマである。孔子は人間の感情と社会秩序の関係について体系的な分析を行う。

孔子は「人情とは何か？喜び、怒り、悲しみ、恐れ、愛しみ、憎しみ、欲望—この七つは学ばずしてできる」と述べる。さらに十義として「父慈、子孝、兄良、弟恭、夫義、婦聴、長恵、幼順、君仁、臣忠」を挙げる。

「食事と性は人間の大きな欲望であり、死と貧乏は人間の大きな嫌悪である。欲望と嫌悪こそが心の大きな発端である」と孔子は言う。礼は人情を調整するためにあるのであり、聖人は礼を通じて七情を治め、十義涵養し、信義を涵養し、争いを払う。
