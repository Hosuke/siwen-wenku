## English

The concept of "beauty" (美) in classical Chinese ritual literature encompasses four distinct domains: speech, court proceedings, sacrifice, and horses. This taxonomy appears in the *Book of Rites, Minor Rituals* (少儀), presenting an aesthetic ideal that pervades the cultivated person's conduct across all social contexts.

**Speech beauty** is described as *mùmù huánghuáng* (穆穆皇皇) — graceful, dignified, and elegant verbal expression. **Court beauty** is *jǐjǐ xiángxiáng* (濟濟翔翔) — the orderly arrangement of officials in their proper ranks. **Sacrifice beauty** is

---

「穆穆皇皇」，語出《假樂》，形容君子威儀莊嚴、容止美好之辭。

穆穆者，肅敬之容；皇皇者，光美之貌。合而言之，言君子之德，於穆肅中見美好，於美盛中存敬謹。以之宜君，以之宜王，則天下取法焉。
