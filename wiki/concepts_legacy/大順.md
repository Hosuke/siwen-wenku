## English

**Great Harmony** (大順) represents the culmination of Confucian political philosophy as described in the *Yunyun* chapter. It is the state of perfect social order that emerges when ritual (禮) and righteousness (義) are properly cultivated throughout society.

The text presents a famous agricultural metaphor for governance: "The human emotions are the fields of the sage-kings. Cultivate ritual to plow them, set forth righteousness to sow them, teach to weed them, gather through benevolence, and安宁 them through music."

The process unfolds in stages: governance without ritual is like plowing without a plowshare. Rituals not based on righteousness are like plowing without planting. Righteousness without teaching is like planting without weeding. Teaching that does not harmonize with benevolence is like weeding without harvesting. Harmonizing with benevolence but not bringing peace through music is like harvesting without eating. Achieving peace but not reaching obedience is like eating without becoming nourished.

When the four limbs are correct and the skin is full, that is physical nourishment. When father and son are sincere, brothers are harmonious, and husband and wife are reconciled, that is the nourishment of the family. When ministers follow the law and officials are incorrupt, when offices assist each other and ruler and ministers correct each other, that is the nourishment of the state. When the Son of Heaven uses virtue as his carriage and music as his driver, when lords meet in friendship, when great officers maintain order through law, when scholars are tested through trustworthiness, and when commoners live in mutual security, that is the nourishment of the world.

This is the Great Harmony—the constant way of serving ghosts and spirits, nurturing life and sending off the dead. It is the state where great accumulations do not stagnate, parallel activities do not conflict, minor matters do not slip through, depth connects without crossing, and movement proceeds without harm. Only by understanding this harmony can one preserve a precarious position.

## 中文

**大順**為《禮記·禮運》篇所論治道之極致，亦儒家政治哲學之歸宿。

《禮運》云：「故人情者，聖王之田也。脩禮以耕之，陳義以種之，講學以耨之，本仁以聚之，播樂以安之。」

此以治國比之農事：治國不以禮，猶無耜而耕也；為禮不本於義，猶耕而弗種也；為義而不講之以學，猶種而弗耨也；講之於學而不合之以仁，猶耨而弗獲也；合之以仁而不安之以樂，猶獲而弗食也；安之以樂而不達於順，猶食而弗肥也。

四體既正，膚革充盈，人之肥也；父子篤，兄弟睦，夫婦和，家之肥也；大臣法，小臣廉，官職相序，君臣相正，國之肥也；天子以德為車，以樂為御，諸侯以禮相與，大夫以法相序，士以信相考，百姓以睦相守，天下之肥也。是謂大順。

大順者，養生送死、事鬼神之常也。事大積焉而不苑，並行而不繆，細行而不失。深而通，茂而有間。連而不相及也，動而不相害也，此順之至也。故明於順，然後能守危也。

## 日本語

**大順**は『礼記』「運」篇における儒家の政治哲学の集大成である。礼と義が適切に涵養されるときに出てくる、完全な社会秩序の状態を表す。

「人情は聖王の田であり、礼で耕し、義で種を蒔き、学びで除草し、仁で集め、楽で安居させる」とテキストは述べる。

治世は農業の過程と比較される：礼なしでの統治は犂なしでの耕作に等しく、義に基づかない礼は蒔きことのない耕作に等しい。この段階的過程を経て、天子は徳を車とし楽を御者とし、天下は調和と安定を達成する。

これが大順であり、鬼神を仕え、生命を涵養し、死を送り出す常道である。大きな蓄積は滞らず、並行するものは衝突せず、小さなものは見失われず、深いものは连通し、運動は害なく行われる。
