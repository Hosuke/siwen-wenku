## English

**Heavenly Officers of Transgression** (司過之神) refers to celestial deities described in early Taoist texts who perpetually monitor human conduct and impose cosmic punishment for moral failings. According to *Baopuzi* (抱朴子), these divine officials observe every transgression, both great and small, and deduct from an individual's allotment of years (算/紀) accordingly.

The text describes a hierarchical system of punishment: minor infractions result in the loss of *suan* (算, three-day units), while serious transgressions cost *ji* (紀, 300-day units). When one's allocations are exhausted, death follows. The *Baopuzi* cites multiple classical sources including the *Yi Neizhi* (易內戒), *Chisongzi Jing* (赤松子經), and the *Heitu Jiming Fu* (河圖記命符) to substantiate this doctrine.

This concept integrates seamlessly with broader Chinese cosmological thought regarding heavenly supervision of human affairs. The mechanism of moral calculus—where virtue accumulates blessings while vice depletes one's divinely-appointed lifespan—appears throughout early Chinese religious literature. [[天賞天罰]] and [[鬼神之賞賢而罰暴]] address related themes of supernatural reward and punishment.

The text emphasizes that these celestial overseers are not limited to observing major crimes; even seemingly trivial matters like "浮沉過於金羽，皂白分於粉墨" (trivial distinctions in daily judgment) fall within their purview. This comprehensive surveillance creates a cosmic accountability system that complements human legal and social frameworks.

## 中文

**司過之神**，道教概念，指監視人間過失並據以奪算減紀之天神。抱朴子引《易內戒》《赤松子經》《河圖記命符》諸典，謂天地設有此神，随人所犯輕重，以奪其算。算減則人貧耗疾病，屢逢憂患；算盡則人死。大者奪紀，一紀三百日；小者奪算，一算三日。

此說與中國傳統天道賞罰觀念相合。天居高而聽卑，物無不鑒，行善不怠，必得吉報。《抱朴子》雖稱「吾亦未能審此事之有無」，然仍據以為誡，示人積善立功之要。

文中列舉諸應奪算之事達數百種，大而至於殺人取位、侵克賢者、誅戮降伏，小而至於決放水火、以術害人、換貸不償，皆在清算之列。見[[天賞天罰]]、[[鬼神之賞賢而罰暴]]。

## 日本語

**司過之神**（しか过神）とは、人間的行いを監視し、その過失に応じて寿命を削る天上の神々を指す。『抱朴子』において、天地には人の過失を糾弾する神がおり、『易內戒』『赤松子経』『河圖記命符』などの古典を引いて、その存在を説いている。

小さな罪は「算」（三日分の命）を奪われ、大きな罪は「紀」（三百日分の命）を奪われる。算と紀が尽きれば、死が訪れる。この概念は、中国伝統の天人合一思想と天命報応説紧密结合しており、善行には天罰、善行には福報という因果応報の体系を形成している。
