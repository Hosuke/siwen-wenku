## English

The **Defensive Wooden Barrier** (薄) is an outer defensive fence system described in the Mozi's defensive warfare chapters. According to "Preparations Against Ant-Attacks," barriers are placed outside the city, ten chi from the wall, with a thickness of ten chi. The construction method requires timber of various sizes, cut into ten-chi sections, arranged in alternating layers with deep foundations and compacted earth, ensuring the barriers cannot be pulled loose.

Every twenty paces, a killing station (殺) is established with walls ten chi thick and two gates five paces wide each. These gates are fitted with removable wooden barricades (not cemented), allowing for rapid counterattacks while maintaining the ability to restore defenses quickly.

The system creates a layered defense: enemies approaching the city must first breach the outer killing stations, then navigate through the barrier field with its alternating timber construction, before reaching the main wall. The city walls above have observation points positioned above the barrier gates, with signal markers for coordination.

## 中文

**薄**者，墨子所述城外木柵防守之法也。《備蛾傅》篇云：「凡殺蛾傅而攻者之法，置薄城外，去城十尺，薄厚十尺。伐薄之法，大小盡木斷之，以十尺爲斷，離而深貍堅築之，毋使可拔。」

其制：薄置城外，去城十尺，厚亦十尺。伐木之法，大小之材皆斬斷之，以十尺為度，交錯深埋，堅實築之，使其不可拔。

二十步一殺，有𡑾，厚十尺，殺有兩門，門廣五步。薄門以板為梯，埋置之，勿築，令易拔。城上之人俯瞰薄門，置楬以望敵。

此城外之防，使敵先薄而遇殺，殺而遇城，三重之備也。

## 日本語

**薄（はく）**は、『墨子』「備蛾傅」篇に記述された城外の木製障壁システムである。城外に城壁から十尺の距離に、厚さ十尺の木柵を配置する。伐木は大小様々な材を使用し、十尺の長さに切断して交互に深く埋め込み、堅固に構築して引き抜き不能とする。二十歩ごとに殺卒（殺）を配置し、厚さ十尺の壁と幅五歩の門を二つ設ける。門には木製の堰板を取り付けて迅速な撤去と再設置を可能にする。城壁上部からは障壁の門を見下ろし、楬で敵情を伝令する多層防御システムを構成する。
