## English

**Sage Governance** (聖人為政) represents the central thesis of *On Frugality*: the greatest act of statesmanship lies not in external conquest but in internal efficiency. The text asserts that when a sage governs a single state, that state's wealth can double; when such a sage governs the world, the world's benefit can likewise double.

This doubling does not come from seizing new territories but from *eliminating useless things* within the existing state. By removing wasteful expenditures on ornamental goods, extravagant ceremonies, or impractical luxuries, and redirecting those resources toward practical necessities, the sage multiplies effective wealth without imposing additional burdens on the people.

The governance philosophy here is deeply pragmatic: every policy must demonstrate "added utility." Whether evaluating clothing, housing, military equipment, or transportation, the criterion is always whether something *adds practical use*. If it does, keep it; if not, discard it.

## 中文

**聖人為政**者，《節用上》之要旨也。墨子以為，聖人治一國，一國可倍；聖人治天下，天下可倍。其倍之之道，非向外取地，乃在「因其國家，去其無用」。

所謂無用者，非僅指財物之奢靡也。凡事之不加實用者，皆在去除之列。衣裳之於溫凊，宮室之於避風雨，甲盾五兵之於禦寇亂，舟車之於通四方，皆以實用為準繩。聖人發令興事，用財行事，無不加用而為。財用不費，民德不勞，興利多矣。

此為政之道，以[[節用]]為基，以實用為衡，去無用之務而行聖王之道者也。

## 日本語

**聖人による統治**とは、『節用上』の中心命題であり、統治の至高の行為は外的征服ではなく内的な効率にあるとする。聖人が一国を統治すればその国の富は倍になり，天下を統治すれば世界の利益もまた倍になることができる。この倍増は新しい領土を獲得することではなく、既存の国家内の「無用なものを取り除く」ことによる。
