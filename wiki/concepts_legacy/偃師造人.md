## English

"Yan Shi Creates an Artificial Man" is a remarkable story from the Tang Wen chapter of the Liezi, presenting one of the earliest accounts of automaton in Chinese literature.

King Mu of Zhou, during his western tour beyond the Kunlun Mountains, encountered a craftsman named Yan Shi who offered to present his creation. Yan Shi announced: "I have made something and wish to present it to Your Majesty first."

The next day, Yan Shi brought his creation to court. King Mu examined it and found it was an "倡者" (a performer puppet). It walked, bowed, and danced with perfect naturalness. Its chin moved in song, harmonizing with the music; its hands moved in dance, responding to the rhythm. "A thousand variations, all as the mind intended." The King believed it was truly human and watched alongside his consort Sheng Ji.

When the performance concluded, the puppet winked at the royal concubines. The furious King immediately ordered Yan Shi executed. Trembling, Yan Shi quickly disassembled the puppet to show the King: inside were only leather, wood, glue, lacquer, white and red pigment—but every part was complete. He could remove the heart, and the mouth could not speak; remove the liver, and the eyes could not see; remove the kidneys, and the feet could not walk.

The King examined it thoroughly and found that the internal organs—the liver, gallbladder, heart, lungs, spleen, kidneys, intestines, stomach—and external parts—skin, flesh, bones, joints, teeth, hair—all functioned through the materials, nothing was missing. When reassembled, it appeared exactly as before.

The King sighed: "Can human cleverness truly rival the works of nature?" He ordered the puppet loaded onto a secondary carriage and taken home.

The text then compares this to the legendary mechanical devices of [[梓匠規矩|Ban Shu]] (Lu Ban) and Mo Di, noting that their disciples, upon hearing of Yan Shi's creation, "dared not speak of their own crafts thereafter, though they continued to hold the ruler and compass in their hands."

## 中文

《湯問篇》述偃師之事：周穆王西巡狩，越崑崙，不至弇山。反還，未及中國，道有獻工人名偃師。

偃師謁見，曰：「臣之所造能倡者。」穆王驚視之，趣步俯仰，信人也。巧夫顉其頤，則歌合律；捧其手，則舞應節。千變萬化，惟意所適。王以為實人也。

技將終，倡者瞬其目而招王之左右侍妾。王大怒，立欲誅偃師。偃師大懾，立剖散倡者以示王，皆傅會革、木、膠、漆、白、黑、丹、青之所為。

王諦料之，內則肝、膽、心、肺、脾、腎、腸、胃，外則筋骨、支節、皮毛、齒髮，皆假物也，而無不畢具者，合會復如初見。

王試廢其心，則口不能言；廢其肝，則目不能視；廢其腎，則足不能步。

穆王始悅而歎曰：「人之巧乃可與造化者同功乎？」

此故事之要義，在於問「人巧」與「天巧」之分際。偃師之技，巧奪天工，然終究是「假物」所為，非真有生命。[[梓匠規矩]]所載班輸、墨翟之技，亦復如是。然偃師之巧，尤在其「合會復如初見」，能拆能合，此則班輸、墨翟之所不及也。
