## English

**"愛人為大"** (Loving People Is Paramount) is a key statement from Confucius's conversation with Duke Ai of Lu in the *Book of Rites*. When Duke Ai asked about the principles of governance, Confucius outlined a hierarchical structure of political values:

> "古之為政，愛人為大。所以治愛人，禮為大。所以治禮，敬為大。"

In rendering: "In ancient governance, loving people was the greatest. To govern loving people, ritual propriety was the greatest. To govern ritual propriety, reverence was the greatest."

This passage presents a three-tiered system: **love** (愛) as the ultimate political value, **ritual** (禮) as the mechanism for expressing love in governance, and **reverence** (敬) as the animating spirit of ritual. Love without ritual would be indiscriminate; ritual without reverence would be hollow formalism. Reverence, in turn, finds its highest expression in the solemnity of the [[婚禮|marriage ceremony]] — "大昏為大" (The great wedding is the culmination of reverence).

Confucius further elaborated the relationship between love and reverence: "弗愛不親；弗敬不正。愛與敬，其政之本與？" — "Without love, there is no intimacy; without reverence, there is no rectification. Love and reverence — are they not the root of governance?"

This teaching reflects the Confucian belief that [[仁-儒學-之|benevolence]] is not merely a private virtue but the foundation of political order. The ruler who genuinely loves his people will naturally seek to govern them through proper [[禮-儒學-之|ritual]] forms, and the citizens will respond with loyalty and cooperation. Conversely, governance divorced from love becomes oppressive or manipulative.

The phrase "愛人為大" also connects to the concept of [[修身|self-cultivation]]: the ruler must first cultivate love within himself before he can extend it to his people.

## 中文

「**愛人為大**」出自《禮記·哀公問》，為孔子答魯哀公問治道所言。孔子明言治道之層次：「古之為政，愛人為大。所以治愛人，禮為大。所以治禮，敬為大。」

愛人者，政治之本也。孔子以愛人為治道之最高價值，猶《論語》所言「仁者愛人」。然愛人不可無法，須以禮節之；禮亦不可無本，須以敬終之。敬者，禮之本也。

愛與敬之關係，孔子言之甚明：「弗愛不親；弗敬不正。愛與敬，其政之本與？」無愛則無以成親親之仁；無敬則無以別尊卑之正。二者相須，方為治道之全。

此說又與[[敬慎|敬]]之德相發明。敬之至者，莫大於昏禮。孔子云：「敬之至矣，大昏為大。」昏禮者，合二姓之好，以繼先聖之後，以為天地宗廟社稷之主。敬慎以對，是重人倫之本也。

愛人為大，非徒感情之愛，乃以[[仁-儒學-之]]為心，以[[禮-儒學-之]]為節，以敬為極之治道也。

（所出：《禮記·哀公問》）

## 日本語
